- Location: Oak Watch master bedroom

### Intimate Scene Writing Guidelines

**Approach:**
- Slow building, consent explicit
- "Checking in" as characters proceed
- Emotional significance over physical mechanics
- Sensory details: scents, textures, light, sound
- Vulnerability as strength
- Aftermath/aftercare included

**Common Elements:**
- Robes in silk (blush, emerald)
- Firelight/candlelight
- Wine present but not driving
- "Take your time" / "We have time"
- Tears as emotional release (not sadness)
- Morning after scenes equally important

**Themes:**
- "Addition, not replacement"
- "Love as expansion, not scarcity"
- "Being seen completely"
- Trust as prerequisite for intimacy
- Worth not tied to performance
