## WIVES CLUB MEMBERS

### Charlotte "Lottie" (Last Name TBD)
**Introduction:** Book 3, Chapter 7 of Bella Series
**Role:** Newer Club member, logistics specialist
**Physical:** Round face, genuine smile, self-described "DUFF" (Designated Ugly Fat Friend)
**Personality:** Self-aware, cheerful, humble about her appearance but confident in her skills
**Superpower:** Seating charts—can optimize any gathering for productive conversation and minimal drama
**Connection to Maison Aurelia:** Works with Elena's events company

**Storyline Potential:** "The DUFF Reclaimed" - A series exploring Lottie's unique perspective on Club events, her logistics genius, and her journey from feeling invisible to owning her power.

### Selene (Last Name TBD)
**Introduction:** Book 3, Chapter 7 of Bella Series
**Role:** Club member, cautionary tale
**Physical:** Dark hair, dramatic features, stunning—"the kind of beautiful that launches ships"
**Personality:** Brilliant but unstable, chaotic, something "broken" in her eyes
