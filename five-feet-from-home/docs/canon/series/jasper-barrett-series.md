## Premise

Jasper Barrett is a blue-collar kid from Gastonia, NC who became a world-class corporate crisis manager. The series follows his journey balancing family, work, and the high-stakes world of BSS (Barrett Strategic Solutions).

## Themes

- Work-Life Balance
- Family
- Crisis Management
- Leadership
- Legacy
