# Gideon Rourke

**Nickname:** Revenant

**Archetype:** Dark Operator / Former DEVGRU

## Affiliations

- **BSS Role:** Contractor / Ally

## Background

Modeled after James Reece (Terminal List/Dark Wolf). Former Navy SEAL DEVGRU Gold Squadron. Medically retired after shrapnel injuries in Yemen. Survivor of compromised op where half his team was lost - still believes intel was deliberately bad. Widower whose wife and daughter died in "car accident" he suspects was targeted.

## Education

University of Virginia BA Political Science; Naval War College irregular warfare

## Appearance

Battle-scarred operator look.

## Personality

Stoic, brooding, tactical thinker. Operates with quiet intensity. Trust earned with blood not words. Hyper-alert, sleeps light.

## Relationships

Widower - wife and daughter killed in suspicious car accident while deployed. Keeps their photo in rifle case.
