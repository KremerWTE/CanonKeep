# Rafe Moreno

**Archetype:** Unconventional Asset / Forensic Specialist

## Affiliations

- **Role:** BSS Specialist
- **BSS Role:** Forensic accounting, unconventional solutions

## Background

Ex-con and former art thief. Recruited by Jasper for his charm, unconventional skills, and street-level instincts. Now uses his talents for legitimate purposes. Has paid his debts to society.

## Appearance

Polished casual. The look of someone who could charm his way into or out of anywhere. Dark hair, well-styled. Chameleon who adapts to any situation.
