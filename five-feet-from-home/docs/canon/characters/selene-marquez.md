# Selene Marquez

**Archetype:** Panel Anchor / Chaos Coordinator

**Age:** 35

## Affiliations

- **Role:** Functions as the "chaos coordinator." She's playful but dangerous, often catching intel during media events. Tied romantically into the BSS orbit.
- **Wives Club:** Chaos Coordinator / Media

## Background

Began as a sideline reporter in college football, transitioned into co-hosting major sports talk shows. Became the sarcastic, quick-fire foil to Harper on Hard Hits.

## Appearance

5'7", brunette with caramel highlights, big smile, athletic-chic fashion.

## Personality

Funny, unfiltered, loves stirring the pot with sharp banter. Social butterfly, thrives on energy and chaos.

## Relationships

Adds social capital in sports/media circles. Harper & Selene have playful banter dynamic.
