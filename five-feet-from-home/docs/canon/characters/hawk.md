# Hawk

**Nickname:** Hawk

**Archetype:** Tier 1 Operator / Security Chief

**Age:** Early-Mid 30s

## Affiliations

- **BSS Role:** Head of Operators
- **Hub Location:** Charlotte, NC (from Vegas)

## Background

Former Delta/Tier 1 operator. "Broken" by service; nearly died before recovery. Transitioned to private security in Vegas (Head of security at The Palmetto casino). Met Addie and becomes her steady support during her collapse. Helps her rebuild; becomes boyfriend/partner. Eventually moves to Charlotte. Marries Addie; builds family. Becomes father figure to Grace.

Phoenix Foundation director/board member. Special projects advisor to Jasper.
