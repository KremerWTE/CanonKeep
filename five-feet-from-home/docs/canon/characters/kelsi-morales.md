## Background

Born in Miami to Cuban-American parents. Grew up around dance, nightlife, and sports. Played soccer competitively until 18, then pivoted to modeling and eventually cam work. Unlike others, embraces the party lifestyle - clubs, music, high energy.

## Appearance

Exotic Latina beauty - sun-kissed skin, long brunette hair with waves, curvy athletic build. Known for hourglass figure and radiant confidence.

## Wardrobe & Style

Bold, colorful, party-ready. Miami nightlife aesthetic.

## Personality

On camera: Bold, fiery, unapologetically sexual. Off camera: Loyal and protective, like big sister to younger Sirens. Huge laugh, curses freely, never hides who she is. Street-smart, knows how to move in Miami/Vegas nightlife without getting burned.
