# Bella's Mother

**Archetype:** Café Owner / Family Matriarch

## Affiliations

## Background

Runs the family café - warm, charismatic, social hub of the community. Always pulling people in. Bella learned warmth, patience, and social ease from her. The café taught Bella hospitality and how to read people through customer service. After COVID, the café struggled financially.
