# Mason Reilly

**Archetype:** Best Friend / Truth-Teller

## Affiliations

- **Role:** Political fixer, Jasper's Duke buddy

## Background

Met Jasper at Duke. They were juniors at a dive bar when Jasper tanked an interview by being too honest. Mason said "You're an idiot, but at least you're an honest idiot." They've been friends ever since.

## Education

Duke Lacrosse teammate of Jasper, UVA Law grad

## Appearance

Comfortable in a dive bar or a boardroom. The kind of guy who shows up with whiskey and hard truths.
