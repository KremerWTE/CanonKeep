# Elise Romano

**Archetype:** Analyst/Anchor - Data Storyteller

**Age:** 33

## Affiliations

- **Role:** The data storyteller. She translates analytics into narratives that sway sponsors, fans, and media. Brings sports-media credibility and crossover to corporate sponsors.
- **Wives Club:** Analyst / Data Storyteller

## Background

Grew up in Chicago, studied communications at DePaul. Got her break in NFL Network's morning show, then became Hard Hits' "stats and strategy" brain.

## Appearance

5'5", brunette with sharp eyes and bright smile. Polished but approachable, with a glam-yet-smart style.

## Personality

Analytical, quick-thinking, balances humor with insight. Has a gift for making complex strategy sound sexy.

## Relationships

Close ally of Nessa and Cassie, often helps package financial/policy decisions for public consumption. Pairs well with Cassie & Nessa on finance/policy/media crossovers.
