# Jordan Vega

**Archetype:** Global Brand Ambassador

**Age:** 35

## Affiliations

- **Role:** The global brand ambassador. She brings star power and connects the group into international media, Olympic networks, and global sponsorships.
- **Wives Club:** Global Brand Ambassador

## Background

One of the most famous U.S. soccer players of her era, the face of women's sports marketing. Continues to play at a high level internationally while balancing family life.

## Appearance

5'7", dark brunette with sleek waves, sharp cheekbones, and a radiant smile.

## Personality

Charismatic, media-savvy, polished. She thrives under the spotlight and uses it deliberately to advocate for equality in sports and pay.

## Relationships

Married to Alex Vega. Rivals Nessa and Cassie in sheer influence.
