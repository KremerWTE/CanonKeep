## Background

Nickname: "Penny"
Full name: Penelope Barker

Bay Area native, highly educated (studied literature and sociology before dropping out of grad school). Worked in education and corporate training before pivoting into cam/adult world. Unlike younger Sirens, chose this world consciously - seeing it as both liberation and way to control power dynamics.

## Appearance

Striking brunette, mid-30s, sharp cheekbones, piercing brown eyes, commanding presence.

## Wardrobe & Style

Subtle power: pencil skirts, silk blouses, heels - more CEO or professor than party girl.

## Personality

On camera: Confident, dominant, maternal - nurturing one second, cuttingly strict the next. Off camera: Incredibly sharp, analytical, planner. Loves debate, literature, wine tastings. The "older sister" of Sirens keeping wilder ones in check. Dry wit, not afraid to tell Selene when she is wrong.

## Relationships

Selene Thorne - Essentially second-in-command (Selene does not admit it)
The Sirens - Trainer and handler

## Character Arc

**Start:** Educated woman who chose this world for power

**Change:** Wrestles with being both performer and leader

**End:** Wants more influence, may push for autonomy or become rival power center
