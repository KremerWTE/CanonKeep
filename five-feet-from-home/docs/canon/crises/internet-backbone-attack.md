# Internet Backbone Attack

**Type:** Cyber / Financial

**Severity:** critical

**Location:** New York City

**Status:** planned

## Description

Attack on U.S. East Coast internet backbone threatening NYC financial district.

## Initial Situation

Trading systems failing. Markets about to halt.

## Complications

Cascading failures, multiple institutions, federal response.

## Resolution

Patch vulnerability before trading halts. Leak as "minor denial-of-service." Protect client hedge funds.
