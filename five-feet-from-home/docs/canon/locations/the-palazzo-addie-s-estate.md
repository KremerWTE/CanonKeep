# The Palazzo (Addie's Estate)

## Description

Addie's Charlotte estate, known informally as the Palace of Honor. A 15,000 sq ft Italian Renaissance-style mansion.

FEATURES:
- Grand ballroom for Wives Club galas
- Private apartments for visiting dignitaries
- The Rose Garden: Site of many intimate gatherings
- Wine cellar with Vatican-sourced vintages
- Private chapel with original medieval artwork

## Rules & World-Building

Invitation only. What happens at the Palazzo stays at the Palazzo.

## Story Significance

Addie's power base. Where the Wives Club's inner circle truly convenes. The most exclusive address in Charlotte.
