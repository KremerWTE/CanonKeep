# BSS Miami Lab

## Description

The Miami outpost of BSS, run by Harper Vance. Located in a renovated art deco building in Coral Gables.

OPERATIONS:
- Latin America and Caribbean focus
- Maritime operations coordination
- Selene's handler base
- Entertainment industry contacts

VIBE:
More casual than Charlotte. Harper's leadership style is reflected in the open floor plan and rooftop bar for debriefs.

## Rules & World-Building

Harper runs it her way. Charlotte respects the autonomy.

## Story Significance

Harper's domain. Where Selene reports. Gateway to Latin American operations.
