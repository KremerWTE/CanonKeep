# Chapter 15b: The Page Begins

## Book 2: "The Rain in Spain"
### Part Six: A New Role

---

The Page role changed everything.

Not immediately—nothing changed immediately. But gradually, like water reshaping stone.

She was no longer just an analyst. She was being groomed for something more.

---

**[THEME: NEW IDENTITY FORMING]**

The training was unlike anything at BSS.

Not intelligence protocols or crisis management. But presence. Holding space. The art of making others feel seen.

"You already know the skills," Addie explained. "What you're learning now is the spirit behind them."

"What spirit?"

"Every woman who comes through the Palace of Honor carries a wound. Your job isn't to fix it—it's to witness it. To say, through your presence: you are not alone, and you are not broken."

---

Bella shadowed Addie at events.

Galas where powerful women gathered. Intimate dinners where masks came off. Retreat weekends where decades of armor finally cracked.

She watched Addie work.

The way she listened—fully, without agenda.
The way she validated—not empty praise, but genuine recognition.
The way she challenged—gently, when someone was ready to grow.

*This is what I want to become. This is who I'm learning to be.*

---

"You're different around the women," Harper observed. "Softer. More present."

"Is that bad?"

"It's remarkable. I've never seen you this... open."

Bella considered that. She was open. With these women, she could be.

*Because they're not judging me. Because they're carrying the same wounds I am. Because we're all learning together.*

---

The pendant came three months in.

Not the full hierarchy—she was years away from that. But the first marker. The acknowledgment that she was on the path.

Addie presented it simply. No ceremony. Just the two of them in her office.

"This is yours now. What it represents is bigger than any metal or stone."

"What does it represent?"

"That you've chosen to serve. To carry the message of worthiness to others." Addie fastened it around her neck. "It's heavy at first. It gets lighter as you grow into it."

---

Bella touched the pendant.

Small. Simple. But it felt like an anchor. Something to hold when the voice got loud.

*You belong here. Someone believes in you. This is real.*

---

*One day at a time. One choice at a time. One step at a time.*

*I'm becoming someone. Someone I'm not ashamed of.*
