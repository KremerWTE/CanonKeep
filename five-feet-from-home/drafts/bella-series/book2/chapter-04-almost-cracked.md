# Chapter 4: Almost Cracked

## Book 2: "The Rain in Spain"
### Part Two: Trial by Fire

---

Doc didn't ask her to explain.

He just sat with her—on the cold tile floor of the hotel bathroom, the shower still running, the water bill climbing higher by the minute. He didn't touch her, didn't crowd her. Just sat close enough that she knew she wasn't alone.

"How long has it been like this?" he asked.

Bella shook her head, wiping at her eyes. "I don't know. A week. Maybe more."

"And you didn't tell anyone."

It wasn't an accusation. Just an observation.

"I didn't want to fail," she whispered. "Everyone was counting on me. Addie trusted me with this. I couldn't—I couldn't be the one who fell apart."

Doc was quiet for a moment. Then: "Bella, you're not falling apart. You're hitting a wall. There's a difference."

"Feels the same."

"It's not." He turned off the shower, handed her a towel. "Falling apart means you can't come back. Hitting a wall means you need to stop, rest, and find a different way through. You're not broken. You're just burning too hot."

Burning too hot.

The words sounded familiar. Something Hawk might say.

"How did you know?" she asked. "To be here. How did you know I'd need you?"

Doc smiled faintly. "Hawk knows things. He reads people the way most of us read books—every page, every line, every space between the words. He saw this coming before you left Charlotte. Didn't tell you because he wanted you to succeed. Just made sure someone was there if you couldn't."

Bella's chest tightened. "He was watching me? This whole time?"

"Bella, he's never stopped watching you. Since the day you walked into BSS, he's been making moves to keep you standing. That's what fathers do."

She started crying again—not from despair this time, but from something she couldn't name. Gratitude. Grief. The overwhelming realization that she wasn't as alone as she'd believed.

Doc helped her to her feet. "We're going to get you through the rest of this assignment. You're going to finish what you started. But you're also going to learn something important."

"What's that?"

"That asking for help isn't weakness. It's strategy."
