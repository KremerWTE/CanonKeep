# Chapter 6b: Proving Herself Again

## Book 2: "The Rain in Spain"
### Part Three: The Return

---

She went back to work after two weeks.

Too soon, probably. Addie had suggested a month. But Bella couldn't stand the waiting anymore. The stillness. The feeling that she was falling behind while everyone else moved forward.

"Are you sure?" Addie asked at the door.

"I'm ready."

She wasn't. They both knew it. But Addie let her go anyway.

---

The office felt different.

Or maybe she felt different in it. The same desks, same people, same rhythm. But now she noticed things. The pace. The pressure. The way everyone seemed to be running from something.

*Or maybe that's just you projecting.*

She settled at her desk. Pulled up her email. Three hundred unread messages. The familiar wave of anxiety—and something else. Almost like recognition.

*This is where it started. This is where you'll do it again if you're not careful.*

---

**[THEME: THE PATTERN REPEATS]**

Within a week, she was back to old habits.

First to arrive. Last to leave. Taking on extra projects. Volunteering for everything.

"You're supposed to be easing back in," Harper observed.

"I am."

"Bella. It's seven PM. You've been here since six AM."

"I have a lot to catch up on."

Harper looked at her for a long moment. Something unreadable in her expression.

"Just... be careful. Whatever happened over there—it can happen again."

"It won't."

But even as she said it, Bella wasn't sure.

---

The difference was the awareness.

Before Europe, she'd been blind to the pattern. Now she could see it—even as she repeated it.

*You're doing it again. You're trying to outwork your fear.*

The voice, but gentler. Not the critical one. Something newer. Something that sounded a little like Addie.

She ignored it. Just for now. Just until she'd proven herself again.

*Then I'll slow down. Once I've shown them I'm reliable. Once I've made up for failing.*

---

Jasper called her into his office.

"How are you settling back in?"

"Good. Fine. I'm taking it slow."

He glanced at the reports on her desk. The stack of completed analyses. The evidence of a week's worth of overtime.

"I can see that."

No judgment in his voice. But no approval either.

"You don't have to prove anything, Bella. Europe wasn't a failure. It was a lesson."

"A lesson in my limits."

"A lesson in human limits. Everyone has them. The best people learn where theirs are." He paused. "The dangerous people pretend they don't have any."

---

She thought about that all night.

*The dangerous people pretend they don't have any.*

Was that her? Pretending she was different? Special? Capable of what others couldn't manage?

Or was she just scared? Scared that without the hustle, she was nothing?

---

The journal entry that night:

*I'm doing it again. I know I'm doing it again. But I don't know how to stop.*

*The fear is too loud. The emptiness without work is too big.*

*How do I prove I'm worthy without proving anything?*
