# Chapter 10: Early Wives Club

## Book 2: "The Rain in Spain"
### Part Four: Finding Her Place

---

Bella's first Wives Club dinner wasn't what she expected.

She'd imagined something formal. Intimidating. A room full of powerful women in designer clothes, discussing things she didn't understand.

What she found was Addie's kitchen. Elena in an apron, stirring something that smelled like home. Harper uncorking wine. Kendra laughing at something Sara had said.

"You're here!" Elena greeted her. "Finally."

Bella hesitated at the threshold. "I wasn't sure if I was supposed to—"

"You're invited. That means you're supposed to." Harper pressed a wine glass into her hand. "Sit. Eat. Be."

---

**[THEME: THE FIRST BELONGING]**

They ate around a long table. No agenda. No hierarchy—at least not visible.

The conversation flowed in ways Bella didn't expect. Business and babies. Operations and outfit choices. Heavy topics and light ones, woven together like they were all the same.

"How are you settling in?" Sara asked. Direct, but kind.

"I'm... finding my way."

"That takes time. Longer than anyone tells you." She smiled. "We've all been the new one. It gets easier."

---

Bella watched how they interacted.

The way Harper tracked logistics even while relaxed.
The way Elena noticed who needed more wine, more food, more attention.
The way Addie held space without dominating.
The way Kendra and Sara anchored each other.

*This is what family looks like. When you're allowed to be yourself.*

She'd never had this. Not really. Not without conditions attached.

---

"You're quiet," Addie observed later, as they cleared dishes.

"I'm just... watching. Learning."

"Learning what?"

"How to be like this. Comfortable. Not performing."

Addie's smile was gentle. "That's not something you learn. It's something you allow. When you're ready."

"What if I'm never ready?"

"Then we'll keep having dinners until you are."

---

The pendant caught Bella's eye.

Harper wore one. Elena too. A small, delicate thing—barely noticeable unless you knew to look.

"Is that a symbol of something?" she asked.

Harper touched her pendant absently. "It's a Wives Club thing. You'll learn about it eventually."

"When?"

"When you're ready." A slight smile. "Everything here happens when you're ready. No rushing. No proving."

---

The voice was quiet that night.

Maybe because there was nothing to perform here. No test to pass. No evaluation happening.

Just women, eating together. Being together. Accepting each other's presence without demands.

*Is this what it feels like? To just... belong?*

She didn't trust it. Not fully. The voice whispered that she was fooling herself, that she'd do something wrong and they'd realize she didn't fit.

But for one evening, she let herself pretend.
