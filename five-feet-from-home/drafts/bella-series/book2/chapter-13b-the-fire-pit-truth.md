# Chapter 13b: The Fire Pit Truth

## Book 2: "The Rain in Spain"
### Part Five: The Reckoning

---

The third night of her forced rest, Bella asked the question she'd been avoiding.

"Hawk. What's wrong with me?"

He looked at her across the fire. No surprise in his expression. He'd been waiting for this.

"Nothing's wrong with you. Something happened to you. There's a difference."

---

**[THEME: NAMING THE ORIGIN]**

"What do you mean?"

"The worthiness wound. It didn't come from nowhere. Something—or someone—taught you that you weren't enough."

Bella felt the familiar tightening in her chest. The urge to change the subject.

"Does it matter where it came from?"

"It matters if you want to heal it. You can't fix what you don't understand."

---

The fire crackled. Stars emerged overhead.

"I don't remember a specific moment," Bella said finally. "It's just... always been there. As far back as I can remember."

"That's common. The earliest wounds are often pre-verbal. You felt unworthy before you had words for it."

"So how do I fix something I can't even name?"

"You don't fix it. You integrate it. Make peace with the child who learned that lesson and carry her forward with more kindness than she received."

---

The concept was foreign.

Kindness toward herself. Compassion for her own failures. Accepting the scared child she'd once been.

"I don't know how to do that."

"No one does at first." Hawk's voice was gentle. "It's a practice. Like anything else. You get better with repetition."

"How long does it take?"

"The rest of your life." He smiled slightly. "But it gets easier. And the peace you find is worth the work."

---

They sat in silence for a long time.

The fire settling into coals. The night deepening around them.

"Thank you," Bella said finally. "For... all of this."

"That's what we do here. We show up for each other."

"But why me? I'm not special. I'm just some analyst who keeps breaking down."

Hawk's gaze was steady.

"Everyone is worth showing up for. That's not about special. It's about human."

---

She cried that night.

Not from sadness—though that was part of it. From something releasing. The beginning of something new.

The voice was still there. Still whispering its old lies.

But for the first time, she could imagine a day when she wouldn't believe it.
