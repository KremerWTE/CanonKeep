# Chapter 15: The POH Page Offer

## Book 2: "The Rain in Spain"
### Part Six: A New Role

---

The call came from Addie.

"Can you come to my office? There's something I want to discuss."

Bella's heart rate spiked immediately. *What did I do wrong? Have they noticed I'm not working as hard? Is this about the guardrails?*

But Addie's voice had been calm. Warm, even.

Maybe it wasn't bad news.

---

**[THEME: RECOGNITION AND FEAR]**

Addie's office was elegant without being ostentatious.

Books lined the walls. Fresh flowers on the desk. A subtle scent of something—lavender, maybe—that made the space feel calm.

"Sit," Addie said. "This is good news. You look terrified."

"I always look terrified."

"I know. We're going to work on that." Addie smiled. "But first—an offer."

---

"I'd like you to become my Page."

The words didn't compute at first.

"Your... Page?"

"For the Palace of Honor. The Patroness needs a Page—someone who learns the role, who shadows me, who eventually takes on responsibilities of their own."

"But that's—"

"A significant step. I know." Addie leaned forward. "I've been watching you. Not just your work—your growth. You're changing, Bella. Slowly, but genuinely. That's rare."

---

"Why me?"

The question that always lived in her throat. Why me, when there are so many others?

"Because you understand worthiness as a wound, not just a concept. Because you're learning to heal, not just perform." Addie's gaze was steady. "The POH exists to remind women of their inherent worth. Who better to carry that message than someone who's lived the opposite and chosen differently?"

---

The offer sat between them.

Heavy with meaning. Terrifying in its implications.

"I don't feel worthy of this."

"Of course you don't. That's exactly why you're right for it." Addie reached across the desk, touched her hand. "This isn't about being worthy. It's about learning worthiness—and then teaching it to others."

"What if I fail?"

"You'll fail sometimes. Everyone does. That's not the question." Addie's voice was gentle. "The question is: will you get back up? Will you keep learning?"

---

Bella thought about the voice.

The one that said she wasn't enough. The one that had driven her to collapse twice. The one that still whispered, even now.

And she thought about the other voice. The one growing stronger. The one that said maybe, just maybe, she was worth more than her productivity.

"Yes," she heard herself say. "I'll do it."
