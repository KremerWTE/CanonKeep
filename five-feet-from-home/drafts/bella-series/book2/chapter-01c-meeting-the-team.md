# Chapter 1c: Meeting the Team

## Book 2: "The Rain in Spain"
### Part One: The Mentorship

---

The women of BSS didn't introduce themselves so much as reveal themselves, layer by layer, over time.

Bella learned their names first. Then their roles. Then, slowly, the people behind the titles.

---

**Harper Collins** was a force of nature.

Bella met her in the kitchen of the main building, both of them reaching for the coffee pot at the same moment.

"You're the new one," Harper said. Not a question. "Addie's protégée."

"I'm not sure I've earned that title yet."

Harper laughed—sharp, surprised. "Good answer. Most people try to claim more than they've earned. It's refreshing to meet someone who claims less." She poured two cups, handing one to Bella. "I'm Harper. I run operations from Miami, but I'm here this week. You'll see me at partner meetings."

Harper dressed like money—tailored blazers, silk blouses, statement jewelry that probably cost more than Bella made in a month. But beneath the polish was something harder. Something that suggested the elegance was armor, not affectation.

"Any advice?" Bella asked.

"Don't try to impress anyone. Just do the work. The right people will notice."

---

**Kendra Chen** was already familiar from the Watch Desk.

But Bella learned more about her in pieces: the former Secret Service background. The analytical mind that could see three moves ahead. The way she mentored her team with brutal honesty and unexpected warmth.

"You're good at patterns," Kendra told her during their second week. "But you second-guess yourself. Stop that."

"Easier said than done."

"Everything worth doing is." Kendra tapped her tablet. "I'm pulling you into a live case tomorrow. You'll work directly under me."

Bella's heart rate spiked. "I'm ready."

"No, you're not. But that's never stopped anyone here."

---

**Elena Barrett** was something else entirely.

Jasper's wife. Addie's sister-in-law. The CEO of Maison Aurelia—an events empire that Bella had read about in lifestyle magazines.

They met at a dinner at the compound. Elena was radiant—perfectly styled blonde hair, a cream silk dress that probably cost four figures, a smile that made you feel like the most important person in the room.

"You're the fixer," Elena said, taking Bella's hand in both of hers. "Addie talks about you."

"She does?"

"Constantly." Elena's eyes crinkled warmly. "She thinks you're going to be extraordinary. And Addie doesn't say that lightly."

Bella didn't know what to do with that information. So she just said, "Thank you."

"Don't thank me. Prove her right." Elena winked. "I have a feeling you will."

---

**Sara Chen** was Kendra's sister—not by blood, by choice. Elena's actual sister, the athletic one who ran a CrossFit empire and served as the family's grounding force.

Bella met her at the gym, both of them arriving for early-morning workouts before the compound woke up.

"You move like someone who's learning their body," Sara said, watching Bella struggle with a kettlebell. "That's good. Most analysts forget they have bodies."

"I'm not very athletic."

"Yet." Sara grinned. "Stick around. We'll fix that."

---

**Jasper Barrett** was the center of gravity.

Bella saw him from a distance at first—the man who'd built BSS from nothing, who commanded rooms without speaking, who Addie and Harper orbited like planets around a sun.

He was handsome in an old-Hollywood way, with silver at his temples and the kind of presence that made you straighten your posture.

Their first real conversation happened by accident. Bella was working late, and Jasper walked into the analysis room to check on something.

"You're Rossi," he said.

"Yes, sir."

"Don't call me sir. We save that for clients." He studied her for a moment. "Addie thinks you have what it takes."

"I hope so."

"Hope is a start. Proof is what matters." He nodded, already turning to leave. "Keep doing what you're doing."

It wasn't much. But it felt like being knighted.
