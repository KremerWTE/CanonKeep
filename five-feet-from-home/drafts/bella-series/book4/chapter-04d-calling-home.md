# Chapter 4d: Calling Home

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Two: Slow and Safe

---

"You're glowing."

Addie's voice through the video call was warm, knowing. She'd noticed immediately—before Bella had said anything, before the conversation had even begun.

"I'm just... good. Things are good here."

"I can see that." Addie smiled. "Tell me about her."

Bella blinked. "How did you—"

"Please. I've known you for years. I can read your face." Addie leaned back in her chair. "Also, Hawk told me."

"Hawk knows?"

"Hawk knows everything. You should be used to this by now."

---

Bella told her about Mandy.

The steel-green eyes. The broken past rebuilt into something beautiful. The way she understood without Bella having to explain. The fire pit conversations that lasted until the stars wheeled overhead.

"She sounds like exactly what you need," Addie said, when Bella finished.

"Is that... okay? With the family?"

"Of course it's okay. Why wouldn't it be?"

"I don't know. I felt like I was betraying something. Being here instead of there. Focusing on... personal things instead of work."

Addie was quiet for a moment.

"Bella, the work will always be there. Crises don't stop. Clients don't stop needing us. But if you burn yourself out serving everyone else, there'll be nothing left to give." She leaned forward. "What you're doing there—learning to receive love, letting yourself be happy—that's not betrayal. That's completion."

"Hawk said something similar."

"He usually does." Addie smiled. "Great minds."

---

Later, she called her parents.

This was harder. She hadn't told them about... any of it, really. Her romantic history was a closed book as far as they were concerned.

"You sound different," her mother said. "Happy."

"I am happy."

"The work?"

"Not just the work." Bella took a breath. "Mom, I'm seeing someone."

Silence on the line.

"His name?" her mother asked.

"Her name. Mandy."

More silence. Then, quietly: "Tell me about her."

So Bella did. About the ranch. About the veterans. About a woman who'd been broken and rebuilt herself into something better. About being seen, being chosen, being loved without conditions.

When she finished, her mother was crying.

"Mom?"

"I'm fine. I'm just..." A sniffle. "You've never talked about love like this. Never. You always kept that part of yourself locked away."

"I know."

"But you're not locked away anymore." Her mother's voice was thick with emotion. "That's all I ever wanted for you, sweetheart. To let someone in."

"I'm letting her in."

"Good. That's good." A pause. "Your father will be surprised, but he'll be fine. He just wants you happy."

"I am happy. Really, truly happy."

"Then that's enough. That's everything."

---

That night, Bella sat at the fire pit with Mandy.

"I told my parents about you."

Mandy looked up sharply. "How did that go?"

"My mom cried. Good tears, I think."

"And your dad?"

"He'll process. He always does." Bella smiled. "They want to meet you eventually."

"That's... big."

"Is it too much?"

Mandy shook her head slowly. "No. It's just... real. Making this real by telling people." She reached for Bella's hand. "I haven't been real with anyone in a long time."

"Me neither."

"Then we'll be real together." Mandy squeezed. "Step by step. Day by day."

"I can do that."

"I know you can." Mandy smiled. "That's why you're here."
