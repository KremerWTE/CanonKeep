# Chapter 4f: Calling Addie

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Two: Building Trust

---

She called Addie on a Thursday evening.

Not a scheduled check-in—just a need to hear a familiar voice. To connect with the life she'd left behind in Charlotte.

"How's my Page?"

"Learning. Slowly."

"Tell me."

---

**[THEME: MENTOR FROM AFAR]**

Bella described the ranch. The horses. The veterans. The stillness that was starting to feel less like threat and more like gift.

"You sound different," Addie observed.

"I feel different. Like something's loosening."

"The wound?"

"Maybe. Or the armor around it. I can't tell the difference yet."

---

"I'm working with Storm," Bella continued. "The gray mare. Former abuse case."

"I know that horse." Addie's voice softened. "Mandy showed me photos years ago. Couldn't be touched."

"She lets me brush her now. Took weeks of just... showing up. No pushing."

"That's how it works. With horses and with people."

---

They talked about the worthiness wound.

"I can see it now," Bella said. "Like it's outside me instead of inside. The voice is still there, but it's... separate. Something I observe instead of something I am."

"That's significant. Most people never get that distance."

"It doesn't feel significant. It feels like the beginning of a very long road."

"It is both." Addie paused. "The insight is the beginning. The practice is the rest of your life."

---

"I'm scared I'll forget," Bella admitted. "That I'll go back to Charlotte and fall into the old patterns."

"You might. Probably will, temporarily. The patterns are deeply grooved."

"Then what's the point?"

"The point is you'll notice faster. Catch yourself sooner. The falls become less deep, the recovery faster." Addie's voice was warm. "That's what growth looks like. Not perfection—just faster recovery."

---

Before hanging up, Addie said something that stuck.

"You're not healing alone, you know. Even from a distance, we're holding space for you. The family. The fire pit. The work we've done together. It's all still there."

"I know."

"And when you come back—when you're ready—it'll still be there. Nothing's been lost."

"Thank you."

"Thank yourself. You're the one doing the work."
