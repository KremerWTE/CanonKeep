# Chapter 4b: After

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Two: Slow and Safe

---

The morning after was soft.

Bella woke to sunlight filtering through the cabin window, Mandy's arm draped across her waist. She lay still, not wanting to break the moment, listening to the sounds of the ranch awakening outside.

This was real. This had happened.

And she wasn't running.

---

Mandy stirred, steel-green eyes opening slowly.

"You're still here," she murmured.

"I'm still here."

"That's good." Mandy's smile was sleep-soft. "I half expected to wake up alone."

"Old patterns?"

"Old experience." Mandy pulled her closer. "People like us—we're good at leaving. We've had to be."

"I don't want to leave."

"I know." Mandy kissed her forehead. "That's why I'm surprised."

---

They stayed in bed longer than either of them should have.

The ranch had schedules, obligations, a rhythm that didn't pause for personal revelations. But Mandy texted her second-in-command to handle the morning feeding, and Bella had no meetings until afternoon.

"Tell me about Charlotte," Mandy said, tracing patterns on Bella's shoulder. "Your life there. The people."

So Bella told her. About Addie, who'd become her mother. About Hawk, who'd taught her to see herself as a queen. About Kendra's precision and Harper's intensity and Elena's elegance.

"You have a family," Mandy observed. "A real one. Chosen."

"I do. I didn't know I was looking for one when I found them."

"That's usually how it works." Mandy's voice was quiet. "The things we need most find us when we stop forcing it."

---

"What about you?" Bella asked. "Family?"

"Not the traditional kind. My parents are gone. No siblings." Mandy shrugged. "The ranch is my family now. Earl. Maria. The staff. The people who show up and do the work."

"Is that enough?"

"It has been." Mandy's eyes met hers. "But I'm starting to wonder if there's more."

The implication hung in the air between them.

"I don't know what I'm doing," Bella admitted. "I've never... this is new."

"For me too. It's been years since I let anyone close." Mandy smiled. "We'll figure it out together. Or we won't. But either way, we're honest about it."

"I can do honest."

"I know you can. That's why you're here."

---

Eventually, they had to face the day.

Shower. Breakfast. Work. The ordinary rhythms that didn't care about emotional breakthroughs or midnight confessions.

But something had fundamentally changed.

Bella felt it in the way she moved through the ranch—lighter somehow, like a weight she'd been carrying had finally lifted. She felt it in the way veterans smiled at her, like they could see something different in her face.

She felt it in the way Mandy's eyes followed her across the dining hall, warm and knowing.

*This is what it feels like,* she thought. *Being loved without conditions. Being wanted for who I am, not what I can do.*

It was terrifying.

And wonderful.

And exactly what she'd needed.
