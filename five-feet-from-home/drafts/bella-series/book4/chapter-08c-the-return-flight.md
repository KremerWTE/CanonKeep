# Chapter 8c: The Return Flight

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Epilogue

---

The flight back to Charlotte was four hours long.

Four hours to think. To process. To prepare for what came next.

Bella spent most of it looking out the window, watching clouds.

---

**[THEME: THRESHOLD]**

Six months had changed her.

Not fixed her—she'd learned that "fixed" wasn't the goal. But changed. Shifted. Opened.

She'd arrived at the ranch broken open by work. She was leaving with tools to live differently.

*You're still the same person. Just... more aware.*

The voice was there—would always be there. But she could hear it now without obeying.

---

She thought about what waited in Charlotte.

The office. The team. The demands that had once consumed her.

And the family. Addie and Hawk. Harper and Elena. Kendra and Sara. The fire pit, waiting for her questions and answers.

*Home.*

The word felt different now. Less like a place she had to earn. More like somewhere she already belonged.

---

She thought about Matt.

Left behind at the ranch—but not really behind. They'd talked about it. About what long-distance looked like. About patience and presence and building something slowly.

"I'll visit," she'd promised.

"And I'll come to Charlotte," he'd replied. "Meet the famous family."

"They'll interrogate you."

"I expect nothing less."

---

The plane began its descent.

Charlotte emerging below—green hills, sprawling suburbs, the skyline she'd called home for years.

Her phone buzzed as they taxied.

Hawk: *Welcome home. Fire pit tonight?*

Harper: *Dinner at Elena's tomorrow. No excuses.*

Addie: *Proud of you. Ready when you are.*

The messages warmed something inside her. Connection. Belonging. People who'd held space while she healed.

---

Journal entry—written on the plane:

*Going home. Different person than when I left.*

*Still scared. Still wounded. Still carrying the voice that says I'm not enough.*

*But also: more aware. More tools. More understanding of what drives me.*

*The healing isn't done. It never will be. But I've made a start. Laid a foundation.*

*Time to see what I can build on it.*

*Charlotte is waiting. The family is waiting. My life is waiting.*

*I'm ready to step back into it.*

---

The plane landed. Bella gathered her things, walked through the terminal, emerged into Carolina sunshine.

She was home.

Not the old home—the place she'd worked herself to exhaustion trying to earn.

A new home. One she already belonged to.

One she'd finally learned to receive.
