# Chapter 7: The Transition

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Four: New Directions

---

The weeks that followed were a strange dance.

Bella and Matt spent more time together—trail rides, dinners, quiet evenings on his porch watching the stars. He moved slowly, deliberately, never pushing, never demanding. Just present.

"He's courting you," Mandy observed, watching them from a distance.

"Is that what this is?"

"Old-fashioned word for an old-fashioned man." Mandy smiled. "He's showing you who he is. Letting you decide if you want it."

"I don't know what I want."

"Yes you do. You just haven't admitted it yet."

---

Bella found herself comparing them.

Mandy: intense, complicated, a survivor who understood damage because she'd lived it. Their connection had been immediate, electric—two wounded people finding each other in the dark.

Matt: steady, simple, a healer who'd chosen to build rather than burn. Their connection was slower, gentler—warmth instead of fire.

Both real. Both valuable. Just... different.

"You can love more than one person," Hawk told her during one of their calls. "Not at the same time the same way, but over the course of a life. Each love teaches you something. Each one shapes you."

"What did Mandy teach me?"

"That you can be vulnerable. That you can let someone in. That you're worth loving." His voice was soft. "She gave you something essential. Now you're ready for the next lesson."

"What lesson is that?"

"Building. Creating. Making a life with someone instead of just surviving together."

---

The transition happened naturally.

Bella's time at the ranch shifted from intensive to occasional. She flew back to Charlotte for a week, then two, then three. BSS needed her. The family needed her. The life she'd built before the ranch was still there, waiting.

But she kept coming back.

For Matt's slow smile. For the trail rides into the mountains. For the way he made her feel—not on fire, but warm. Steady. Home.

"You're different when you're with him," Mandy observed, during one of Bella's visits.

"Good different?"

"Calm different. Like you finally stopped running."

"I didn't know I was running."

"We all run, Bella. Until we find something worth staying for."

---

One evening, after a long day at the ranch, Bella and Mandy had their final fire pit conversation.

Not final-final—they'd talk again, many times, for years to come. But final in the sense that it marked the end of one chapter and the beginning of another.

"I'm going to be okay," Mandy said.

"I know."

"No, I mean... I'm really going to be okay. You coming here, loving me, choosing me even briefly—that proved something." Her eyes were bright. "I thought I was too broken for love. You showed me I wasn't."

"You showed me the same thing."

"Then we saved each other." Mandy smiled. "That's not nothing."

---

"What happens now?" Bella asked.

"Now you go back to your life. You see where things go with Matt. You keep coming here when you can." Mandy shrugged. "We figure it out as we go."

"And us?"

"We're family now. Different than before, but family." Mandy reached over, squeezed her hand. "You don't get rid of me that easily."

"I don't want to get rid of you."

"Good. Because I'm invested in how your story turns out." Mandy's eyes sparkled. "And I have a feeling it's going to be a good one."
