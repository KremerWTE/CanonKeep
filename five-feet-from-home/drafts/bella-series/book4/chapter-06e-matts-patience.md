# Chapter 6e: Matt's Patience

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Three: Love and Learning

---

Matt was nothing like the men Bella had imagined for herself.

Not that she'd imagined men at all—her focus had always been on work, on proving, on earning. Romance was a distraction she couldn't afford.

But Matt was there anyway. Present. Patient. Impossible to ignore.

---

**[THEME: SLOW COURTSHIP]**

"You're not very subtle," Bella told him one evening.

"Never claimed to be." He smiled, that easy grin that seemed to come naturally. "Life's too short for games. If I like someone, I let them know."

"And you like me."

"I like you." No hesitation. No qualification. Just truth.

"Why?"

The question surprised him. "Why do I like you?"

"Most people like me for what I can do. What I produce. What I offer." She paused. "What's your reason?"

---

He thought for a long moment.

"I like how you are with the horses. Gentle but focused. I like how you listen—really listen—when the veterans talk. I like that you came here to heal instead of hiding behind work."

"That's not... achievement stuff."

"I know." His eyes were warm. "I like who you are, Bella. Not what you do. There's a difference."

The distinction felt foreign. She'd spent her whole life conflating the two.

---

Matt didn't rush.

Over the following weeks, he simply... appeared. At the barn when she was grooming. On the trails when she was riding. At the fire pit when she needed quiet company.

Always welcome. Never pushing.

"He's courting you," Mandy observed.

"Old-fashioned word."

"He's an old-fashioned man." Mandy smiled. "Not in a bad way. Just... patient. Steady. The kind who builds things to last."

---

Bella found herself looking forward to his presence.

Not the electric intensity she'd felt with Mandy—something different. Calmer. Like warm water instead of fire.

"I don't know how to do this," she admitted one evening.

"Do what?"

"Be... wanted. Without earning it. Without proving I deserve it."

Matt set down his coffee. Turned to face her fully.

"You don't have to prove anything to me. I'm already here. Already decided. The only question is whether you want me back."

---

"I don't know what I want."

"That's okay." His voice was gentle. "I'm patient. I can wait while you figure it out."

"What if I decide I don't want this?"

"Then I'll be disappointed. But I'll respect it." He paused. "Love isn't about getting what you want. It's about offering what you have and letting the other person choose."

---

Journal entry:

*Matt asked nothing from me today. Just showed up. Was present. Let me be however I needed to be.*

*It's disorienting. I keep waiting for the demand. The expectation. The moment when he reveals what I have to do to deserve his attention.*

*But it doesn't come. He just... offers. And waits.*

*Maybe this is what healthy love looks like. Patient. Present. No strings attached.*

*I don't know if I can accept it. But I want to try.*
