# Chapter 6d: The Second Trail Ride

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Four: New Directions

---

Matt asked her on a trail ride.

Not through Mandy. Not arranged or orchestrated. Just him, finding her at the barn one morning, hat in hand.

"There's a place I want to show you. If you're interested."

"What kind of place?"

"The kind that's better experienced than described." His smile was shy. "Bring a jacket. We'll be out a while."

---

They rode for hours.

Through aspen groves and meadows and along ridgelines with views that made Bella's breath catch. Matt led, silent mostly, pointing out landmarks occasionally. A creek where he'd seen a moose last spring. A rock formation that looked like a sleeping giant if you squinted right.

The destination was a small lake, perfectly round, fed by snowmelt from the peaks above.

"I come here when I need to think," Matt said, dismounting. "Nobody else knows about it."

"Nobody?"

"Just me. And now you."

---

They sat by the water, horses grazing nearby.

The silence was comfortable—not awkward, not pregnant with unspoken tension. Just two people existing in a beautiful place.

"Tell me about Charlotte," Matt said eventually.

So Bella told him. Not the sanitized version—the real one. About BSS and crisis management. About Hawk and Addie and the fire pit lessons. About becoming someone new.

Matt listened without interrupting.

"That's a lot," he said, when she finished.

"Is it too much?"

"It's just... a lot." He smiled. "I'm used to simple. Horses. Property. Fixing things that are broken. Your world sounds complicated."

"It is. But it's also... meaningful. We help people."

"I can see that." His eyes were thoughtful. "You light up when you talk about it. The helping part."

"Do I?"

"Like someone who's found their purpose." He picked up a stone, turned it over in his hands. "That's rare. Most people never find what they're meant to do."

---

"What about you?" Bella asked. "What are you meant to do?"

"I'm still figuring that out." Matt skipped the stone across the water. "The Army gave me purpose for a while. Then it didn't. The ranch helped me heal. Now I'm looking for what comes next."

"The vacation home business?"

"That's part of it. But there's something else. Something I haven't found yet." He glanced at her. "Maybe I'm waiting for it to find me."

The way he said it—the way he looked at her—made Bella's heart flutter.

"You're patient," she said.

"I have to be. Rushing things never works out."

"Even with people?"

"Especially with people." His voice was gentle. "The ones worth having are the ones you don't force. You create the conditions for them to choose you. Then you wait."

---

On the ride back, something had shifted.

The silence was still comfortable, but charged now with possibility. Bella found herself watching Matt's back—the way he sat in the saddle, the easy confidence of someone who knew exactly where they belonged.

*Maybe this is what it feels like,* she thought. *Being courted instead of chased. Being respected instead of pursued.*

When they reached the barn, Matt helped her dismount. His hands lingered at her waist—not inappropriate, just present.

"I had a good time," he said.

"Me too."

He leaned in, kissed her cheek—soft, brief, respectful.

"I had a good time too, Bella."

Then he walked away, leaving her standing in the barn with a racing heart and no idea what came next.

But for the first time in months, she was excited to find out.
