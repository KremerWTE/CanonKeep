# Chapter 7b: The Call from Hawk

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Four: New Directions

---

The weekly calls had become ritual.

Every Sunday at 7 PM, Bella found a quiet spot on the ranch and called Hawk. The fire pit in Charlotte, translated across miles.

"How are you doing?"

"Better. Different. Still figuring it out."

"That's honest."

---

**[THEME: DISTANCE AND GROWTH]**

"I miss the fire pit," Bella admitted.

"It'll be here when you're ready."

"What if I'm never ready? What if I stay here forever?"

Hawk was quiet for a moment. "Would that be bad?"

The question caught her off guard. She'd been assuming she had to return. That leaving Charlotte meant failing somehow.

"I don't know. Everyone's there. The family. The work. My life."

"Your life is wherever you are. Geography doesn't determine belonging."

---

"The worthiness thing," Bella said. "It's getting better."

"Tell me."

"I'm starting to see it. The voice, the hustle, the constant need to prove. It's like... I can watch it now instead of just being swept away."

"That's progress. Significant progress."

"It doesn't feel like much."

"It never does. From inside, growth feels like standing still. From outside, I can see how far you've come."

---

Hawk asked about Matt.

"He's patient. Steady. Nothing like what I expected."

"And what did you expect?"

"I don't know. Someone who'd push. Demand. Make me work for it." She laughed, embarrassed. "Because that's all I know how to do."

"And instead he just... waits?"

"He just waits. Says he's already decided. Says the only question is what I want."

"That must be terrifying."

"It's terrifying." Bella exhaled. "I keep looking for the catch. The condition. The moment when he reveals what I have to do to deserve him."

---

"What if there is no catch?"

Silence on the line.

"Then I don't know what to do." Bella's voice was small. "If I can't earn love, how do I get it?"

"You receive it. That's all. Just receive."

"I don't know how."

"You're learning. That's what this time is for." Hawk's voice was warm. "The ranch, the horses, Mandy, Matt—they're all teachers. Different lessons, same curriculum."

"What's the curriculum?"

"That you're already worthy. That you always were. That the hustle was never necessary—just a wall you built around a wound."

---

The call ended with the usual questions, reversed.

"How are you doing, Hawk?"

"Missing you. But glad you're where you need to be."

"And how can I support you?"

He laughed softly. "You already are. By growing. By healing. By becoming who you were meant to be."

"That's not support. That's just... living."

"Same thing, sometimes."

---
