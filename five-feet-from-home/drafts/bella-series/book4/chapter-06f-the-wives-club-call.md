# Chapter 6f: The Wives Club Call

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Three: Love and Learning

---

Harper called on a Sunday afternoon.

"The girls miss you," she said without preamble. "Elena wants to know if you're ever coming back. Kendra says you're dodging her texts."

"I'm not dodging. I'm just... slow to respond."

"Slow to respond is dodging, Bella."

---

**[THEME: FAMILY REACHING OUT]**

They talked for an hour.

Harper updating her on Charlotte—the office, the team, the subtle politics she'd missed while healing. Elena's latest Maison Aurelia triumph. Kendra's new certification. Sara's gym expansion.

"We're growing," Harper said. "All of us. But there's a Bella-shaped gap at the table."

"I'm not sure I'm ready to fill it."

"You don't have to be ready. You just have to show up."

---

"How are you really?" Harper asked eventually. "Not the polished version. The truth."

Bella thought for a moment.

"Healing. Slowly. Learning things I should have learned years ago."

"Like what?"

"That I don't have to earn my place. That rest is allowed. That the voice in my head is a liar."

Harper was quiet for a long moment.

"I fought that voice for years. Still do sometimes."

"You?"

"Me." Harper's laugh was rueful. "You think you're the only one with a worthiness wound? We all have one. Different flavors, same core."

---

The admission hit different.

Harper—confident, capable, seemingly untouchable Harper—had struggled with the same thing.

"How did you learn to manage it?"

"Practice. Support. Daniel, mostly—he kept holding up the mirror until I believed what I saw." She paused. "And the family. Knowing I wasn't alone."

"I thought I had to figure it out alone."

"No one figures anything out alone. That's the trap. The wound wants you isolated because isolation makes it stronger."

---

Before hanging up, Harper said something unexpected.

"We're proud of you, you know. For taking this time. For doing the work."

"It doesn't feel like something to be proud of."

"That's the wound talking. From out here, you look brave. Like someone who finally chose herself."

The words sat with Bella long after the call ended.

*Chose herself.*

Maybe that's what healing was. Choosing yourself, over and over, until it became natural.
