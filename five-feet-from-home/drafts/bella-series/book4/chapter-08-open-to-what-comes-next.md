# Chapter 8: Open to What Comes Next

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Epilogue

---

On her last night of that particular visit, Bella sat with Matt at his fire pit.

His property was smaller than Mandy's, but beautiful in its own way—a cabin he'd built himself, a paddock with three horses, a view of the mountains that never got old.

"You're leaving tomorrow," he said.

"I'll be back."

"I know." He smiled. "You always come back."

"Is that okay?"

"It's more than okay." He shifted to face her. "I know I'm not your first choice. I know you had something real with Mandy. I'm not trying to compete with that."

"Then what are you trying to do?"

"I'm trying to show you what's possible." His voice was steady. "Something different. Something that could grow into whatever you need it to be."

---

"I don't know what I need," Bella admitted.

"That's okay. We'll figure it out together." Matt reached for her hand. "I'm patient, Bella. I've been waiting for the right person my whole life. A few more months—a few more years—won't change that."

"You'd wait years?"

"For the right one? Absolutely." His eyes were warm. "And something tells me you're the right one."

---

She kissed him.

Not impulsive—deliberate. A choice, not a reaction.

He kissed her back, soft and sure. When they parted, he was smiling.

"That was nice."

"It was."

"Can I take you to dinner when you come back next time?"

"Like a real date?"

"Like a real date." His grin was shy. "I figure we've been dancing around this long enough."

"Yes." Bella smiled. "Yes, you can take me to dinner."

---

She left for Charlotte the next morning.

At the airport, she called Hawk.

"I'm coming home."

"Good. We've missed you." A pause. "How are things with Matt?"

"Good. Slow. But good." Bella smiled. "He's taking me to dinner next time I'm back."

"Progress."

"Yeah. Progress."

---

*Book 4 ends with Bella open to Matt, Mandy still in her life as friend/family/support.*

*She's learned what it means to be loved. She's learned what it means to let go without losing. She's learned that life isn't always about first choices—sometimes it's about the person who shows up at the right time, in the right way.*

*The fire is still burning. The dance isn't over.*

*But for the first time, Bella knows what she wants.*

*And she's ready to reach for it.*
