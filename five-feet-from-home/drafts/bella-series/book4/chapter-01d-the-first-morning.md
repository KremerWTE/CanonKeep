# Chapter 1d: The First Morning

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part One: The Ranch

---

Bella woke before dawn.

Old habits. The body's internal alarm that said *get up, get moving, prove something before anyone else is awake.*

But there was nothing to prove here. No meetings. No reports. No one tracking her hours.

Just silence. Mountains. The faint smell of horses and hay.

---

**[THEME: STILLNESS AS CONFRONTATION]**

She lay in bed, staring at the ceiling.

The stillness felt wrong. Dangerous. Like something would collapse if she didn't fill it with activity.

*This is what you came here for. Learning to be still. Learning to exist without producing.*

The voice was skeptical: *And what happens when they realize you're just... here? Taking up space? Not contributing?*

She didn't have an answer.

---

At 6 AM, she finally got up.

Padded to the kitchen. Found coffee already brewing—Mandy was an early riser too.

"Couldn't sleep?" Mandy asked, without turning around.

"Couldn't stay in bed. Different problem."

"You can sit on the porch if you want. Watch the sun come up. Nothing else required."

"Nothing else required." Bella repeated it like a foreign phrase. "I don't know what that means."

"You will." Mandy handed her a mug. "Give it time."

---

The porch faced east.

Blue Ridge mountains in the distance, catching the first light. Mist in the valleys. The world waking up slowly, without urgency.

Bella sat there for an hour.

Not reading. Not planning. Just... sitting.

The discomfort was physical. Her leg bounced. Her fingers tapped. Every cell screaming for action, for purpose, for something to do.

But she stayed. Forced herself to stay.

And somewhere around the 45-minute mark, something shifted.

---

Not peace exactly. More like... pause.

The voice quieted. The urgency faded. And for a few minutes, she was just a person on a porch, watching light change colors.

*This is what they mean. This is what I've been missing.*

The revelation was small but profound. She could exist without performing. The world didn't end.

---

When Mandy found her an hour later, Bella was still there.

"How do you feel?"

"Terrified. And a little bit... okay?"

Mandy smiled. "That's how it starts. Terrified and okay. They learn to coexist."

"Does it get easier?"

"The stillness? Eventually. You're retraining your nervous system. It takes time."

"How much time?"

"As long as it takes." Mandy sat beside her. "We're not on a schedule here. That's the whole point."

---

Journal entry:

*First morning. Sat on the porch for an hour. Nothing happened and everything happened.*

*I don't know how to describe it. The voice was there, screaming about productivity. But I stayed anyway. Waited it out.*

*And for a few minutes, I felt something like peace. Not permanent. Not complete. But real.*

*Maybe that's how healing works. Not all at once, but in glimpses. Small moments that accumulate.*

*I'm here. I'm trying. That has to count for something.*
