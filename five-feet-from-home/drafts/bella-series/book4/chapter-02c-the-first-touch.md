# Chapter 2c: The First Touch

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part One: Denver

---

It happened in the horse barn.

Bella was grooming one of the therapy horses—a gentle mare named Whisper—when Mandy appeared in the doorway. She leaned against the frame, watching.

"You're good with her," Mandy said.

"She's easy to be good with." Bella ran the brush along Whisper's flank. "She doesn't expect anything. She just wants to be cared for."

"Most creatures do." Mandy walked closer. "Most of us just forget how to ask for it."

She was standing close now. Close enough that Bella could smell her—something earthy, natural, like she'd been working outside.

"Can I show you something?" Mandy asked.

"Okay."

Mandy moved behind her, hands coming to rest on Bella's shoulders. "Feel how you're holding tension here?" She pressed gently. "You carry everything in your body. You've learned to hide it, but it's still there."

Bella felt herself stiffen at the touch—and then, slowly, relax.

"How do you know?"

"I recognize it. I was the same way." Mandy's hands moved to her arms, guiding her into a slightly different stance. "When I first started working with horses, I couldn't get any of them to trust me. They felt my tension. Smelled my fear. Until I learned to let it go, they kept their distance."

"And then?"

"Then they came to me." Mandy stepped back, leaving Bella oddly bereft of her warmth. "Just like people. Just like you."

---

Something had shifted.

Bella felt it in the way she noticed Mandy over the following days—the precise movements, the quiet competence, the steel-green eyes that seemed to see everything.

She felt it in the way her pulse quickened when Mandy entered a room.

She felt it in the dreams that left her waking, restless, confused.

---

"You're distracted," Earl observed, during one of their horse sessions.

"I'm processing."

"Processing what?"

Bella didn't answer. Couldn't answer. What would she say? *I think I'm developing feelings for the woman who runs this place, and I don't know what to do about it.*

"You've got that look," Earl continued. "Like you're fighting something internal."

"Maybe I am."

"Here's what I've learned: the fighting makes it worse. Whatever you're wrestling with—let it happen. Stop trying to control it." He patted Whisper's neck. "The horses taught me that. Some things, you just have to let unfold."

---

That evening, Bella found Mandy at the fire pit.

She was alone, staring into the flames the way Hawk did—present but distant, thinking thoughts she didn't share.

"Can I join you?"

Mandy nodded without speaking.

Bella sat. The fire crackled. The mountains were dark shapes against the stars.

"I feel something," Bella said finally. "I don't know what to call it."

"You don't have to call it anything." Mandy's voice was quiet. "It's okay to just... feel it. Without labeling it."

"Is that healthy?"

"It's honest. Which is better than healthy, sometimes." Mandy turned to look at her. "I feel it too, if that helps."

Bella's breath caught. "You do?"

"Since you arrived. Maybe before—when Addie first told me about you." Mandy smiled, soft and sad. "But I'm not going to push anything. Whatever this is, it moves at your pace. Or it doesn't move at all."

"I don't know what my pace is."

"Then you take your time figuring it out." Mandy reached over, squeezed her hand briefly, then let go. "There's no deadline here. No expectations. Just... space. To figure out what you want."

---

That night, Bella called Hawk.

"How's Colorado?" he asked.

"Complicated."

"Good complicated or bad complicated?"

"I don't know yet." She stared at the cabin ceiling. "There's someone here. Someone I might have feelings for."

"And?"

"And I'm terrified."

"That sounds right." Hawk's voice was warm. "Real connection is always terrifying. It means you have something to lose."

"What if I mess it up?"

"Then you'll learn something. And try again." A pause. "But you won't mess it up just by feeling, daughter. The messing up comes from running away. From refusing to sit with what's real."

"I've never been good at sitting with what's real."

"You've gotten better. That's what the last two years have been about." Another pause. "Trust yourself, Bella. You're further along than you think."
