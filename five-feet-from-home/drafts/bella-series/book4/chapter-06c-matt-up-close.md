# Chapter 6c: Matt Up Close

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Four: New Directions

---

She started watching him differently.

Not romantically—not yet. But with attention. Curiosity. The way she'd been trained to observe people at BSS, but softer. More personal.

Matt worked the ranch most mornings, helping with horses and fences before returning to his own property. His movements were unhurried, deliberate. He never rushed. He never raised his voice.

"You're staring," Earl observed.

"I'm noticing."

"Same thing, sometimes."

---

She learned about him in pieces.

From the veterans: Matt had served too. Army, not special forces. Regular infantry. Two deployments. Came home whole but changed.

"He doesn't talk about it much," James said. "But you can see it in his eyes. The thing we all have. The awareness that doesn't go away."

From Mandy: He'd been at the ranch for three years. Started as a client, stayed as a helper, eventually bought the adjacent property to build his vacation home business.

"He's good with horses because he understands patience," Mandy explained. "The same way you'd gentle a wild horse—reading its moods, adjusting your approach, never forcing anything."

From the staff: He was single. Never married. Had dated occasionally but nothing serious.

"He's waiting for the right one," the cook said knowingly. "Some people know what they want and won't settle for less."

---

Their first real conversation happened by accident.

Bella was in the barn, struggling with a stubborn latch on one of the stall doors. Matt appeared, tools in hand.

"Need help?"

"I've got it." The latch refused to budge. "Okay, maybe I don't have it."

Matt smiled—a small, quiet thing—and moved to examine the mechanism. His hands were sure, competent. Within thirty seconds, the latch clicked free.

"It sticks when the humidity changes. Happens every spring."

"How did you know that?"

"I've been fixing things around here for a while." He stepped back, wiping his hands on his jeans. "Most problems have simple solutions if you're patient enough to find them."

"That sounds like a philosophy."

"Just experience." His eyes met hers—brown, warm, deep. "You're Bella. From Charlotte."

"And you're Matt. From... here?"

"Originally Wyoming. But here now." He tipped his cap. "Nice to officially meet you."

---

Their conversations stayed brief. Practical.

A question about the horses. A comment about the weather. Small exchanges that lasted minutes but felt significant.

Bella found herself looking forward to them. To the quiet steadiness of his presence. To the way he didn't demand anything—just existed, comfortable in his own skin.

"You like him," Mandy said one evening.

"I don't know what I like."

"Yes, you do. You're just afraid to admit it." Mandy's smile was kind. "It's okay. Take your time."

"Aren't you... I don't know... jealous?"

"Jealous of what? The chance for you to be happy?" Mandy shook her head. "I told you—love isn't possession. I want you to find what you're looking for, even if it's not me."

---

That night, Bella wrote in her journal:

*Matt is different from anyone I've known.*

*He doesn't analyze everything to death. He doesn't have five contingency plans for every scenario. He just... lives. Moment to moment. Present.*

*I don't know if I'm attracted to him or to what he represents. Maybe both. Maybe that's okay.*

*Mandy says he's patient. That he'll wait. That some people know what they want and won't settle.*

*Maybe I'm what he wants. Maybe I'm not. But for the first time in months, I'm curious about something new instead of grieving something old.*

*That feels like progress.*
