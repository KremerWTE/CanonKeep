# Chapter 3c: The Trail Ride

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Two: Slow and Safe

---

The trail ride was Mandy's idea.

"There's a place I want to show you," she said one morning. "It's a few hours out. We'd need to pack supplies."

"Like camping?"

"Like being in the world without walls." Mandy's eyes sparkled. "Can you handle that?"

Bella thought about her climate-controlled compound apartment, her structured schedule, the carefully managed chaos of her life.

"I think I need to try."

---

They rode out after lunch.

The horses were steady, experienced trail animals. Mandy led on a black gelding named Shadow; Bella followed on Whisper, who she'd bonded with over the past weeks.

The trail wound through aspen groves, along ridge lines with views that stretched forever. The air got thinner as they climbed, cleaner somehow, like the altitude purified everything.

"This is my thinking place," Mandy said, as they paused at a lookout. "When the ranch feels too small, I come up here."

"It's beautiful."

"It's empty. That's what I need sometimes. Space with nothing in it." Mandy dismounted, leading Shadow to a stream to drink. "The Agency never gave us empty space. There was always the next mission, the next target, the next crisis. Here, I can just... exist."

Bella dismounted too, stretching legs that weren't used to hours in the saddle.

"I understand that. BSS can feel the same way. Always something brewing."

"But you're here now."

"I am." Bella looked at the mountains, the sky, the endless quiet. "I almost didn't come. I almost convinced myself I was too busy."

"What changed your mind?"

"Hawk told me to stop being afraid of possibilities." She smiled. "And he's usually right."

---

They made camp in a meadow as the sun set.

Mandy had packed light but efficiently: a small tent, sleeping bags, food that didn't need refrigeration. They built a fire together, the familiar ritual somehow different in this wild setting.

"No walls," Mandy said, settling onto a rock. "No obligations. Just us and the world."

"It's strange. Peaceful, but strange."

"Strange how?"

"I keep waiting for something to go wrong. For my phone to buzz with a crisis. For someone to need me." Bella pulled her jacket tighter. "I'm not used to being unnecessary."

"You're not unnecessary. You're just... off the clock." Mandy handed her a canteen. "That's not the same thing."

---

After dinner, they lay side by side, looking at the stars.

The Milky Way was visible here—actually visible, not washed out by city lights. Bella hadn't seen stars like this since... ever, maybe.

"I never saw the sky like this growing up," she said.

"Where did you grow up?"

"Small town, but still too much light pollution. And then Boston, and Charlotte." Bella traced patterns in the darkness. "I feel like I've been missing something my whole life without knowing it."

"That's what happens when you only know one way of living. You assume it's the only way." Mandy turned her head to look at Bella. "This life—ranches, mountains, sky—it's not for everyone. But for the right people, it's everything."

"Are you one of those people?"

"I became one. After everything else fell apart." Mandy's voice was soft. "The question is whether you could be too."

Bella let the question hang in the air, unanswered.

But something in her chest said *maybe*.

And that was new.
