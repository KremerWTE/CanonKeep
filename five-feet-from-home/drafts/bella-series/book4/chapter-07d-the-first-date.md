# Chapter 7d: The First Date

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Four: New Directions

---

"I'd like to take you to dinner."

Matt's words hung in the evening air. Simple. Direct. No games.

"Like a date."

"Exactly like a date." He smiled. "We've been dancing around this for months. I think it's time."

Bella felt the familiar surge of fear. The voice: *He'll expect something. You'll disappoint him. This is where it all falls apart.*

But another voice, newer and quieter: *Or maybe it's where something begins.*

"Yes," she heard herself say. "I'd like that."

---

**[THEME: CHOOSING CONNECTION]**

He took her to a small Italian place in town.

Nothing fancy—checkered tablecloths, candles in wine bottles, an owner who knew Matt by name.

"You're a regular."

"I live alone. Can't cook." He shrugged. "Maria takes pity on me."

The normalcy of it was disarming. No performance. No proving. Just two people eating pasta.

---

"Tell me something about yourself I don't know," Matt said.

Bella thought for a moment. "I've never actually dated. Like, properly."

"Never?"

"Too busy. Too focused on work. Too..." She paused. "Afraid, probably. That I'd be rejected. That whoever it was would see through me."

"See through to what?"

"To the truth. That I'm not as capable as I seem. That I'm scared all the time. That I don't actually deserve any of this."

Matt was quiet for a long moment.

"That's not what I see."

"What do you see?"

"Someone brave enough to tell the truth. Someone honest about her fears." His eyes were warm. "That's rare, Bella. Most people hide their whole lives."

---

She told him about the worthiness wound.

Not all of it—that would take years. But the outline. The constant voice. The hustle as coping. The crash that brought her here.

He listened without interrupting. Without fixing. Just... heard her.

"That sounds exhausting."

"It is. Every day, for as long as I can remember."

"And now?"

"Better. The ranch helps. The horses. The people." She met his eyes. "You help."

"I'm glad." No ego in it. Just warmth.

---

Walking back to his truck, he took her hand.

Natural. Easy. Like they'd been doing it for years.

"I had a nice time."

"Me too."

"Can we do it again?"

She smiled. "I'd like that."

---

Journal entry:

*I keep waiting for the moment when people realize I'm too much. Too broken. Too complicated.*

*Maybe I've been wrong about what people can handle. Maybe vulnerability isn't weakness—it's connection.*

*I'm terrified. But I said yes anyway. That has to count for something.*
