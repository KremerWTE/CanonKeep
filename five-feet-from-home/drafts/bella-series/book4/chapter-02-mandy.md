# Chapter 2: Mandy

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part One: Denver

---

Mandy's story came out in pieces.

Not all at once—she wasn't the type to unload her history on a stranger. But over the first few days, as Bella shadowed her around the ranch, fragments emerged.

Former Agency. The kind of operative who went places that didn't exist on official maps and did things that never appeared in reports. She'd been good at it. Maybe too good.

"I left as a wreck," Mandy admitted one evening, sitting on the porch as the sun dipped behind the mountains. "Nights sitting in the shower, crying until the water went cold. Couldn't sleep without nightmares. Couldn't wake up without wishing I hadn't."

Bella was quiet. She understood that kind of silence—the kind that said *I'm listening* without demanding more.

"This place saved me," Mandy continued, gesturing at the ranch around them. "Started as therapy. Ended up becoming my mission. Now I help other veterans find what I found here."

The ranch hosted veterans with PTSD—men and women who'd served, who'd seen things, who needed a place to heal that wasn't a hospital or a therapist's office. Mandy taught them to work with horses, to find rhythm in manual labor, to rebuild themselves one day at a time.

"Why veterans?" Bella asked.

Mandy's steel-green eyes met hers. "Because I know what it's like to carry weight you can't put down. And I know what it takes to learn how to set it down anyway."

The connection between them was immediate and disorienting.

Bella recognized something in Mandy—not the Agency background or the trauma, but the way she moved through the world. Careful. Observant. Carrying scars she didn't show.

And Mandy seemed to recognize something in Bella too.

"Addie sent you here for a reason," Mandy said on the third night. "She doesn't do anything without a reason."

"What do you think that reason is?"

Mandy smiled, slow and knowing. "I think she saw someone who needed a different kind of healing. The kind you can't get from fire pit talks and analyst reports."

Bella's breath caught.

She didn't ask what Mandy meant. She suspected she already knew.
