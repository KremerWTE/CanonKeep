# Chapter 3d: The Horse Who Knew

## Book 4: "Show Me"
### Part Two: Building Trust

---

Her name was Storm.

A gray mare with scars on her flanks and suspicion in her eyes. Former abuse case—Mandy had rescued her three years ago. Took two years before she'd let anyone near her.

"She knows," Mandy said, watching Bella approach the paddock.

"Knows what?"

"Pain. Trauma. The feeling of not being safe." Mandy paused. "She'll see it in you too."

---

**[THEME: MIRRORS]**

The first time Bella tried to approach, Storm bolted.

Not aggressive—just gone. To the far corner of the paddock, watching from a distance, ears flattened.

"Don't take it personally," Mandy said. "She does that with everyone at first."

"How do I get her to trust me?"

"You don't 'get' her to do anything. You show up. Day after day. No demands. No expectations. Eventually, she decides you're safe."

"That sounds like therapy."

"It is therapy. For both of you."

---

Bella showed up every day.

Just stood at the paddock fence. Didn't try to approach. Didn't push. Just... present.

The first three days, Storm stayed at the far end.

Day four, she came a little closer.

Day seven, she stood in the middle, watching.

Day ten, she walked up to the fence.

---

"Hello," Bella said softly.

Storm studied her with ancient eyes. Seeing something. Processing.

Then she lowered her head. Just slightly. A sign of acceptance.

"You did it," Mandy said from behind her.

"I didn't do anything."

"Exactly. You didn't push. You didn't demand. You just waited." Mandy smiled. "That's the hardest thing for people like you. Waiting without working."

---

Over the following weeks, Storm became Bella's teacher.

Not through words—horses don't speak. Through presence. Through reaction. Through the subtle language of body and breath.

When Bella arrived anxious, Storm kept her distance.
When Bella arrived calm, Storm came close.
When Bella tried to rush, Storm retreated.
When Bella practiced patience, Storm rewarded her with trust.

"She's a mirror," Bella realized.

"All horses are. They show you yourself—the parts you try to hide."

---

One afternoon, Storm let Bella brush her.

Standing still, trusting, as Bella worked through the tangles in her mane. The intimacy of the moment caught Bella off guard.

*This creature was hurt. Badly. And she's choosing to trust me anyway.*

The parallels weren't subtle.

Bella had been hurt too. Different wounds, but real ones. And she'd been hiding from trust for years.

*If Storm can learn to let someone close again...*

---

"The worthiness thing," Bella said at dinner that night. "It's connected, isn't it? To why I can't trust people."

"How so?"

"If I don't believe I'm worth loving, I can't accept love when it's offered. I keep waiting for the catch. The condition. The moment when they realize I was never good enough."

Mandy nodded slowly. "That's exactly right. The wound makes you push away the medicine."

"How do I stop?"

"Same way you approached Storm. Show up. Be present. Don't demand. Let trust happen naturally."

Journal entry:

*Storm let me brush her today. A horse who couldn't be touched for two years let me touch her.*

*I want to believe it's about me—that I'm special, that I did something right. But that's not it. I just showed up. Consistently. Without agenda.*

*Maybe that's how healing works. Not through effort or achievement. Through presence. Through patience. Through proving, day after day, that you're safe.*

*I want to be safe for people. I want to be the person others can trust.*

*First I have to trust myself. That's the part I keep skipping.*
