# Chapter 5c: The Almost

## Book 1: "Wouldn't It Be Loverly"
### Part Two: Boston College Years

---

The winter formal was supposed to be unremarkable.

Bella was there as always—not on the arm of a date, but working the event from behind the scenes. Checking on the DJ. Making sure the caterer didn't run out of appetizers. Smoothing over the drama when two girls showed up in nearly identical dresses.

Sophie had come alone too. She stood at the edge of the dance floor, watching the crowd with the same analytical distance she brought to everything.

Their eyes met across the room. Sophie raised an eyebrow. Bella rolled hers.

Two observers, observing each other observe.

---

"You look nice," Sophie said when Bella finally took a break. They'd found a quiet corner near the windows, away from the thumping bass.

Bella glanced down at herself—a simple black dress she'd worn to three previous events, heels she'd kicked off under a table somewhere. "I look like I've been running around for four hours."

"That too." Sophie's smile was softer than usual. "But still nice."

Bella didn't know what to do with the compliment. She never knew what to do with compliments.

---

They ended up outside, sitting on the steps of the venue, sharing a bottle of water Sophie had liberated from the catering staff.

"Can I tell you something?" Sophie asked, her voice quieter than Bella had ever heard it.

"Of course."

"I'm not... great at this. Connections. Feelings. I spent most of my life studying how people work from the outside because the inside felt too complicated."

Bella waited.

"But with you..." Sophie turned to look at her. The winter air had pinked her cheeks. Her dark hair was coming loose from its updo. "I don't have to explain myself with you. You already get it."

Bella's heart was beating faster than it should have been.

"I get it too," she said. "The not having to explain part."

Sophie leaned closer. Bella could smell her perfume—something floral, unexpected, softer than she would have guessed.

"What do you want, Bella?" Sophie asked. "Not what you should want. Not what people expect you to want. What do you actually want?"

The question hung between them.

---

Later, Bella would tell herself she didn't know what happened next. That the moment had been ambiguous. That Sophie had simply been asking an intellectual question, the way she asked about everything.

But that wasn't true.

What happened was: Bella froze. The proximity, the intensity, the feeling that something was about to shift permanently—it overwhelmed her. All the old programming kicked in. Be careful. Don't want too much. Don't reach for things that might be taken away.

"I should go check on the caterers," Bella said, standing too quickly, nearly spilling the water.

Sophie's face closed off. The softness vanished, replaced by something careful and controlled.

"Of course," she said. "The event."

"Right. The event."

Bella walked back inside on shaky legs, feeling like she'd failed a test she hadn't known she was taking.

---

That night, lying in bed, she replayed the moment over and over.

*What do you want?*

She wanted to be seen. She wanted to matter. She wanted someone to choose her—not for what she could do, but for who she was.

And Sophie had been offering exactly that.

And Bella had run.
