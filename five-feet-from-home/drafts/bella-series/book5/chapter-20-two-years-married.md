# Chapter 20: Two Years Married

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Eight: The Endgame

---

Their anniversary fell on a Tuesday.

No grand celebration—Charlotte was teething, and neither of them had slept properly in days. Instead, they had takeout on the porch after bedtime, wine that tasted like victory.

"Two years," Matt said. "We made it."

"Was there doubt?"

"Not about us. About everything else—the world, the challenges, whether we'd survive the first year of parenthood." He grinned. "But we did. We're still here."

"Still here. Still us."

---

Bella thought about the changes.

She was a different person than the woman who'd walked down the aisle. Softer in some ways. Harder in others. More certain of what mattered and less tolerant of what didn't.

"You're thinking too much," Matt observed.

"Occupational hazard."

"What are you thinking about?"

"Identity. How it shifts. Whether I'm still the person you married."

Matt set down his glass.

"You're you. The essential you hasn't changed—the drive, the compassion, the way you see five steps ahead while making the current step look effortless." He took her hand. "You've grown. Evolved. That's what people do. But the core? The part I fell in love with? That's exactly the same."

"How can you be sure?"

"Because I pay attention." He kissed her knuckles. "Every day. To everything."

---

She'd made an effort, despite the exhaustion.

A simple dress—sage green, flowing, comfortable enough for a mother of an active toddler. Sandals that slipped on and off easily. Hair loose, because she couldn't summon the energy for more.

But she'd worn the pendant. And earrings—the first time in weeks. And a touch of lipstick that Matt had noticed immediately.

"You look beautiful," he'd said.

"I look tired."

"Beautifully tired."

"That's still not a thing."

"It's definitely a thing. I made it a thing. It's ours now."

---

The gift exchange was simple.

Matt gave her a photo album—their first two years, documented. Wedding. Honeymoon. Pregnancy. Charlotte. All of it, printed and preserved.

"So we remember," he said. "When things get hard. When we forget why we started."

Bella gave him a letter.

Not a gift, exactly—words on paper. Everything she felt and struggled to say aloud. The gratitude, the love, the daily miracle of being chosen by someone who kept choosing her.

He read it slowly. Twice. Then set it aside and pulled her close.

"I don't deserve you."

"You deserve everything. And you'll spend the rest of our lives getting it."

---

Charlotte woke at 11 PM.

Anniversary dinner interrupted by a crying child with sore gums. They tag-teamed the soothing: Matt walking, Bella singing, eventually both of them slumped on the nursery floor while Charlotte finally settled.

"Not how I imagined tonight ending," Bella admitted.

"Better, though."

"Better how?"

"Real." Matt's arm was around her shoulders. Charlotte was a warm weight in his other arm. "This is real life. Messy and exhausting and perfect in its imperfection."

"That's very poetic."

"Two years with you. It was bound to rub off."

---

They made it to bed around 1 AM.

Too tired for anything but sleep. But Matt held her close, and she listened to his heartbeat, and somewhere in the half-awake haze, she understood something.

This was what she'd been looking for. Her whole life, through all the searching and struggling and becoming. This—a partner who stayed. A child who needed her. A life that mattered.

She'd found home.

Not a place. Not a position on someone else's chessboard.

Home was this. Here. Now. Them.
