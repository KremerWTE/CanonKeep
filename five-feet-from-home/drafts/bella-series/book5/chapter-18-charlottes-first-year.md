# Chapter 18: Charlotte's First Year

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Seven: A New Chapter

---

The year unfolded in milestones.

First smile—real, deliberate, in response to Matt making ridiculous faces. First laugh—at the horses, of course, because Charlotte was her father's daughter. First solid food, first tooth, first word ("Dada," which Matt never let Bella forget).

Each moment felt both eternal and fleeting. Time moved strangely in the presence of a growing child.

---

At six months, Charlotte began sitting up.

At seven months, she was crawling—fast, determined, heading for whatever she wasn't supposed to touch.

At nine months, she pulled herself up on the furniture and looked around like she'd conquered Everest.

"She's going to walk early," Matt predicted.

"She's going to run," Bella corrected. "And we're going to spend the rest of our lives chasing her."

---

The ranch became Charlotte's playground.

Supervised, always—but she knew the barn, the horses, the wide open spaces that had become home. She reached for everything, curious about everything, fearless in a way that both thrilled and terrified her parents.

"She has no sense of self-preservation," Bella observed, pulling Charlotte away from a fence for the third time in an hour.

"She has total confidence that someone will catch her." Matt grinned. "That's different."

"Is it better?"

"Ask me when she's a teenager."

---

**[MILESTONE MONTAGE - FIRST YEAR]**

The photos accumulated.

Charlotte in her christening dress—yes, they'd done that, for both grandmothers' sake—looking uncertain about the whole affair.

Charlotte at the Charlotte compound, held by a beaming Hawk who'd softened in ways Bella hadn't thought possible.

Charlotte covered in birthday cake, her first birthday party a magnificent disaster of frosting and joy.

Charlotte on Matt's shoulders, tiny hands gripping his hair, surveying her kingdom from the highest point available.

Each image a chapter. Each chapter part of a story still being written.

---

The first birthday party was held at the ranch.

Everyone came. Everyone. Matt's family, Bella's families—all of them—the BSS contingent, Mandy with a ridiculous number of gifts. Even some of the veteran ranch hands, tough men who melted visibly in Charlotte's presence.

"She's going to be something," one of them said, watching her toddle between guests. "Look at that determination."

"She's already something," Bella replied. "She's ours."

---

That night, after the guests left and Charlotte was asleep, Bella and Matt sat on the porch.

"One year," she said. "We've kept a human alive for one year."

"We've done more than that. She's thriving."

"She is, isn't she?" Bella smiled. "We're actually good at this."

"Are you surprised?"

"A little. Are you?"

"No." Matt took her hand. "I knew you'd be an amazing mother. I saw it in how you care for everyone around you—the family, the program kids, even the horses. You have a gift for seeing what people need."

"So do you."

"We make a good team."

"The best team."

---

Looking back on the year, Bella could mark the changes.

Charlotte had transformed them—softened edges, shifted priorities, revealed capacities neither had known they possessed. But they'd also remained themselves. Still partners. Still passionate about their work. Still building something meaningful.

"What's next?" Matt asked.

"More of this. Growing. Evolving. Seeing who she becomes."

"And who we become, watching her."

"That too."

Bella leaned into him, watching the stars emerge.

"I never imagined this. Any of it. When I was young, when I was lost—I couldn't have pictured this life."

"Is it what you wanted?"

"It's better." She turned to kiss him. "It's so much better."
