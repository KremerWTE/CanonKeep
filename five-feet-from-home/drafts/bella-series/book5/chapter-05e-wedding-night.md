# Chapter 5e: Wedding Night

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Three: The Wedding

---

Mandy had prepared the cabin.

Not the guest cabin where Bella had stayed during her first visit—a different one, set apart from the main property, overlooking the valley. Private. Perfect.

Candles flickered on every surface. Rose petals scattered across the floor. A fire crackled in the stone fireplace, casting dancing shadows.

"She's a romantic," Matt observed, surveying the scene. "Who knew?"

"Anyone who's paying attention." Bella stepped inside, breathing in the scent of roses and woodsmoke. "This is beautiful."

"You're beautiful."

She turned to find him watching her, that look in his eyes—the one that still made her breath catch, even after all this time.

"We're married," she said.

"So people keep telling me."

"It still doesn't feel real."

"Give it time." He crossed to her, took her hands. "By tomorrow morning, it'll feel very real."

---

**[INTIMATE SCENE - EXPAND]**

They took their time.

The wedding night wasn't frantic or desperate—they'd been together long enough for that urgency to have settled into something deeper. Instead, it was intentional. Savoring.

Matt undressed her slowly, each button and hook a small ceremony of its own. The dress pooled at her feet. The bracelet stayed—she couldn't bring herself to remove it.

"My wife," he murmured against her skin. "I get to say that now."

"Your wife." She tested the word. Liked how it tasted.

They moved to the bed—turned down, fresh sheets, more rose petals. Matt was gentle, thorough, patient as always. He touched her like she was precious. Like he had all the time in the world.

And maybe he did. They both did, now.

---

Afterward, they lay tangled together, watching the fire die.

"No regrets?" he asked, echoing his question from the dance floor.

"Zero." She traced patterns on his chest. "You?"

"Only that I didn't meet you sooner."

"We weren't ready sooner. Either of us."

"Maybe." He kissed her hair. "Or maybe we would have figured it out."

"Maybe." She smiled against him. "But I like our story the way it is. Mandy. The ranch. All of it leading here."

"Our story," he repeated. "I like the sound of that."

---

They talked until the small hours.

About the wedding—their favorite moments, the things they'd been too busy to notice. About the future—the ranch they'd share, the life they'd build. About trivial things—whether they preferred waffles or pancakes, what to name the dog they might get someday.

"We have forever to figure all this out," Bella said, yawning. "We don't have to solve everything tonight."

"I know. But I like planning with you."

"Because you're a planner."

"Because I'm a planner who's married to another planner." He grinned. "Our kids are going to be so organized."

"Kids," she said softly. "You really want them?"

"With you? Absolutely." He looked at her, serious now. "But only if you do. And only when you're ready."

"I think I might be ready sooner than I expected."

"Yeah?"

"Being here, at the ranch, with Mandy's veterans..." She paused, choosing words. "It made me think about family differently. About what I want to build."

"Tell me."

"I want roots. Real ones. The kind I never had growing up." She met his eyes. "I want a home full of noise and mess and people who belong to each other. I want Sunday dinners that we host. I want traditions we create. I want..."

"Everything," he finished.

"Everything."

---

They slept eventually.

Wrapped around each other, rings catching the last glow of the embers, breathing in sync. Husband and wife. Partners for life.

Outside, the mountains stood silent and eternal, bearing witness to another love story beginning.
