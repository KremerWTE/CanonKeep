# Chapter 2b: Fire Pit About Matt

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part One: Courtship

---

Back in Charlotte, the fire pit felt different.

Not the ritual itself—that was unchanged. Hawk's presence, the whiskey, the flames dancing in the darkness. But Bella's heart was elsewhere, and Hawk knew it.

"Tell me about him," Hawk said. "The real version, not the edited one."

So Bella told him.

About Matt's patience. His steadiness. The way he treated every creature—horses, dogs, people—with the same gentle respect. About how he listened more than he talked, but when he spoke, every word mattered.

"He sounds like a builder," Hawk observed.

"What do you mean?"

"Some people destroy. Some people maintain. And some people build." Hawk stirred the fire. "Your whole life, you've been around maintainers—people managing crises, keeping things from falling apart. Matt sounds like someone who creates. Who makes things grow."

"Is that good?"

"It's different. And different might be exactly what you need."

---

"He wants to meet you," Bella said.

Hawk raised an eyebrow. "Does he know what that means?"

"He knows I call you Dad. He knows you're important." She paused. "I think he's nervous."

"He should be." Hawk's grin was wolfish. "I'm terrifying."

"You're a teddy bear."

"Don't tell anyone. It'll ruin my reputation."

---

They sat in comfortable silence for a while.

"I used to think love was about finding someone who completed me," Bella said finally. "Like there was a piece missing, and I needed someone else to fill it."

"And now?"

"Now I think I was wrong. I'm not missing anything—not anymore." She looked at the flames. "Matt doesn't complete me. He... complements me. Like we're two whole people who happen to fit together."

"That's a mature understanding." Hawk's voice was approving. "Most people never get there."

"I had good teachers."

"You had decent teachers and you were an excellent student." He raised his glass. "To growth, daughter. And to the rancher who's smart enough to see what you're worth."

---

"There's something else," Bella said.

"Tell me."

"Mandy helped. With Matt, I mean. She saw him before I did—saw that we might fit. She practically positioned him for me to find."

"That doesn't surprise me."

"It doesn't?"

"Real love isn't possessive." Hawk's eyes were thoughtful. "Mandy loved you enough to want your happiness, even if it wasn't with her. That's rare. That's valuable. And that's exactly the kind of person you should keep in your life."

"So I should still be friends with her?"

"You should be whatever works for both of you." He shrugged. "There are no rules about this, Bella. No templates. You figure it out as you go, with honesty and care."

---

Before she left the fire pit, Hawk said one more thing:

"The black king makes his move."

Bella smiled. "You and your chess metaphors."

"They work." He stood, stretched. "You've been playing white for a while now—setting the board, controlling the game. But Matt's playing black. He responds. He waits. He lets you make the first move."

"Is that good?"

"It means he trusts you. He's not trying to control the game—he's trying to play it with you." Hawk met her eyes. "That's what partnership looks like, daughter. Two people moving in response to each other, building something together."

"I think I'm ready for that."

"I know you are." He smiled. "That's why I'm not worried."
