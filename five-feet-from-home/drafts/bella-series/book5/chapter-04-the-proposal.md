# Chapter 4: The Proposal

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Two: The Proposal

---

Matt proposed on a Tuesday.

Not at sunset. Not at a fancy restaurant. Not with a crowd of onlookers and a photographer hiding in the bushes.

They were in the barn, mucking stalls, covered in hay and horse smell. Bella was complaining about a difficult case at BSS, gesturing with her pitchfork, when she realized Matt had stopped working.

He was looking at her the way he always looked at her. But there was something different in his eyes.

"What?" she asked, suddenly self-conscious.

Matt set down his shovel. Took off his hat. And knelt in the hay.

"Bella," he said. "I've been waiting my whole life for someone like you. And I've been watching you for months now, waiting for you to believe you deserve to be loved. I'm not gonna wait anymore."

He pulled a small box from his pocket. Inside was a ring—simple, elegant, not flashy.

"Will you marry me?"

The world stopped.

Every voice in Bella's head went silent. The old doubts, the familiar fears—all of it, gone. There was only this moment. Only Matt, kneeling in the hay, looking at her like she was the only thing in the world that mattered.

"Yes," she whispered.

Then, louder: "Yes."

Matt slid the ring onto her finger, stood up, and pulled her into a kiss that tasted like dust and horses and something she'd never dared to hope for.

When they finally pulled apart, she was laughing and crying at the same time.

"This is really happening," she said.

"This is really happening," Matt confirmed. "And I'm not going anywhere."

Later, she called Hawk and Addie. Their reaction was everything she'd hoped for—joy and pride and the particular warmth of parents watching their daughter find happiness.

"The black king found his square," Hawk said.

"Took him long enough," Addie added.

And Bella, for the first time in her life, let herself believe she'd won.
