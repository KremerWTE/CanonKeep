# Chapter 2: Gentling

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part One: Courtship

---

Mandy had said it first: Matt treated her the same way you'd gentle a horse.

Bella didn't understand what that meant until she watched him work.

One morning, she arrived at the barn early and found Matt with a new rescue—a mare who'd been neglected, skittish and defensive, flinching at every shadow. Most trainers would have tried to push through the fear, force the horse to submit.

Matt just stood there.

He didn't reach for her. Didn't approach. He just existed in her space, calm and unhurried, until the mare's breathing slowed.

It took an hour before she let him touch her.

"That's how you do it," Bella said from the fence, awed.

Matt glanced over. "Do what?"

"That's how you've been with me. The whole time. You just... wait."

He walked over, leaned against the fence beside her. "Some things can't be rushed. A scared horse. A heart that's been hurt. You push too fast, you lose the trust you're trying to build."

"Most people don't have that kind of patience."

"Most people aren't worth waiting for." He looked at her steadily. "You are."

Bella's breath caught.

She'd spent years convincing herself she wasn't worth the effort. That anyone who got close would eventually realize they'd made a mistake. That love was a transaction she could never afford.

But Matt wasn't transacting. He wasn't calculating. He was just present.

"How do you know?" she whispered. "That I'm worth waiting for?"

Matt smiled—slow, warm, certain. "Because I know horses. And I know people. And I've never been wrong about either."

He reached over and took her hand. Gentle. Unhurried.

And Bella let him.
