# Chapter 3c: Nine Months

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part One: Courtship

---

Nine months.

Nine months of trail rides and fire pits. Nine months of flights between Charlotte and Colorado. Nine months of learning each other's rhythms, tolerating each other's flaws, building something steady and real.

"You're commuting across the country," Kendra observed one morning.

"It's working."

"For now. But at some point, someone's going to have to move."

Bella had thought about this. Constantly. But every time she approached the question, she flinched away from it.

"We'll figure it out."

"I'm sure you will." Kendra's expression was unreadable. "Just make sure you're not avoiding the conversation because you're afraid of the answer."

---

Matt raised it first.

They were on one of their trail rides, the mountains gold with autumn color. He'd been quieter than usual, and Bella could feel him building toward something.

"I've been thinking," he said finally.

"About?"

"About us. About the future." He stopped his horse, turned to face her. "I love you, Bella. That hasn't changed, isn't going to change. But we can't keep doing this forever—splitting time, living in airports."

"I know."

"So what do we do?"

Bella had her own thoughts, but she wanted to hear his first. "What do you want?"

"I want you. Full stop." His eyes were steady. "I'd move to Charlotte if that's what it took. I'd sell the ranch, the cabins, everything. But I don't think that's what you want."

"What do I want?"

"I think you want what the ranch offers. Peace. Quiet. Space to breathe." He smiled. "I think you're tired of crisis mode. I think you want a life that moves at a different pace."

She couldn't deny it. She'd been feeling it for months—the pull toward something slower, simpler, more grounded.

"But my family is in Charlotte. My work is in Charlotte."

"Your family loves you wherever you are. And your work..." He shrugged. "You're smart, Bella. You'd figure out how to work from anywhere."

---

That night, she called Addie.

"He wants me to move to Colorado."

"And what do you want?"

"I don't know." Bella stared at the ceiling of her cabin. "Part of me wants to say yes. But part of me is terrified of leaving everything I built in Charlotte."

"What are you actually afraid of?"

Bella thought about it. "Being forgotten. Being replaced. Losing my place in the family."

"That's not how family works." Addie's voice was gentle. "You'll always have a place with us, Bella. Whether you're in Charlotte or Colorado or the moon. Geography doesn't change love."

"But I'd be so far away."

"You're already far away half the time. The only difference would be which city you call home." A pause. "What does your gut say?"

Bella closed her eyes. Listened to herself.

"It says yes."

"Then trust it." Addie smiled—Bella could hear it. "We'll miss seeing you every day. But we'd rather have you happy and far away than miserable and close."

---

She told Matt the next morning.

"I want to move here. With you."

His expression shifted—surprise, hope, joy. "You're sure?"

"I'm sure. I'll need to figure out work, and I'll fly back for important things, but..." She took his hands. "I want to build a life here. With you."

"That's all I've ever wanted."

"I know." She kissed him. "I'm done running, Matt. I'm ready to stay."
