# Chapter 13: Adjustments

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Six: Growth

---

Pregnancy changed everything.

Not in dramatic ways—more like a thousand small adjustments, each one reshaping her daily life.

Coffee was out. The smell alone made her nauseous. Matt switched to decaf in solidarity, though she caught him sneaking regular at the barn.

Riding was restricted. "Light riding only," the doctor said. "Nothing strenuous. Nothing that could result in a fall."

Travel was complicated. BSS work continued, but the constant flights were harder. More exhausting. More uncomfortable.

"You could work remotely," Matt suggested. "Full-time. Let Hawk handle the in-person stuff."

"That feels like quitting."

"That feels like prioritizing."

---

The first trimester was hardest.

Constant nausea. Bone-deep exhaustion. Mood swings that caught them both off guard.

"I cried at a horse commercial yesterday," Bella admitted. "A COMMERCIAL. There wasn't even a sad part."

"Hormones."

"I know it's hormones. That doesn't make it less annoying."

Matt handed her crackers—the only food that consistently stayed down—and said nothing. Smart man.

---

Work adapted.

Bella restructured her BSS role, delegating more to the Charlotte team, focusing on strategic oversight rather than operational involvement.

"It's the right move regardless," Hawk told her. "You've been doing the job of three people. This forces you to trust your team."

"I do trust my team."

"You trust them to do things your way. That's different from trusting them to find their own way."

---

The second trimester brought relief.

The nausea faded. Energy returned—not to pre-pregnancy levels, but enough. She found herself nesting: organizing closets, ordering baby supplies, reading parenting books like they were case files.

"You're preparing for a child, not a hostile negotiation," Matt observed, watching her take notes.

"Preparation is preparation."

"Fair enough."

---

Her wardrobe evolved again.

At first, she'd resisted maternity clothes—stubborn, trying to make regular clothing work. But by month five, surrender was necessary.

She found a style that still felt like her: fitted dresses that accommodated her belly without being frumpy. Stretchy jeans paired with flowing tops. Comfortable but still polished.

The pendant stayed. Now it rested just above her bump, a reminder of where she'd been and how far she'd come.

---

The baby shower was held at the compound.

Addie insisted on hosting. The Wives Club women came—not just the Charlotte contingent, but representatives from around the country. The barn was decorated in neutral yellows and greens.

"We didn't find out the sex," Bella had reminded everyone. "So no pink or blue."

They'd listened. Mostly.

Gifts piled high: practical items, beautiful items, sentimental items. Tessa flew in from New York. Harper from Miami. Even Mandy came, bringing a hand-knitted blanket that her mother had made.

"She made it before she passed," Mandy explained. "She always hoped one of us would have kids. I think she'd want your baby to have it."

Bella held the blanket—soft, worn, full of love from a woman she'd never met.

"Thank you. This means... I can't even..."

"I know." Mandy squeezed her hand. "Some gifts don't need words."

---

That night, setting up the nursery, Bella held the blanket again.

"We're bringing a child into a complicated world," she said. "A world with chosen families and biological families and all these overlapping relationships."

"That's not complicated," Matt said. "That's rich."

"What do you mean?"

"Our kid is going to have grandparents everywhere. Aunts and uncles who aren't related by blood. A network of people who love them before they're even born." He draped the blanket over the crib rail. "That's not a burden. That's a gift."

Bella considered.

"When did you get so wise?"

"Married you. Your wisdom rubbed off."

"That's not how wisdom works."

"How do you know? Are you a wisdom expert?"

She laughed—real, genuine laughter, the kind that had become more common since she'd found him.

"I love you."

"I love you too." He kissed her forehead. "Now let's finish this nursery. The baby will be here before we know it."
