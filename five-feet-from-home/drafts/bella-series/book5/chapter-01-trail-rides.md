# Chapter 1: Trail Rides

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part One: Courtship

---

The trail rides became their ritual.

Every weekend that Bella was at the ranch, Matt would saddle two horses and they'd ride out into the hills. Sometimes they talked. Sometimes they didn't. The silence between them was comfortable in a way Bella had never experienced with anyone besides Hawk at the fire pit.

Matt was patient.

Not the kind of patience that came from waiting for something, but the kind that came from being content with where he was. He didn't push her to define what they were. Didn't ask for more than she was ready to give. He just showed up, steady and present, and let things unfold at their own pace.

"You're different," Bella said one afternoon, as they paused on a ridge overlooking the valley.

"Different how?"

"Most people want something. They're always moving toward the next thing. You just... are."

Matt considered this, adjusting his hat against the sun. "I've had the life where I chased things. Didn't make me happy. Learned that the good stuff usually finds you when you stop running."

"What's the good stuff?"

He looked at her, really looked at her. "This. Right here. A beautiful day. Good company. Nowhere else I need to be."

Bella felt something shift in her chest.

She'd spent her whole life trying to be enough. Trying to prove herself. Trying to earn the right to be seen.

Matt didn't seem to need any of that. He just liked being around her.

"You make it sound simple," she said.

"It is simple. People make it complicated."

They rode on, the horses picking their way down the trail.

And for the first time in her life, Bella let herself imagine that maybe—just maybe—love didn't have to be earned.

Maybe it could just be.
