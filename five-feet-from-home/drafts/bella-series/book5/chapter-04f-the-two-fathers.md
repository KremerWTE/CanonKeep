# Chapter 4f: The Two Fathers

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Two: The Proposal

---

The question hung in the air for weeks.

Who would walk her down the aisle?

Hawk was her father in every way that mattered—the one who'd taught her, claimed her, shaped her into who she'd become. But her biological father was still her father. The man who'd given her life. The man who'd watched her grow up, even if he'd never quite understood her.

"I don't know what to do," Bella admitted to Matt one evening.

"What does your gut say?"

"My gut says both. But that's not how it works."

"Says who?"

---

She called her biological father first.

"Dad, I need to talk to you about something."

"The wedding?"

"About who walks me down the aisle." Bella took a breath. "I love you. I've always loved you. But there's someone else who's important to me—Hawk. He's been like a father to me for years now."

Silence on the line.

"I know," her father said finally.

"You know?"

"Bella, I'm not blind. I see how you talk about him. How your face lights up when you mention Charlotte." His voice was quiet but not hurt. "I've always known you'd find people who understood you better than I could."

"That doesn't mean I love you less."

"I know that too." A pause. "What are you asking me?"

"Would you be willing to share the walk? You and Hawk, together? He could take the first part, and then hand off to you for the final steps. Or the other way around."

Another silence. Then, to her surprise, her father laughed.

"Your mother said you'd figure out a way to include everyone. She was right."

---

The conversation with Hawk was harder.

They sat at the fire pit, the familiar ritual providing comfort.

"I want you to walk me down the aisle," Bella said. "But I also want my biological father there. I don't want to choose."

"Then don't."

"It's not that simple."

"Why not?" Hawk's eyes were steady. "You have two fathers. Both love you. Both should be part of this moment."

"You're okay with sharing?"

"Daughter, I'd share anything for you." He smiled. "Besides, your biological father and I talked at the Charlotte celebration. He's a good man. Quiet, like you described. But good."

"You talked?"

"We had a lot in common. A daughter we both love. A woman who exceeded anything we could have imagined." Hawk shrugged. "He's not threatened by me, Bella. And I'm not threatened by him. We're just... different parts of your story."

---

The solution came together.

Her biological father would wait at the altar, beside Matt. Hawk would walk her down the aisle, the way she'd always imagined. At the halfway point, her biological father would step forward to meet them. The three would walk the final stretch together. Then both fathers would kiss her cheek, shake Matt's hand, and take their seats.

"It's unconventional," Elena said, when Bella explained the plan.

"So is my entire life."

"Fair point." Elena smiled. "We'll make it beautiful."

---

The night before the wedding, Bella sat with both fathers.

The compound fire pit—Hawk's territory. But her biological father was there too, uncomfortable but willing, because she'd asked.

"Thank you," she said. "Both of you. For being here. For sharing this."

"There's nothing to share," her biological father said quietly. "We're just two men who love the same woman in different ways."

"Both of you shaped me," Bella continued. "Dad, you gave me roots. You showed me what steadiness looked like, even when I didn't appreciate it. And Hawk, you gave me wings. You showed me I could fly."

"Roots and wings," Hawk murmured. "Not a bad combination."

"The best combination." Bella smiled through tears. "Tomorrow, when we walk together, I want you both to know—I'm not choosing between you. I'm honoring both of you. Because both of you are my father. And I'm grateful for you both."
