# Chapter 6b: Settling In

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Four: New Beginnings

---

The first week was an adjustment.

Bella had lived in Charlotte for years—the compound, the family, the familiar rhythms of BSS. Now she was hours away, in a place that was hers but still felt borrowed.

"It takes time," Matt reminded her. "This isn't supposed to feel like home on day one."

"I know. I just..."

"Miss them?"

"Terribly."

"Then call them. Visit them. This isn't exile, Bella. It's just... geography."

---

She unpacked slowly.

Clothes into the closet Matt had emptied for her. Books onto the shelves he'd built. The few personal items she'd brought—photos, mementos, the leather journal she'd been keeping since Charlotte.

The pendant went into a jewelry box on the dresser. She wore it less now, but its meaning hadn't faded.

"Where do you want this?" Matt asked, holding up a framed photo.

It was from the wedding—her with both fathers, beaming between them. Hawk in his suit, her biological father in his. Both looking at her like she was their greatest achievement.

"The mantel," she decided. "Where we'll see it every day."

---

**[SETTING DETAILS - THE RANCH HOUSE]**

The house was comfortable, not grand.

Two stories, built for function over show. A wraparound porch with rocking chairs and a view of the mountains. A kitchen that Matt had renovated himself, with butcher block counters and open shelving.

The living room was the heart—stone fireplace, worn leather furniture, walls lined with windows. Books everywhere. Horse photographs. A few of Matt's rodeo trophies gathering dust in a corner.

The bedroom was simple: large bed, soft linens, more windows looking out at the property. A bathroom with a claw-foot tub that Bella had already claimed as her favorite feature.

There was a guest room, currently empty. A room that could become an office. And a small room at the end of the hall that neither of them mentioned but both knew would someday be a nursery.

---

She established routines.

Morning coffee on the porch, watching the sun climb the mountains. Then a call to Charlotte—Hawk or Addie, sometimes both. Then work, because BSS didn't stop just because she'd moved.

The guest house had been converted into her office. Satellite internet, secure communications, everything she needed to stay connected. She could run operations from here almost as easily as from Charlotte.

Almost.

---

"You're working too much," Matt observed, a week in.

"I'm working exactly enough."

"Bella." He sat on the edge of her desk. "We talked about this. Balance. Being present. Not letting the job consume you."

"I know. But there's a lot happening—"

"There's always a lot happening. That's the nature of the business." He took her hands. "Let Hawk and the others handle some of it. You don't have to carry everything."

She wanted to argue. Instead, she sighed.

"You're right."

"I know I'm right. I'm also making dinner. Come inside when you're ready."

---

The ranch hands were welcoming.

There were four of them—men who'd worked with Matt for years, who knew the property and the horses better than anyone. They accepted Bella without question, even if they weren't quite sure what to make of her.

"She's city," she overheard one say. "Fancy clothes, soft hands."

"She's also the boss's wife," another replied. "And I've seen her ride. She's got skills."

"Skills from where? Those horses aren't show ponies."

"Give her time. She'll prove herself."

Bella smiled and went to prove herself.

---

By the end of the second week, she'd found her place.

Mornings with the horses—not just riding, but learning the operation. Feeding schedules, veterinary care, the business side of running a working ranch.

Afternoons at her desk, managing the parts of BSS she couldn't delegate. Video calls with Hawk, strategy sessions with Elena, occasional crisis management that reminded her why she'd loved this work.

Evenings with Matt. Cooking together, talking about their days, planning for the future. The youth program they wanted to build. The expansion they were dreaming of.

Nights in his arms, feeling more at peace than she'd ever imagined possible.
