# Chapter 5: The Wedding

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Three: The Wedding

The wedding was at the ranch.

Not the compound. Not a fancy venue. The place where Bella had learned to heal, to trust, to believe she was worthy of love.

Mandy gave them her blessing. More than that—she helped plan the whole thing.

"This is your day," she told Bella, steel-green eyes soft. "I want to see you happy. That's all I've ever wanted."

The Wives Club came in force. Elena and Harper and Kendra and all the women who had become Bella's sisters over the years. They decorated the barn with lights and flowers. They filled the guest cabins. They treated the event like the coronation it was.

And when the day finally came, Bella stood in front of a mirror in her wedding dress, unable to believe the woman looking back at her.

Addie appeared behind her.

"You're beautiful," Addie said simply.

"I don't feel real," Bella admitted. "Like any minute I'm going to wake up and this will all have been a dream."

Addie turned her around, held her by the shoulders.

"Listen to me. You are real. This is real. You have worked harder than anyone I know to get to this moment—not just the wedding, but the ability to let yourself have it. That's the victory, Bella. Not the ring. Not the dress. The fact that you're standing here, ready to say yes."

Bella's eyes filled with tears.

"Thank you," she whispered. "For everything. For seeing me. For choosing me."

"We chose each other," Addie said. "That's how family works."

---

Hawk walked her down the aisle.

Not her biological father—he was there too, beaming from the front row. But Hawk was the one who'd taught her she was worth the journey. It felt right that he be the one to deliver her to the end of it.

"You ready?" he asked, arm steady under hers.

"I think so."

"Wrong answer." He looked at her with those sharp, knowing eyes. "You are ready. You've been ready for longer than you know. Say it."

Bella took a breath. "I'm ready."

"Damn right you are."

They walked together, toward Matt, toward the future, toward everything Bella had never believed she could have.

And when she said "I do," she meant it with every fiber of her being.
