# Chapter 3: Falling

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part One: Courtship

---

It happened gradually, then all at once.

One morning, Bella woke up in her cabin and realized she'd been thinking about Matt before she opened her eyes. His laugh. The way he tipped his hat. The quiet steadiness of his presence.

She was falling.

The terror came with it.

Every old voice in her head screamed warnings: *This is when it falls apart. This is when he realizes you're not worth it. This is when you get hurt.*

But for the first time, another voice answered back. Hawk's voice, from all those fire pit lessons: *The armor doesn't protect you. It just keeps you from feeling the good parts too.*

She called Hawk that night.

"I'm scared," she admitted, pacing her cabin in the dark.

"Of what?" His voice was steady, unsurprised.

"Of wanting this. Of believing it could be real."

"That's growth," Hawk said. "The old Bella would've run by now. Would've ended it before he could. The fact that you're still there? That's the progress."

"What if I'm making a mistake?"

"What if you're not?"

Silence stretched between them.

"Here's what I know," Hawk continued. "Matt's a good man. I've had him checked out—don't look at me like that, I can hear your expression through the phone. He's solid. And more importantly, he sees you. The real you. Not the performance. Not the fixer. You."

Bella's eyes stung. "How do I trust that?"

"Practice. Same as everything else. Every time the doubt comes, you tell it to shut up. Every time you want to run, you stay. And every time it feels too good to be true, you let yourself believe it might just be true."

She took a shaky breath. "I don't know if I can do that."

"You can. You've already been doing it. With Addie. With me. With the fire pit. This is just the next level."

She laughed, despite herself. "You make everything sound like a video game."

"Life is a game, daughter. The only question is whether you're playing to win or playing not to lose. Those are very different things."

That night, Bella didn't run.

And the next morning, when Matt showed up for their trail ride, she let herself smile—really smile—and believe, just for a moment, that this might be real.
