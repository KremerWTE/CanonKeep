# Chapter 5c: The Ceremony

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Three: The Wedding

---

The barn was transformed.

String lights cascaded from the rafters. Wildflowers—mountain laurel, wild azaleas, black-eyed Susans—filled every corner. The afternoon sun streamed through the new windows Matt had installed, painting everything gold.

Bella stood at the entrance, heart pounding.

Hawk was beside her, dressed in a dark suit that somehow didn't diminish his essential wildness. His arm was steady under hers.

"Last chance to run," he said.

"Not a chance."

"Good." He smiled. "Then let's go make you a wife."

---

The music started—a string quartet playing something soft and romantic.

Grace went first, scattering flower petals with the intense concentration of a child who'd been practicing for weeks. Then Tessa, elegant in a dusty rose bridesmaid dress. Then Harper. Then Kendra. Then Elena.

And then Bella.

The guests rose. She could see Matt at the altar—tears already streaming down his face, a smile so wide it looked like it might split him open.

Hawk walked her forward, step by step.

Halfway down the aisle, her biological father rose from his seat. He joined them, taking her other arm. The three of them walked together—two fathers delivering their daughter to the man who would love her next.

---

At the altar, Hawk kissed her cheek.

"You're the best investment I ever made," he whispered. "Be happy, daughter."

Her biological father kissed her other cheek.

"I'm proud of you," he said simply. "I always have been."

Then they shook Matt's hand and took their seats.

And Bella turned to face the man she was about to marry.

---

The vows were their own.

Matt went first:

"Bella, when I met you, I'd given up on finding someone. I'd made peace with being alone, with building my life around horses and mountains instead of a partner. Then you showed up at Mandy's ranch, and everything changed.

"You're the bravest person I know. You've fought battles I can't imagine. You've rebuilt yourself from the ground up. And somehow, in the middle of all that, you found room for me.

"I don't have fancy words. I'm just a rancher who got lucky. But I promise you this: I will be your steady ground. When the world is spinning, I'll be the thing that doesn't move. When you doubt yourself—and I know you will, because old habits die hard—I'll be there to remind you of who you really are.

"I love you. Every version of you. The confident one and the scared one. The capable one and the vulnerable one. All of you. Forever."

---

Then Bella:

"Matt, I spent my whole life believing I wasn't worth choosing. I ended relationships before they could fail. I pushed away anyone who got too close. I built walls so high even I couldn't see over them.

"And then you appeared. Not with a battering ram. Not with demands or ultimatums. Just... patience. You waited for me to be ready. You showed me what love looked like when it wasn't desperate or conditional.

"I choose you. Not because you're the only option. Not because you're safe or convenient. I choose you because you see me—really see me—and you love what you see. That's the greatest gift anyone has ever given me.

"I promise to be your partner. To build a life with you, not just beside you. To let you gentle me when I'm wild, and to push you when you need pushing. To grow old together, riding trails, watching sunsets, being grateful for every day.

"I love you. I can't wait to see what we become."

---

The officiant pronounced them husband and wife.

Matt kissed her—soft at first, then deeper, like he was sealing a promise.

The guests erupted in cheers.

And Bella, for the first time in her life, felt completely, utterly, unconditionally whole.
