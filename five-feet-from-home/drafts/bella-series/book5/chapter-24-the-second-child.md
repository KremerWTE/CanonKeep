# Chapter 24: The Second Child

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Eight: The Endgame

---

The conversation happened over breakfast.

Charlotte was in her high chair, methodically destroying a pancake. Matt was reading emails. Bella was trying to drink coffee before it got cold.

"I've been thinking," she said.

Matt looked up. "About?"

"Another one."

He set down his phone. Charlotte, sensing a Moment, paused her pancake destruction.

"Another child?"

"Maybe. If you want to. If we're ready." She paused. "Are we ready?"

---

The question opened a cascade.

Could they handle two? Was the ranch set up for it? What about work—both BSS and the program? What about Charlotte, who'd been the center of their universe for two years?

"We survived one," Matt said slowly. "We'd survive two."

"That's not the most enthusiastic endorsement."

"I'm not trying to endorse. I'm trying to think out loud." He reached for her hand. "I want more kids with you. I've always wanted that. The question is timing."

"When is the timing ever right?"

"Never. Which might mean it's always right." He grinned. "Circular logic, but it works."

---

They decided to try.

Not intensely, not with tracking and schedules. Just... stop preventing. Let nature decide.

"If it happens, it happens," Bella said.

"And if it doesn't?"

"Then Charlotte is enough. More than enough." She looked at their daughter, currently wearing more pancake than she'd eaten. "She's already everything."

---

**[FLASH FORWARD - THREE MONTHS]**

It happened faster than expected.

The test—different bathroom, same two lines—came back positive on a Tuesday morning.

This time, Bella knew immediately. Her body remembered.

"Again," she said to Matt, showing him the stick.

His face split into a grin so wide it looked almost painful.

"AGAIN."

---

Charlotte's reaction was mixed.

At two and a half, she understood enough to know something was changing. Not enough to grasp what.

"Baby in Mama's tummy," she repeated, poking Bella's still-flat stomach. "When come out?"

"In a while, sweetheart. Many months."

"How many?"

"A lot of months."

"That's SO LONG." Charlotte's outrage was complete. "Baby come NOW."

"Patience," Matt said, laughing. "You're going to need a lot of it."

---

The pregnancy was different this time.

Less nausea. More fatigue. An awareness of what was coming that hadn't existed before.

"You know the ending now," Addie said, during a visit. "That makes the journey different."

"Better or worse?"

"Just different. Less fear of the unknown. More fear of the known." Addie smiled. "But also more excitement. Because you know how good that ending is."

---

The announcement was simpler.

A video call with both families. Charlotte holding a sign that said "BIG SISTER" in wobbly letters Matt had helped her draw.

Reactions ranged from screaming (Grace) to silent tears (both grandmothers) to quiet pride (Hawk, predictably).

"Two grandchildren," he said. "I'm becoming greedy."

"You've earned it."

"Have I?"

"Dad." Bella's voice was firm. "You've earned everything you have. Stop questioning it."

---

As her belly grew, Bella felt the shape of her future solidifying.

Two children. A partner. A purpose.

This was the life she'd been moving toward all along, even when she couldn't see it. Every piece—Charlotte (the compound), Charlotte (the child), Matt, the program—all of it forming a picture she'd never imagined but now couldn't imagine without.

"Black moves second," she murmured, one evening, feeling the baby kick.

The baby kicked back. Agreement, maybe. Or just a future full of energy, waiting to be born.
