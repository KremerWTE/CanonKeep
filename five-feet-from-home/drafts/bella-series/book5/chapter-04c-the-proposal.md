# Chapter 4c: The Proposal

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Two: The Proposal

---

It happened on a Sunday.

Nothing special about the day—just a quiet morning, the kind they'd had dozens of times before. Coffee on the porch. Horses grazing in the paddock. The mountains catching the first light of sunrise.

But Matt was different. Bella could feel it—a tension in his shoulders, a carefulness in his movements.

"Everything okay?" she asked.

"Everything's perfect." He smiled, but his hands were shaking slightly. "I want to show you something."

---

They rode out to his secret lake.

The same place he'd taken her on their first extended trail ride, months ago now. The water was mirror-still, reflecting the peaks above, untouched and pristine.

"This is where I knew," Matt said, dismounting.

"Knew what?"

"That you were the one." He helped her down, keeping her hands in his. "When we sat here, that first time, you asked me what I wanted out of life. I told you I was still figuring it out."

"I remember."

"I'm not figuring anymore." He reached into his jacket pocket. "I know exactly what I want, Bella. I want you. I want to build a life with you. I want to wake up next to you every morning and watch the sun set with you every evening."

He knelt.

Bella's heart stopped.

"Bella Rossi, will you marry me?"

---

The ring was simple—a single diamond on a gold band, elegant without being ostentatious. It looked like something that would survive ranch work, survive real life, survive everything they'd face together.

"I chose it myself," Matt said, his voice catching. "If you don't like it, we can—"

"I love it." Bella's eyes were streaming. "I love you."

"Is that a yes?"

She laughed—a joyful, uncontrollable sound. "Yes. Yes, of course it's yes."

Matt slid the ring onto her finger. A perfect fit.

Then he stood, pulled her into his arms, and kissed her like the world was ending and beginning all at once.

---

They stayed at the lake for hours.

Talking about the future. About where they'd live. About whether they'd have children (yes, someday, when they were ready). About all the small and large details of building a life together.

"I have to tell Hawk," Bella said eventually.

"I already asked him."

"You what?"

"Last time I was in Charlotte." Matt grinned. "Went to him privately. Asked for his blessing. He said yes, on one condition."

"What condition?"

"That I let him plan some kind of tactical operation for the proposal. I told him I had my own plans." Matt shrugged. "He seemed disappointed, but he understood."

"That sounds like Hawk."

"He also said if I hurt you, he'd make sure no one ever found my body."

Bella laughed. "Also sounds like Hawk."

---

That evening, back at the cabin, she called Charlotte.

Addie answered on the first ring. "Well?"

"He asked."

"AND?"

"I said yes."

The scream on the other end was so loud Bella had to hold the phone away from her ear. She could hear Hawk in the background, laughing, already pouring drinks.

"We're celebrating tonight," Addie announced. "And you're flying back next weekend so we can celebrate properly. The whole family. Everyone."

"I wouldn't miss it."

"I love you, Bella."

"I love you too, Mom."

The word slipped out—natural, unforced. The first time she'd said it without thinking.

Addie went quiet. Then, softly: "Mom. You called me Mom."

"You are my mom. In every way that matters."

"Get off this phone before you make me cry harder than I already am." Addie's voice was thick. "Go be with your fiancé. We'll see you next weekend."
