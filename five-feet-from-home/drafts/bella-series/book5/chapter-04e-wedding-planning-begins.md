# Chapter 4e: Wedding Planning Begins

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Two: The Proposal

---

Elena took charge.

"This is what I do," she reminded Bella, when Bella tried to protest. "Maison Aurelia has planned hundreds of events. Let me do this for you."

"But you're family. I don't want you to work—"

"This IS work I want to do." Elena's eyes sparkled. "Besides, you'll be working alongside me. I'm not excluding you from your own wedding. I'm just... providing structure."

Structure, it turned out, meant spreadsheets, timelines, vendor lists, and more decisions than Bella had ever imagined.

"How many decisions can there possibly be about flowers?"

"More than you think." Elena pointed to her screen. "Color palette. Arrangement style. Which blooms for bouquets versus table settings. Whether to incorporate local Colorado wildflowers—which I recommend, given the ranch setting."

"Can't we just say 'pretty flowers' and call it done?"

"We could. But you'd regret it." Elena smiled. "Trust me. The details matter."

---

The venue was settled: Mandy's ranch.

Specifically, the barn that Matt and a crew of veterans were already renovating. Mandy had offered it immediately, and Matt had volunteered to lead the construction.

"You're building the place where we're getting married?" Bella asked, watching them frame out new windows.

"Seems right." Matt wiped sweat from his forehead. "I want to put my hands on this. Make it mean something."

"It already means something."

"Then it'll mean more."

---

The guest list was more complicated.

"You have three families," Kendra pointed out. "Biological parents. Chosen family. And now Matt's people. That's a lot of dynamics to balance."

"I know."

"Plus the Wives Club. Plus BSS colleagues. Plus college friends." Kendra whistled. "How big were you thinking?"

"I don't know. Intimate? Fifty people?"

Kendra laughed. "Honey, your immediate family is more than fifty people."

---

The dress shopping was scheduled for a weekend in New York.

Addie flew up. Elena came from Charlotte. Tessa insisted on participating. Even Harper video-called from Miami to offer opinions.

"This is a lot," Bella said, standing in the bridal salon, surrounded by white fabric and expectations.

"This is love," Addie corrected. "Let us love you."

---

They tried on seventeen dresses.

Some too simple. Some too elaborate. Some that made Bella feel like she was playing dress-up rather than preparing for her wedding.

Then the consultant brought out number eighteen.

It was ivory—not stark white, but warm, like cream or candlelight. A-line silhouette. Subtle lace overlay on the bodice. Sleeves that fell just off the shoulder. A train that was present but not overwhelming.

Bella put it on.

The room went silent.

"That's the one," Addie whispered.

Bella looked in the mirror. The woman looking back was someone she almost didn't recognize—confident, beautiful, radiant in a way that had nothing to do with the dress and everything to do with who she'd become.

"Yeah," she said. "That's the one."
