# Chapter 12: The Announcement

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Six: Growth

---

She'd suspected for weeks.

Little things: fatigue that coffee couldn't fix. Food aversions she'd never had before. A tenderness in her body that felt unfamiliar.

But suspecting and knowing were different things.

---

The test sat on the bathroom counter.

Two lines. Undeniable.

Bella stared at it for a full minute, processing. Then she walked out to the porch where Matt was having his morning coffee.

"We need to talk."

His face shifted—the particular tension of a husband hearing those words from his wife.

"Everything okay?"

She handed him the test.

Matt looked at it. Looked at her. Back at it.

"Is this... are you..."

"I'm pregnant."

---

The joy came first.

Matt swept her into his arms, spinning her around the porch, laughing with his whole body. "We're having a baby. We're having a baby. Bella, we're having a BABY."

"Put me down. I might throw up."

He set her down immediately, face shifting to concern.

"Are you okay? Do you need water? Should I call someone?"

"I'm fine. Just—morning sickness. Apparently that's real."

"Right. Right." He was pacing now. "We need a doctor. A good one. When do you start seeing doctors? Is it too early?"

"Matt."

"And the house—we need to childproof. Do you childproof before or after? What about the horses? Are horses safe around pregnant women?"

"Matt."

He stopped. Looked at her.

"Breathe," she said. "We have nine months. We'll figure it out."

"Nine months." He exhaled. "That seems simultaneously forever and not nearly enough time."

"Welcome to parenthood, I guess."

---

They told Matt's mother first.

She cried for twenty minutes. Literally. Bella counted.

"A grandchild. Finally. I'd given up hope." She hugged them both, repeatedly. "You'll be such good parents. I just know it."

"We're terrified," Matt admitted.

"Of course you are. Every good parent is terrified." She smiled through tears. "That's how you know you'll do it right."

---

The Charlotte call was harder.

Not because anyone would be upset—the opposite. Because the intensity of Charlotte joy was a force of nature.

"Are you sitting down?" Bella asked, when Addie answered.

"Why would I need to—" Addie paused. "Oh my God."

"I haven't said anything yet."

"Your voice. Your face. You're pregnant."

"I'm pregnant."

The scream that came through the phone could have shattered glass. Then Addie was crying, and then there was shuffling as she apparently ran through the house shouting for Hawk.

"WHAT?" Hawk's voice, distant but urgent. "What's happening?"

"Bella's PREGNANT!"

More shuffling. Then Hawk's face appeared on screen, looking like someone had hit him with a beautiful brick.

"Daughter?"

"It's true, Dad. We just found out."

He was quiet for a long moment. Then, gruffly: "I'm going to be a grandfather."

"You're already a grandfather. Grace calls you Grandpa."

"A grandfather of my daughter's child." He shook his head. "I never... I didn't think I'd get this."

"Get what?"

"This. A legacy. A bloodline—not by blood, but by choice." His voice cracked. "A grandchild I can teach the way I taught you."

---

Grace was the most excited.

"I'M GOING TO BE AN AUNT! A REAL ONE! Can I hold the baby? Can I teach it things? Can I—"

"Grace." Elena's voice, calming. "The baby won't be here for months."

"But when it IS here, can I—"

"Yes. You can do all of those things."

"BEST DAY EVER!"

---

That night, Bella and Matt sat by their fire.

"Scared?" he asked.

"Terrified. Excited. Both."

"Same." He took her hand. "We're really doing this."

"We're really doing this."

A pause.

"Do you think we're ready?"

Bella considered.

"I think ready is a myth. I think you become ready by doing. By showing up every day and trying." She looked at him. "We're good at that."

"We are."

"So we'll figure it out. Like everything else."

"Together?"

"Always together."
