# Chapter 5b: The Morning Of

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Three: The Wedding

---

She woke at dawn.

The guest cabin at Mandy's ranch was quiet, but Bella's mind was racing. Today. It was today. In twelve hours, she would be married.

She lay in bed, watching the light change, trying to memorize this moment—the last morning of her old life.

---

Addie arrived first.

"You're awake." She carried a tray of coffee and pastries. "Couldn't sleep?"

"Too excited. Too nervous. Too... everything."

Addie set down the tray and sat on the edge of the bed. "That's exactly how you should feel."

"Were you nervous when you married Hawk?"

"Terrified." Addie smiled. "Not because I doubted him. Because I understood what I was saying yes to. A whole life. Every day, forever. That's a big thing to wrap your mind around."

"How did you get through it?"

"I focused on him. On the moment we'd say our vows." Addie took her hand. "Everything else—the dress, the guests, the details—it all fades. What stays is looking into his eyes and meaning every word."

---

The women arrived in waves.

Tessa, loud and excited, carrying bags of makeup and hair tools. Elena, calm and organized, with a clipboard of the day's schedule. Harper, having flown in from Miami the night before, already coordinating with the photographer. Kendra, practical as always, making sure everyone had eaten.

And Mandy.

She stood in the doorway, watching the chaos with those steel-green eyes.

"Welcome to your wedding day," she said quietly.

"Thank you for making this possible."

"Thank you for letting me be part of it." Mandy crossed the room, hugged her briefly. "I'm going to check on the barn. Matt's been there since 5 AM, making sure everything's perfect."

"He's not supposed to see me."

"He knows. He's staying in the other wing." Mandy smiled. "But he wanted to be there. To feel like he's part of making this happen."

---

The morning was a blur of preparation.

Hair first—pinned up in an elegant updo, with soft tendrils framing her face. Then makeup—natural, glowing, nothing too dramatic. Just enhanced versions of her own features.

The dress came last.

Addie and Tessa helped her into it—the ivory gown they'd chosen in New York, with its lace overlay and off-the-shoulder sleeves. The pendant from the Wives Club hung at her throat, as always. Simple diamond studs in her ears—a gift from Matt.

"Something old," Tessa announced, handing her a handkerchief. "My grandmother's. She gave it to my mom for her wedding, my mom gave it to me, and now I'm lending it to you."

"Something new is the dress," Elena added. "Something borrowed is the pendant—technically it belongs to the Club."

"And something blue..." Addie produced a small box. Inside was a delicate blue sapphire bracelet. "This was Hawk's mother's. He wanted you to have it."

Bella's eyes filled with tears.

"Don't cry yet," Tessa warned. "The mascara isn't waterproof."

---

At 2:30 PM, there was a knock at the door.

Grace's voice: "Are you ready? They're waiting!"

Bella looked at herself in the mirror one final time.

The woman looking back was radiant. Confident. Complete.

"Yes," she called back. "I'm ready."
