# Chapter 4d: Telling Everyone

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part Two: The Proposal

---

The Charlotte celebration was everything Bella expected.

The whole family gathered at the compound—Hawk and Addie, Jasper and Elena, Kendra and Chris, Harper via video call from Miami. Grace was there too, practically vibrating with excitement.

"You're getting MARRIED!" she announced, as if Bella might have forgotten. "That means I get to be in the wedding, right?"

"Of course you do."

"Can I throw the flowers?"

"Flower girl is definitely on the table."

Grace beamed like she'd won the lottery.

---

Hawk pulled her aside during the celebration.

They stood on the back porch, looking out over the property she'd called home for years. The fire pit was visible in the distance, cold and waiting.

"I'm happy for you, daughter."

"I know you are."

"But I'm also..." He paused, searching for words. "I'm going to miss you. Having you here. Knowing you're just down the hall."

"I'm not disappearing, Dad. I'll be back constantly. For work, for family, for everything important."

"I know. It's just..." He smiled, a little sadly. "You were the first one I chose. The first one I called daughter and meant it. Watching you leave is harder than I expected."

Bella hugged him—fierce and long.

"You're not losing me. You're gaining a son-in-law."

"Matt's a good man."

"He is."

"He'll take care of you."

"He'll try. But I can take care of myself." She pulled back, meeting his eyes. "You taught me that."

---

Mandy's reaction was the one Bella was most uncertain about.

She called from her cabin at the ranch, ring visible on her finger. Mandy answered with those steel-green eyes, already knowing.

"He asked."

"I can see that." Mandy's expression was unreadable for a moment. Then, slowly, she smiled. "I'm happy for you."

"You are?"

"I positioned him on the board, remember? This is exactly what I wanted." Mandy's voice softened. "I wanted you to have the life you deserved. With someone who could give you what I couldn't."

"You're really okay?"

"I'm really okay." Mandy leaned forward. "Bella, you're my family. That hasn't changed. Whatever you need for this wedding—my ranch, my help, anything—it's yours."

"I was actually hoping... the wedding could be there. At the ranch."

Mandy's eyes widened. "You want to get married here?"

"It's where I learned to heal. Where I learned to trust love again." Bella smiled. "It seems right."

"Then it's yours." Mandy's voice was thick with emotion. "We'll make it beautiful."

---

Tessa flew in from New York.

They met at a wine bar in Charlotte—the kind of trendy place Tessa loved and Bella tolerated. The moment Tessa saw the ring, she screamed loud enough to turn every head in the restaurant.

"BELLA ROSSI IS GETTING MARRIED."

"Could you announce it a little louder? I don't think they heard you in Canada."

"Oh, shut up and let me see it." Tessa grabbed her hand, examining the ring from every angle. "Simple. Elegant. Not too flashy. It's perfect for you."

"Matt chose it himself."

"The cowboy has taste. I'm impressed." Tessa looked up. "Are you happy?"

"So happy it scares me sometimes."

"That's exactly how it should be." Tessa pulled her into a hug. "You deserve this, Bella. After everything—the doubt, the fear, the years of not believing you were worth it—you deserve to be loved."

"You always believed in me."

"Someone had to." Tessa grinned. "Now tell me everything. Venue. Dress. Bridesmaids. I want all the details."

---

Vee sent a card.

Elegant. Handwritten. Classic Vee.

*Bella—*

*I heard the news. I'm so happy for you.*

*I remember when we talked about the Wives Club, back in college. I thought I knew what the future held. I was wrong—about everything.*

*You found something real. Something better than what I was chasing. Hold onto it.*

*With love,*
*Vee*

*P.S. I expect an invitation.*
