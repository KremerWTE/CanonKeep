# Chapter 2c: Meeting the Family

## Book 5: "Black Moves Second"
### Part One: Courtship

---

Matt came to Charlotte.

It was his first time east of the Mississippi in years. He looked out of place in a way that made Bella smile—the cowboy hat, the boots, the careful way he observed everything as if cataloguing a new territory.

"Different," he said, as they drove from the airport.

"Different good or different bad?"

"Just different." He watched the city rise around them. "More buildings. More people. More... intensity."

"Wait until you meet the family."

---

The compound seemed to intimidate him at first.

The security. The scale. The obvious wealth and power that radiated from every carefully maintained corner. Matt stood in the driveway for a long moment, taking it in.

"This is your world," he said quietly.

"Part of it." Bella took his hand. "You're my world too. Both can be true."

"I'm a rancher, Bella. I build vacation cabins and train horses. This is..." He gestured at the estate. "This is something else entirely."

"It's family. That's all. Unusual family, but family." She squeezed his hand. "They're going to love you. I promise."

---

Hawk was waiting at the door.

The two men sized each other up—the retired Tier One operator and the quiet rancher. Something passed between them, some silent assessment that Bella couldn't quite read.

Then Hawk extended his hand.

"Matt. Welcome."

"Sir." Matt's handshake was firm. "Thank you for having me."

"Don't call me sir. I work for a living." Hawk's grin broke the tension. "Come inside. Addie's been cooking all day."

---

Dinner was smaller than a full family gathering—just Hawk, Addie, Kendra, and Elena. Enough to meet him without overwhelming.

Matt was quiet at first, listening more than talking. But gradually, he relaxed. He asked Elena about her business. He discussed CrossFit techniques with Kendra. He answered Addie's questions about Colorado with thoughtful precision.

"You're observing him," Bella murmured to Hawk, between courses.

"Of course I am."

"And?"

"And I like what I see." Hawk's voice was low. "He's solid. Grounded. Not threatened by any of this." He glanced at Matt, who was laughing at something Elena had said. "He'll do."

"High praise."

"It's accurate." Hawk met her eyes. "He loves you, daughter. Really loves you, not the version of you that's useful or impressive. That's rare. That's worth keeping."

---

After dinner, Hawk took Matt to the fire pit.

Bella wasn't invited. She watched from the window as the two men sat in the same chairs she and Hawk had occupied so many times. Watched them talk, gesture, fall into silence.

"They'll be fine," Addie said, appearing beside her.

"What if Hawk scares him off?"

"If Hawk could scare him off, he's not the right one." Addie smiled. "And I don't think he scares easily."

An hour later, Matt returned to the main house. His expression was thoughtful but not worried.

"Everything okay?" Bella asked.

"More than okay." He pulled her into a hug. "I understand now. Why you love him. Why this place matters so much."

"What did he say?"

"He told me about the fire pit rules. About the two questions." Matt smiled. "He asked me how I was doing, and how he could support me. I've never had anyone ask me that before. Not like that."

"That's Hawk."

"That's family." Matt kissed her forehead. "I want to be part of this, Bella. If you'll have me."
