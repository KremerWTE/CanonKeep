# Chapter 24: The Pendant Passed

## Book 7: "A Hymn to Her"
### Part Seven: The Settling

---

Rose was thirty-five when Bella made the decision.

Thirty-five—the age Bella had been when she'd finally started to believe she was worthy. A full circle of sorts.

"I have something for you," Bella said, during a private visit.

She reached behind her neck and unclasped the pendant. The Wives Club symbol that had hung at her throat for over thirty years.

"Mom, no—"

"Yes." Bella pressed it into Rose's hand. "It's yours now."

---

The moment was heavier than jewelry.

The pendant had been given to Bella when she was lost. When she needed something tangible to remind her she belonged. When external validation was the only thing keeping her afloat.

"I don't need it anymore," Bella explained. "I know my worth from the inside now. But you might need it. Not because you're lost—because everyone needs reminders sometimes."

Rose looked at the pendant, tears forming.

"You wore this every day of my life."

"I wore it until I didn't need it. Now it can do the same for you."

---

**[THEME: PASSING SYMBOLS FORWARD]**

Rose put it on.

The chain was the right length, the symbol settling at her collarbone exactly as it had on Bella for decades.

"It fits," Rose said.

"It was always going to."

"What if I don't deserve it?"

Bella took her daughter's face in her hands.

"That's the voice that kept me hustling for forty years. That's the lie that almost destroyed me." She spoke firmly. "You deserve it. Not because of what you've achieved. Because you exist. Because you're worthy by nature, not by earning."

"That's what James's book says."

"He learned it from watching me learn it. Now I'm teaching you directly."

---

The Wives Club had evolved over the decades.

Less secretive. More intentional. A network of women supporting women, without the hierarchies of old.

Rose had been part of it for years—her own membership, her own connections. But wearing the pendant was different. It was inheritance.

"What does it mean to you?" Rose asked. "The pendant?"

"It meant belonging when I didn't belong anywhere. It meant being chosen when I couldn't choose myself." Bella paused. "Now it means memory. Of who I was. Of who helped me become who I am."

"And for me?"

"For you, it can mean whatever you need. That's how symbols work—they grow with us."

---

That night, Bella looked in the mirror.

For thirty-two years, the pendant had been there. A constant presence at her throat. A talisman against the worthiness wound.

Now there was just skin. Just her.

"You're still you," Matt said, coming up behind her.

"I know. It's just strange." She touched her bare collarbone. "Like a part of me is missing."

"A part of you is carried by Rose now. That's not missing—that's continuing."

She smiled.

"When did you get so wise?"

"Married you. Wisdom by osmosis."
