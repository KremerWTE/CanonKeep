# Chapter 15: Twenty-Five Years

## Book 7: "A Hymn to Her"
### Part Five: The Fullness

---

Twenty-five years of marriage.

Silver anniversary. A quarter century of waking up next to the same person, choosing each other again and again through every season.

"I barely remember who I was before you," Bella said, the morning of their anniversary.

"You were still you. Just... unfinished."

"We're all unfinished."

"That's what keeps it interesting."

---

The celebration was at the lake house.

The same place where Matt had proposed, a lifetime ago. The same quiet water, the same mountain views, the same sense of being apart from the world.

But they were different now. Gray in their hair. Lines on their faces. Decades of experience behind them.

"You're still beautiful," Matt said.

"You need glasses."

"I have glasses. You're still beautiful."

---

**[REFLECTION - TWENTY-FIVE YEARS]**

The weekend was for savoring.

No agenda. No schedules. Just them, remembering and dreaming.

"What's your favorite memory?" Bella asked—the same question from ten years ago, from twenty.

"Still all of them. But if I had to choose..." Matt thought. "The moment Rose was born. Watching you become a mother. Seeing this whole new capacity for love emerge."

"And the hardest time?"

"The years before you learned to rest. Watching you run yourself into the ground. Wanting to help but not knowing how."

"You helped just by being patient."

"Patience isn't passive, you know." He looked at her. "Watching someone you love struggle, and staying anyway—that's its own kind of work."

---

She told him what she'd never fully articulated.

"I was afraid you'd leave. When I was at my worst—the hustle, the absence, the way I couldn't stop working. I was sure you'd realize I wasn't worth the trouble."

"That was never on the table."

"I know that now. I didn't then."

"What changed?"

"Everything. Therapy. Time. Watching myself become someone who could actually receive love." She took his hand. "And you. Your consistency. The way you showed up, day after day, even when I couldn't show up for myself."

---

They talked about what came next.

Rose married, possibly thinking about kids. James's practice flourishing. The businesses running smoothly without their constant involvement.

"What do you want for the next twenty-five years?" Bella asked.

"More of this. Less stress. Time to actually enjoy what we've built."

"That sounds nice."

"It's what you deserve. What we both deserve." Matt smiled. "We spent decades building. Now we get to live in it."

---

That night, fire pit by the lake.

The ritual they'd maintained for twenty-five years. The questions that never got old.

"How are you doing?" Matt asked.

"I'm happy. Actually, genuinely happy. Not the frantic happiness of achievement—the quiet kind that comes from knowing you're where you belong."

"And how can I support you?"

Bella thought about it.

"Keep being you. Keep choosing me. Keep reminding me that the hustle isn't the point—connection is."

"I can do that."

"I know." She leaned into him. "That's why I married you."
