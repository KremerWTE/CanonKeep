# Chapter 23: Matt's Health Scare

## Book 7: "A Hymn to Her"
### Part Seven: The Settling

---

The call came at 2 PM on a Thursday.

Matt had collapsed at the ranch. One of the hands found him. The ambulance was already on its way.

Bella drove to the hospital on autopilot. The old crisis-management instincts kicked in—gathering information, staying calm, preparing for the worst while hoping for the best.

But inside, she was terrified.

---

The diagnosis was serious but treatable.

Heart issues—decades of physical labor catching up with him. Surgery required. Recovery expected, but uncertain.

"He's strong," the surgeon said. "That's in his favor."

"How strong does he need to be?"

"As strong as his reasons for fighting."

---

**[THEME: FACING LOSS]**

The surgery was eight hours.

Bella sat in the waiting room, surrounded by family who'd rushed in—Rose, James, Grace, the extended clan. All of them holding space, holding breath, holding hope.

For the first time in years, she prayed.

Not formally—she wasn't religious in the traditional sense. But something like prayer. A reaching toward whatever forces might be listening.

*Please. Not yet. We're not done. I need more time.*

---

He survived.

The surgeon emerged, tired but smiling. "He's strong. He fought."

Bella collapsed into Rose's arms, sobbing with relief.

"He's okay," Rose kept saying. "He's going to be okay."

"I was so scared."

"I know, Mom. We all were."

---

The recovery was slow.

Weeks in the hospital, then months at home. Matt, who'd never been still in his life, learning to rest. Bella, who'd just learned to rest herself, becoming his anchor.

"You're good at this," he said, one afternoon. "The caretaking thing."

"I had practice. You took care of me for years."

"That was different."

"Was it?" She sat beside him. "Love is love. Care is care. The details change, but the essence stays the same."

---

The scare changed things.

Priorities sharpened. Time felt more precious. The quiet years became intentionally quiet—not just default, but chosen.

"I don't want to waste any more days," Bella said.

"What does not wasting look like?"

"Being present. Saying what matters. Not letting business or productivity or achievement get in the way of connection."

"You've been learning that for years."

"This is the advanced course."

---

Matt recovered fully.

Within a year, he was back to limited activity at the ranch. Not the grueling physical work of before, but enough to feel useful. Enough to matter.

"I have new appreciation for being alive," he told Bella.

"So do I."

"For watching you become this."

"What's 'this'?"

"At peace. Content. The woman you always could have been, if the world had let you."

"The world didn't let me. I had to fight for it."

"Maybe that made it mean more."
