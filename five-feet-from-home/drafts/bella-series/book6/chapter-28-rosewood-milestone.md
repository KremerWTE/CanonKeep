# Chapter 28: Rosewood Milestone

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part Ten: A Decade

---

Ten years of Rosewood Venues.

From a broken-down barn to a five-property empire. From a solo operation to a team of sixty. From a dream to an institution.

"We should celebrate," Sara said, during a planning call. "This is significant."

"It feels strange celebrating myself."

"You're not celebrating yourself. You're celebrating what we've all built together."

---

The anniversary gala was held at the original venue.

The barn that had started it all, now polished and perfected, filled with the people who'd made it possible. Staff from all five properties. Key vendors. Long-time clients. Family and friends.

"Look at this," Elena said, surveying the crowd. "Look at what you've created."

"What we've created. You were there from the beginning."

"I provided consulting. You provided vision." Elena smiled. "Don't deflect the credit, Bella. You've earned it."

---

The speeches were short but meaningful.

Sara spoke about the culture Bella had built—the focus on quality over quantity, the genuine care for every couple.

Morgan, now managing the Charleston property, talked about what it meant to grow within an organization that valued people over profit.

A couple from the very first wedding returned to share how that day had set the tone for their entire marriage.

"You gave us something magic," the bride said. "Not just an event—a foundation."

---

Bella's speech was brief.

"Ten years ago, I bought a broken-down barn because I saw potential. Not just in the building—in myself. I'd spent years making other people's visions real. This was my chance to have a vision of my own."

She looked around the room.

"But I learned something along the way. A vision is nothing without the people who share it. Every person in this room has shaped what Rosewood became. The team members who work late. The vendors who exceed expectations. The couples who trust us with their most important days."

Her voice caught.

"Thank you. For believing in this dream. For making it more than I could have made alone. For teaching me that success isn't solo—it's shared."

---

After the speeches, after the toasts, after the dancing:

Bella stood alone in the barn, lights dimmed, guests departed. Matt found her there, as he always did.

"What are you thinking?"

"About who I was when I started this. And who I am now."

"Are they different people?"

"Same person. Just... more. More confident. More grateful. More at peace with taking up space in the world."

"That sounds like growth."

"That sounds like ten years of work."

---

She locked up the barn herself.

A ritual, unnecessary—they had staff for this. But she wanted to do it. To close one chapter before opening the next.

"Here's to the next ten," she whispered to the empty room.

The barn didn't respond. But she felt the answer anyway.
