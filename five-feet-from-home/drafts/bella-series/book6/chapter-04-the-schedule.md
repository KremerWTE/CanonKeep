# Chapter 4: The Schedule

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part One: The Vision

---

Bella's days had a rhythm.

4:30 AM: Wake up. No alarm needed anymore—her body had adjusted.

5:00 AM: Workout, if time allowed. Usually running, sometimes the home gym Matt had set up.

6:00 AM: First work block. BSS communications, crisis monitoring, anything urgent from overnight.

8:00 AM: Maison Aurelia hours. COO duties, marketing coordination, client management.

12:00 PM: Rosewood Farm. Venue operations, vendor relationships, bookings.

3:00 PM: POH work, when scheduled. Ceremonial preparations, donor communications.

6:00 PM: Family time. Calls to Charlotte. Dinner with Matt. Whatever breathing room she could find.

9:00 PM: Second work block. Catch-up, planning, the overflow that didn't fit elsewhere.

1:30 AM: Sleep. If she was lucky.

---

"This isn't sustainable," Addie said, reviewing her schedule during a visit.

"It's temporary."

"You've been saying that for two years."

Bella didn't have a good answer for that.

---

**[REFLECTION - THE DRIVE]**

She knew, intellectually, that she was running on fumes. That no one could maintain this pace indefinitely. That something would eventually give.

But stopping felt impossible. Every role fed the others. BSS gave her crisis management skills that made Rosewood events flawless. Maison Aurelia provided design resources. POH connected her to clientele. The network was the point.

"You're building an empire," Hawk observed, during one of their fire pit sessions. "That requires foundation work. Foundation work is exhausting."

"Is this how you felt? Building BSS?"

"Worse. I didn't have anyone to come home to." His eyes were warm. "You have Matt. You have us. Don't forget to let us help."

---

Matt was endlessly patient.

He understood the demands. He supported the vision. He made sure she ate, made sure she occasionally rested, made sure she didn't completely disappear into work.

"I married a force of nature," he said, one night when she came to bed at 2 AM. "I knew what I was getting into."

"Do you regret it?"

"Not for a second." He pulled her close. "Just promise me you'll slow down eventually. When the foundation is built."

"I promise."

"Soon?"

"Soonish."

---

The breaking point came quietly.

Not a crisis—just a moment. Bella was on a call, simultaneously reviewing contracts and answering emails, when she realized she'd typed the same sentence three times without registering what it said.

She stopped. Sat back. Looked at her desk, covered in the evidence of a life lived at full throttle.

"I need a break," she said to no one.

The words were revolutionary.

---

Matt found her on the porch that evening, doing nothing. Actually nothing—not reading, not planning, not working.

"Are you okay?"

"I think I need to recalibrate."

"Tell me what you need."

"I need to figure out what I can let go of." She looked at him. "I can't keep doing everything at this pace. Something has to shift."

"Whatever you decide, I'm with you."

"I know." She took his hand. "That's the only reason I can consider it."
