# Chapter 13: The IVF Process

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part Five: The Decision

---

IVF was nothing like she'd expected.

Not the procedure itself—she'd researched that extensively. But the reality of living through it. The hormones. The appointments. The constant monitoring of a body that suddenly felt foreign.

"How are you feeling?" the nurse asked, during her third week of injections.

"Like a science experiment that's gone off the rails."

The nurse smiled sympathetically. "That's normal."

---

The process consumed everything.

Work continued—Rosewood, BSS, POH—but at reduced capacity. For the first time in her professional life, Bella delegated not from strategy but from necessity.

"Take whatever time you need," Elena said. "The business will survive."

"What if I can't come back the same?"

"Then you'll come back different. That's not a failure. That's growth."

---

The egg retrieval was successful—eleven viable embryos.

"Eleven?" Bella repeated, stunned. "That's a lot."

"It's a good number. Gives us options." The doctor smiled. "Now we wait."

The waiting was the hardest part.

Three days to see which embryos developed. Another two for genetic testing. Then the transfer—a single embryo, carefully selected, placed with precision and hope.

And then: two weeks of limbo. The longest two weeks of her life.

---

Matt was present for all of it.

Every injection, he helped administer. Every appointment, he attended. Every breakdown—and there were many—he held her through.

"You're doing this too," she said once. "I keep forgetting. This is happening to both of us."

"It's different for me. The physical part. But the emotional part—that's shared." He kissed her forehead. "We're in this together. Every step."

"What if it doesn't work?"

"Then we try again. Or we don't. Either way, we're still us."

---

The morning of the blood test, Bella couldn't eat.

She'd taken home tests—too early, she knew—and convinced herself she could interpret shadows that weren't there. The clinical result was the only thing that would tell the truth.

The call came at 2 PM.

"Mrs. Holloway? I have your results."

Bella gripped Matt's hand so hard she heard something pop.

"Your HCG levels are positive. You're pregnant."

---

The emotion that followed was complicated.

Joy—overwhelming, consuming joy. But also fear. They'd been here before, in a sense—hoped and been disappointed. What if this was the beginning of another ending?

"One step at a time," Matt said, reading her thoughts. "Today, we're pregnant. Tomorrow we'll face tomorrow."

"That's very zen."

"I've been practicing."

She laughed—the first real laugh in weeks.

"I love you."

"I love you more."

"That's not possible."

"Then we're equal. That's the best kind of love."

---

The waiting continued. But now it was waiting for something, not just waiting against something.

And that made all the difference.
