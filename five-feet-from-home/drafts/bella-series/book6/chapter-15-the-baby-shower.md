# Chapter 15: The Baby Shower

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part Six: The Transition

---

Elena insisted on planning it.

"You've done this for everyone else," she argued. "Helped with showers, coordinated events, made everyone feel special. Now it's your turn."

"I don't need—"

"You don't get to decide what you need right now. That's not how being pregnant works."

Bella surrendered.

---

The shower was held at Rosewood Farm.

Fitting, in a way—celebrating new life in the space she'd built for celebrating life's moments. The venue had been transformed: soft colors, elegant flowers, touches that were personal without being saccharine.

"How does it feel?" Matt asked, watching the guests arrive.

"Strange. Being on this side of it."

"Strange good?"

"Strange... humbling." She watched women she loved streaming through the doors. "Everyone I care about is here to celebrate us. That's a lot."

"You deserve a lot."

---

**[OUTFIT DETAILS - BABY SHOWER]**

At seven months pregnant, Bella's wardrobe had evolved significantly.

Today: a flowing dress in soft sage green that accommodated her belly while still feeling elegant. Low heels—the highest she could manage anymore. Hair down, because arms tired quickly these days.

The pendant, always. Now it rested atop the curve of her belly, a constant companion to both of them.

"You look beautiful," Addie said, arriving with a gift bag that was clearly too large for a single person to carry alone.

"I look round."

"Beautifully round."

---

The guests were a cross-section of her life.

Charlotte family: Addie, Elena, Kendra, Harper. Wives Club members who'd become genuine friends. Rosewood staff who'd watched her build an empire while building a family. Matt's mother and sisters. Even Tessa, flown in from New York for the weekend.

"I can't believe it," Tessa said, hugging her carefully. "Bella Rossi—Holloway—is having a baby."

"Join the club."

"I've been in the club. I've been waiting for you to catch up." Tessa grinned. "This kid is going to be something. With your brains and Matt's patience? Unstoppable."

"What about Matt's brains?"

"I assume those too. But your brains are the scary ones."

---

The games were mercifully restrained.

No guessing belly sizes. No baby food taste tests. Just good conversation, elegant food, and the warmth of being surrounded by people who loved you.

Grace had appointed herself chief gift coordinator.

"This one next!" she announced, handing Bella a large box. "It's from me and Mom. I picked it out myself."

Inside: a tiny onesie that read "Future Boss."

"I saw it and I HAD to get it," Grace explained. "Because your baby is going to be a boss. Like you."

"The best gift," Bella said, and meant it.

---

Later, a quiet moment.

Bella sat in the garden, resting, watching the party continue without her. The baby was active today—kicking and shifting, making its presence known.

Mandy found her there.

"Sneaking away?"

"Resting. The baby takes a lot out of me these days."

"I remember." Mandy sat beside her. "Different circumstances, obviously. But I remember the exhaustion. The way your body becomes someone else's."

"You've never talked about that."

"I don't talk about a lot of things." Mandy's eyes were distant. "I lost a pregnancy, years ago. Before the ranch. Before everything."

Bella's heart clenched. "I'm sorry."

"It was a long time ago. But I remember." She looked at Bella's belly. "I remember hoping. And then not having."

"Mandy—"

"I'm not telling you to make you sad. I'm telling you because I want you to understand—what you have is precious. Don't take a single moment for granted."

"I won't."

"Good." Mandy smiled, soft and rare. "Now go back to your party. Let people love you. That's what today is for."
