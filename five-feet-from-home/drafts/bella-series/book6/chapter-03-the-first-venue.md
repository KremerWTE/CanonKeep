# Chapter 3: The First Venue

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part One: The Vision

---

The property was a disaster.

An old barn on twenty acres, halfway between Charlotte and Raleigh. The roof leaked. The foundation was questionable. The previous owners had let it fall into disrepair years ago.

Bella saw potential.

"You want to buy this?" Matt asked, surveying the wreckage.

"I want to transform this."

---

Elena walked the property with them, her designer's eye cataloging every flaw and possibility.

"The bones are good," she said finally. "The space is workable. But you're looking at a significant investment before it's functional."

"How significant?"

"Low six figures. Minimum."

Bella didn't flinch. She'd been saving. She'd been planning. And she had partners who believed in her vision.

"I can make that work."

---

The financing came together through a combination of personal savings, a small loan from Hawk (insisting it was an investment, not a gift), and a partnership with Elena's Maison Aurelia.

"We'll use it for some of our events," Elena explained. "That gives you guaranteed revenue while you build your client base."

"What do you get out of it?"

"A venue I trust. And a partner I trust running it." Elena smiled. "Sometimes business is just finding people you can rely on."

---

**[SETTING DETAILS - THE BARN PROPERTY]**

The renovation took six months.

Bella was on-site every spare moment—which, given her schedule, meant dawn hours and late nights. She learned to read blueprints, negotiate with contractors, make decisions that balanced aesthetics with budget.

Matt helped when he could, his construction experience invaluable. The veteran network provided labor at fair rates.

The barn emerged from its cocoon transformed: rustic but elegant, with exposed beams and modern infrastructure. A bridal suite in the loft. A commercial kitchen in the back addition. Gardens that would bloom in spring.

They named it Rosewood Farm.

"Rose for your middle name," Matt said. "And wood for... the barn, I guess."

"Very poetic."

"I try."

---

The first event was a wedding.

Not just any wedding—a high-profile Charlotte family, connected to the POH network, willing to take a chance on a new venue.

Bella worked twenty-hour days in the week leading up to it. Coordinated vendors, managed last-minute crises, ensured every detail was perfect.

The wedding was flawless.

The bride cried. The groom's mother sent a handwritten thank-you note. The photographer's portfolio shots went viral in wedding planning circles.

"You did it," Matt said, afterward, finding her sitting alone in the cleaned-up barn.

"We did it." Bella looked around at the space she'd built. "This is real now. Not just an idea. A real thing in the real world."

"How does that feel?"

"Terrifying." She smiled. "And exactly right."
