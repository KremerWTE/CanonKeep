# Chapter 17: The Arrival

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part Six: The Transition

---

She arrived on a Wednesday.

3:47 AM. After eighteen hours of labor. 7 pounds, 3 ounces. A shock of dark hair that Bella would swear was the exact shade of Matt's.

They named her Rose.

Rose after the middle name, the garden, the beauty that persists despite thorns. Rose for resilience. Rose for grace.

"Rose Holloway," Matt whispered, holding her for the first time. "Welcome to the world."

---

The labor had been harder than expected.

Complications—nothing life-threatening, but enough to extend the process, to require interventions they hadn't planned for. Bella had pushed through on sheer will, refusing the cesarean until the very last option.

"You're stubborn," the doctor said afterward. "That's not always a bad thing."

"That's how I get through everything."

"Well, it worked. She's here. She's healthy. That's what matters."

---

The first hours were a blur.

Skin-to-skin contact. The first latch, awkward and uncertain. Matt crying, then laughing, then crying again. Nurses moving efficiently around them, checking vitals, offering guidance.

And Rose—tiny, perfect, already making her presence known.

"She's here," Bella said, over and over. "She's actually here."

"She's here," Matt confirmed. "And she's ours."

---

The calls went out at dawn.

Hawk and Addie first—they'd been waiting by the phone all night.

"A girl," Bella said. "Rose. She's perfect."

Silence on the other end. Then Hawk's voice, cracking: "Show me."

She turned the camera. Rose was sleeping in the hospital bassinet, one tiny fist raised like she was already ready to fight.

"She looks like you," Hawk said. "The jaw. The set of her mouth."

"She looks like Matt."

"She looks like both of you." His voice steadied. "She looks like exactly who she's supposed to be."

---

The visitors came in waves.

Matt's mother first—she'd been local, able to arrive within hours. Then Elena and Jasper, with a subdued Grace who stared at the baby with awe.

"She's so small," Grace whispered.

"You were that small once."

"No way. I was definitely bigger."

"You were actually smaller."

Grace considered this impossible information, then reached out a careful finger to touch Rose's hand. The baby grabbed on.

"She likes me!" Grace announced. "We're going to be best friends."

---

Hawk and Addie arrived that evening.

They'd taken the first flight available. Hawk was still in his travel clothes, eyes rimmed red.

He stood in the doorway, looking at Bella holding Rose, and didn't move for a long moment.

"Dad?"

"I'm just... taking it in." He crossed to her, slowly. Looked down at his granddaughter. "She's beautiful, Bella."

"She is."

"You did this." His voice was thick. "You built this. A family of your own."

"I had help."

"You had love. That's different." He touched Rose's cheek with one careful finger. "Hello, little one. I'm your grandfather. And I'm going to love you for the rest of my life."

---

That night, alone in the hospital room—Matt asleep on the uncomfortable chair, Rose in her bassinet—Bella watched her daughter breathe.

The face that would become her most accustomed.

The future she'd fought for, planned for, hoped for.

She was here.

It had only just begun.
