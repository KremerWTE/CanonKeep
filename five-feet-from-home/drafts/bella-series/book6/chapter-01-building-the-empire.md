# Chapter 1: Building the Empire

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part One: The Vision

---

Marriage changed Bella, but not in the ways she expected.

She didn't become smaller. She became bigger. More certain. More willing to take up space in the world.

"What's next?" Matt asked one evening, as they sat on the porch of their home—a cabin they'd built together on the edge of the ranch property.

Bella looked out at the mountains, the sunset painting the sky in shades of gold and rose.

"I want to build something," she said. "Something that's mine."

She'd been working three jobs for years now—BSS analyst grooming for fixer, TX event support and venue growth, COO and marketing for Elena's company Maison Aurelia. Her schedule was brutal: 4:30 AM starts, 1:30 AM bedtimes, constant travel and crisis management.

But she was good at it. All of it.

And she was ready to stop being the one who made other people's visions real. Ready to have a vision of her own.

The TX event support company had been growing organically for months. Bella had a talent for it—the same skills that had made her the sorority fixer, the finance firm's reliable analyst, the BSS crisis manager. She could see problems before they became disasters. She could read rooms and adjust on the fly.

"I want to expand the venue business," she told Matt. "Not just support other people's events. Build our own spaces. Create something that lasts."

Matt nodded slowly. "You'll need capital. Partners. A plan."

"I know." She smiled. "Good thing I've been learning from the best."

Elena had taught her business. Hawk had taught her strategy. Addie had taught her how to lead without apologizing for it.

Now it was time to put it all together.
