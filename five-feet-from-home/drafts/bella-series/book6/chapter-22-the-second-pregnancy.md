# Chapter 22: The Second Pregnancy

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part Eight: Growing Together

---

They hadn't planned for it to happen so soon.

Rose was fourteen months old when the test came back positive. No IVF this time—just old-fashioned luck after they'd given up worrying about it.

"How is this possible?" Bella asked, staring at the lines. "It took us two years and medical intervention last time."

"Bodies are mysterious." Matt pulled her close. "How do you feel?"

"Shocked. Scared. Happy." She laughed, a little hysterical. "All of the above."

---

The pregnancy was different the second time.

Less anxiety about the process—she knew her body could do this now. But more logistical stress. Rose was mobile, demanding, unaware that her mother was growing exhausted in new ways.

"I can't be as sick as I was," Bella said, during the first trimester. "There's no time."

Her body disagreed. The nausea was worse than before.

They learned from the first time.

Hired help earlier. Delegated more aggressively at work. Built in rest that wasn't optional.

"You're being smarter about this," Elena observed.

"I'm being more realistic. Last time I thought I could power through. This time I know better."

"That's growth."

"That's desperation."

---

Rose didn't understand the concept of a sibling.

"Baby in Mama's tummy," Bella explained, over and over.

Rose would pat her belly, then lose interest and toddle off to more pressing concerns.

"She'll understand when the baby arrives," Matt said.

"What if she doesn't? What if she feels replaced?"

"Then we'll help her through it. That's what parents do."

---

The anatomy scan came at twenty weeks.

A boy this time. Healthy, developing perfectly, already a distinct presence on the ultrasound screen.

"A boy," Bella breathed. "A son."

"A son." Matt's voice was thick. "Rose is going to have a brother."

They named him James—after Matt's father, who'd passed before they met. James Matthew Holloway.

---

Telling Hawk was different this time.

He wasn't shocked—he'd learned to expect the unexpected. But the joy was just as profound.

"Another grandchild. A grandson." He shook his head. "Bella, you're going to run out of firsts to give me."

"I'll find more."

"You always do." He smiled. "I never imagined, you know. When you first came into my life—all that uncertainty, all that potential—I never imagined this."

"Neither did I."

"And yet here we are."

"Here we are."

---

The third trimester brought familiar challenges.

Exhaustion compounded by chasing a toddler. Discomfort that no position could ease. The particular fatigue of being pregnant while parenting.

But also: Rose, pressing kisses to her belly. Matt, steady as always. The family, ready to expand again.

"Two kids under three," Bella said, during a moment of quiet panic. "What were we thinking?"

"We were thinking we wanted a family. And now we're getting one."

"Is that enough?"

"It's everything."
