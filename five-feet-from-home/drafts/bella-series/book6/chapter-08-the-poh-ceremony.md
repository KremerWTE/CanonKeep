# Chapter 8: The POH Ceremony

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part Three: The Business

---

The Palace of Honor spring investiture was the largest in a decade.

Fifteen new knights. Seven new dames. A ceremony that would bring together the entire Order, from founding members to the newest initiates.

And Bella, as Page, was coordinating all of it.

"This is enormous," she admitted to Addie, reviewing the logistics. "I don't know if I'm ready."

"You've been ready for years. Stop questioning yourself."

---

The preparation took months.

Venue coordination—the ceremony would be held at a historic estate in Virginia, secured through Wives Club connections. Catering for three hundred. Security protocols that made BSS look casual.

Bella worked with the Herald, with the Grand Marshal, with every rank of the Order. She learned names, histories, the intricate web of relationships that held everything together.

"You're good at this," the Grand Master observed, during a planning session. "The organization. The diplomacy."

"I've had good teachers."

"More than that. You have instincts." He studied her. "Have you considered what comes after Page?"

"I haven't thought that far ahead."

"Perhaps you should."

---

**[CEREMONY DETAILS]**

The day of the ceremony arrived.

Bella wore her formal Page attire—elegant but understated, designed to facilitate rather than stand out. The pendant was tucked beneath, felt but not seen.

The guests arrived in waves. Knights in their regalia. Dames in ceremonial dress. Families and supporters filling the viewing areas.

Everything was positioned. Everything was ready.

And then, as always, things went wrong.

---

The crisis was small but visible.

One of the initiates—a young man being knighted for exceptional community service—was having a panic attack in the preparation room. The ceremony was minutes away.

"I can't do this," he gasped. "All those people. What if I mess up the words? What if I trip?"

Bella found him. Knelt beside him.

"What's your name?"

"Thomas."

"Thomas. Look at me." She waited until his eyes met hers. "You've already done the hard part. The service. The years of work. This ceremony is just recognition of what you've already accomplished."

"But everyone's watching—"

"They're watching because they're proud of you. Because you've earned this." She took his hand. "You're not alone out there. I'll be in your line of sight the whole time. If you feel lost, look at me. I'll get you through."

He took a breath. Then another.

"Okay."

"Okay."

---

The ceremony was flawless.

Thomas performed beautifully. All fifteen knights, all seven dames—each one stepping into their new role with dignity and grace.

Bella watched from her designated position, managing the flow, troubleshooting the invisible problems that only she could see.

When it was over, the Grand Master found her.

"Exceptional work, Page."

"Thank you."

"I meant what I said earlier. Think about what comes next." His eyes were knowing. "The Order needs people like you. Not just organizers—leaders."

---

That night, alone in her hotel room, Bella thought about the path ahead.

Page. Herald. Eventually, perhaps, something more.

Or perhaps not. Perhaps her path was about building her own empire, not climbing someone else's ladder.

She didn't have to decide tonight.

But the question—what comes next?—had planted itself. And she knew it would keep growing.
