# Chapter 2: POH Page

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part Two: The Page Role

---

The Palace of Honor elevation came during her second year of marriage.

Bella had been doing POH work in the evenings for months—donor research, ceremonial write-ups, background briefs on knights and dames. It was the kind of quiet, essential work that kept the organization running smoothly.

The kind of work she'd always been good at.

But now, she was being asked to step into something more visible. POH Page—a role that carried weight and responsibility beyond the administrative. She would be the face of ceremonial communications, the coordinator for major events, the bridge between the Order's traditions and its modern operations.

"Are you sure?" she asked Addie, when the offer was made. "There are women who've been doing this longer than me."

Addie's smile was knowing. "Experience isn't always about time, Bella. It's about readiness. And you've been ready for a while now. The question is whether you'll let yourself accept it."

The old voice in Bella's head still whispered sometimes—*you're not enough, you don't belong*—but it was quieter now. Easier to ignore.

She said yes.

The POH work folded into her schedule: late nights after BSS duties, ceremonial prep during travel, donor communications squeezed into the margins of an already-packed day.

Her priorities remained:
1. Grace
2. Work
3. Families
4. Hawk
5. Mandy/Matt
6. Self enjoyment
7. Sleep
8. Workouts

Sleep came last. It always had. But the work felt meaningful in a way that made the exhaustion worth it.

She was building something. Not just for herself, but for the community that had chosen her.
