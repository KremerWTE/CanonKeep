# Chapter 16: The Accustomed Face

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part Six: The Transition

---

The title came from a song.

"My Fair Lady"—the musical that had been playing in Bella's head for years now. Hawk had first made the connection, calling her his Eliza Doolittle, the street girl transformed into a queen.

But the song that stuck wasn't the famous one about the rain in Spain. It was the quiet one at the end. "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face."

*I've grown accustomed to her face.*
*She almost makes the day begin.*

---

Bella thought about it often as she prepared for motherhood.

The accustomed faces in her life. Hawk's—stern and loving, the father she'd chosen. Addie's—warm and wise, always knowing what she needed before she did. Matt's—patient and steady, the face she woke up to every morning.

And soon, a new face. One she hadn't met yet. One that would become more familiar than her own.

---

"Why that song?" Matt asked, one evening, when she explained it.

"Because it's about belonging. About someone becoming so integral to your life that you can't imagine it without them."

"Like us?"

"Like us. Like Hawk. Like all of it." She placed his hand on her belly. "And like this one. Who's going to become the most accustomed face of all."

The baby kicked, as if agreeing.

---

Eight months now.

The nursery was ready—a labor of love involving Matt, Elena's design eye, and several weekends of careful assembly. Soft colors. Practical furniture. A rocking chair for the long nights ahead.

"We're ready," Matt said, surveying their work.

"The room is ready. I'm not sure about us."

"We'll never be ready. Not really." He smiled. "But we'll figure it out. That's what we do."

---

The nesting instinct had fully taken hold.

Bella, who'd never been particularly domestic, found herself reorganizing closets, washing tiny clothes, preparing freezer meals for the postpartum period.

"Who are you?" Elena teased, watching her batch-cook lasagna.

"I have no idea. The hormones have taken over."

"The hormones know what they're doing." Elena smiled. "Your body's preparing you for what comes next. Trust it."

---

The fire pit sessions had shifted.

Travel was restricted now—Bella couldn't fly this late in pregnancy, and long car rides were uncomfortable. But video calls kept them connected.

"How are you doing, daughter?" Hawk's face on the screen, the familiar room behind him.

"Enormous. Uncomfortable. Ready for this to be over."

"And how can I support you?"

"Just... be there. When she arrives." She'd learned the gender at the twenty-week scan—a girl. "Be there for both of us."

"Nothing could keep me away." His voice was thick. "I've waited for this, Bella. For years. The chance to be a grandfather. A real one."

"You've always been real, Dad."

"I know. But this is..." He paused. "This is more than I ever let myself hope for."

---

At thirty-six weeks, they had the final ultrasound.

The baby was healthy. Head down. Ready.

"Any day now," the doctor said. "Could be two weeks. Could be tomorrow. Babies have their own schedule."

Bella looked at the screen—at the face that would soon become her most accustomed.

"We're ready," she said.

And this time, she almost believed it.
