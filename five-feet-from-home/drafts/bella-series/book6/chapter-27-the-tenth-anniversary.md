# Chapter 27: The Tenth Anniversary

## Book 6: "I've Grown Accustomed to Her Face"
### Part Ten: A Decade

---

Ten years married.

The number felt impossible. Ten years since the barn wedding at Mandy's ranch. Ten years of building a life together.

"What do you want to do?" Matt asked. "For the anniversary. We could go somewhere."

"We have two kids under five."

"We have grandparents who are begging for more time with them."

Bella considered.

"Italy. We never did the honeymoon we originally planned."

"Italy it is."

---

Hawk and Addie took Rose and James for two weeks.

Two weeks. Bella couldn't remember the last time she'd had two consecutive hours without a child in range, let alone two weeks.

"Go," Addie said, practically pushing them toward the airport. "Live. Remember what it's like to be just two people."

"What if something happens—"

"Then we'll handle it. We raised multiple children. We can manage two grandchildren for a fortnight." Addie kissed her cheek. "Go be in love. That's an order."

---

Rome first.

Wandering ancient streets, eating at tiny restaurants, rediscovering each other without the constant interruption of parenthood.

"I forgot what this feels like," Bella admitted, on the second night. "Being alone with you. Being able to finish a conversation."

"I forgot what you look like without spit-up on your shoulder."

"That's romance for you."

"That's ten years of marriage for you."

---

Tuscany next.

A villa in the hills, surrounded by vineyards. Long afternoons reading, longer evenings drinking wine and talking about nothing and everything.

"What do you want the next ten years to look like?" Matt asked.

"More of this. The kids growing up. The business evolving. Us staying... us."

"That's specific."

"I don't need specific. I need sustainable." She looked at him. "I need to know that in another decade, we'll still be sitting on some porch somewhere, talking about what comes next."

"We will be."

"Promise?"

"Promise."

---

Positano last.

Cliffside hotels and dramatic sunsets. The Mediterranean stretching toward eternity.

"I love you," Bella said, watching the sun sink into the sea. "More than when we started. Which seems impossible, but it's true."

"Same." Matt's arm was around her. "The loving more part. Not the impossible part. Nothing about loving you has ever felt impossible."

"Except when I'm running on four hours of sleep and being irrational."

"Even then." He kissed her temple. "Maybe especially then. That's when you need it most."

---

Coming home was bittersweet.

The kids launched themselves at Bella the moment she walked through the door. Rose talked for twenty minutes straight about everything that had happened. James showed off a new word he'd learned ("Dino!").

"I missed you so much," Rose said, clutching her leg.

"I missed you too, baby."

"Did you bring me something?"

Some things never changed.

---

That night, after the kids were in bed:

"Good trip?" Matt asked.

"The best." Bella curled into him. "But I'm glad to be home."

"Me too."

"Ten years down."

"A lifetime to go."

"That sounds exactly right."
