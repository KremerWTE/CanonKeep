# Chapter 34: Ready for What Comes Next

## Book 3: "I Could Have Danced All Night"
### Epilogue

---

The morning after the gala, Bella woke early.

The compound was quiet—that particular stillness of a household recovering from celebration. She dressed simply, made coffee, and carried her mug to the fire pit.

The ashes were cold from the previous night's flames. But she sat there anyway, watching the sun rise over the Charlotte skyline.

---

Two years.

She'd been counting, tracking the milestones. Two years since the ask. Two years since the first fire pit conversation. Two years since she'd started believing she might be worth something.

But the counting felt less necessary now.

She wasn't measuring anymore. She was just... living.

---

Her phone buzzed. A text from Mandy.

**Mandy:** Thinking about our conversation. Let me know if you want to continue it in person.

Bella stared at the message for a long time.

Then she typed a response:

**Bella:** I'm coming back. Next month. If you have time.

**Mandy:** I'll make time.

---

Footsteps behind her. Addie, in a robe, carrying her own coffee.

"You're up early."

"Couldn't sleep. Good kind of restless."

Addie settled into the chair beside her. "You've been quiet since the gala. Processing?"

"Planning." Bella smiled. "I'm going back to Colorado."

"The ranch?"

"The ranch. The woman." Bella took a breath. "I don't know what it is yet. But I want to find out."

"That's growth." Addie's voice was warm. "The Bella I met two years ago would have talked herself out of this before even trying."

"The Bella you met two years ago was terrified of wanting things."

"And now?"

"Now I'm still terrified. But I'm doing it anyway."

---

They sat together as the sun climbed higher.

"Thank you," Bella said finally. "For everything. For seeing me before I could see myself. For being patient when I couldn't believe any of it was real."

"You did the hard work."

"I had help." Bella met Addie's eyes. "You gave me something I'd never had. A mother who chose me. A family that wanted me. A chance to become someone different."

"Someone better."

"Someone truer." Bella smiled. "I was always her. I just couldn't find her through all the noise."

---

Hawk joined them eventually, bringing fresh coffee and his quiet presence.

"I'm going to Colorado," Bella told him.

"I know. Addie told me." His eyes were knowing. "That's good. You need this."

"Need what?"

"To explore something that's just for you. Not work, not family, not obligation. Just... possibility." He passed her a refill. "That's rare in this life. When you find it, you chase it."

"What if it doesn't work out?"

"Then you come home. We're not going anywhere." His smile was warm. "That's what family means, daughter. A place to land when the exploration ends. Whatever you find out there, this will still be here."

---

That evening, Bella packed a bag.

Not to leave immediately—she had assignments to complete, transitions to manage, logistics to arrange. But the act of packing felt significant. A commitment to what came next.

She placed the white queen chess piece on her dresser, where she could see it every morning.

*The piece you've become,* Hawk had said.

She was starting to believe it.

---

Her journal entry that night was simple:

*Book 3 ends here.*

*I came to Charlotte hoping to be chosen. I was chosen—beyond anything I could have imagined. I have mentors, family, purpose, belonging. I have a place at the table and a name on the napkin and a pendant that means something.*

*But that's not the end of the story. That's just the foundation.*

*Now comes the part where I build on it. Where I figure out what I want for myself, not just what others want for me. Where I stop waiting to be chosen and start doing the choosing.*

*I'm going to Colorado. I'm going to see Mandy. I don't know what I'll find there—love, friendship, something else entirely. But I'm done being afraid to look.*

*The queen is on the board. And she's ready to move.*
