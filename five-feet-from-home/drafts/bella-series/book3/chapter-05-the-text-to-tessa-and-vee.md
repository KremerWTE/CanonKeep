# Chapter 5: The Text to Tessa and Vee

## Book 3: "I Could Have Danced All Night"
### Part Two: After

---

Later that night, alone in her apartment, Bella sat on the edge of her bed with her phone in her hands.

The pendant was cool against her skin. The book rested on her nightstand. The fire pit's warmth still clung to her bones.

She opened a group chat she hadn't used in months. Tessa and Vee. Two anchors to her old self—one who knew her depths, one who represented everything she'd once aspired to be.

Her thumb hovered over the keyboard.

**Bella:** Tonight... Addie gave me this.

She snapped a picture of the pendant, the symbol clear in the photo.

The replies came quickly.

**Vee (Vivian "Vee" Sinclair):**
...Bells. That's not just jewelry. That's the symbol. Do you understand what that means?

**Tessa:**
Holy sh*t. Bella, that's... wow. I knew you were going places, but this?

Bella stared at the screen, the pendant cool in her palm. She typed, then deleted, then typed again.

**Bella:** I didn't believe it would ever happen. I still don't know if I believe it.

**Vee:** Careful, Bells. That's not something you "see." That's something you're invited into. If you're right... you've just brushed shoulders with something real.

**Tessa:** Wait. You're serious? Bells, what did you get yourself into?

For years, Vee had been the mirror of everything Bella thought she wasn't—the polished Harvard friend who glided through circles Bella could only dream of entering. And Tessa had been the one who knew her heart, even when their lives drifted apart.

Now, here she was. Wearing the symbol. Holding the book. Belonging to something real.

**Bella:** Not sure yet. But I think I'm closer than I ever thought I'd be.

**Vee:** Then don't let go. Whatever you had to do to get there—it was worth it. And Bella? If you ever need someone who understands what that world is like... I'm here.

**Tessa:** Same. Minus the fancy Wives Club knowledge. But I'm always here for you. You know that.

Bella smiled at the screen, her reflection faint in the glass.

For years she'd dreamed quietly, convinced it was never meant for her. She'd compared herself to Vee, mourned the distance from Tessa, convinced herself she was destined for the background.

But tonight, something had shifted.

She wasn't the fixer anymore. She wasn't the shadow.

She was chosen. She was family. She was enough.

And for the first time in years, she let herself believe it might be true.
