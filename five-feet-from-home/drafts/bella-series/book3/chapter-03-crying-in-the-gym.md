# Chapter 3: Crying in the Gym

## Book 3: "I Could Have Danced All Night"
### Part One: The Ceremony

---

Bella didn't make it back to her room that night.

After the book—after "Mom" and "Dad" written in their hands, after Hawk's steady voice saying *you don't walk this board alone*—she needed to move. To process. To be somewhere the walls weren't pressing in.

The gym was empty. Dark except for the emergency lights casting long shadows across the equipment.

She found a corner, slid down against the wall, and pulled out her journal.

The words came in fragments at first. Half-sentences. Lists that didn't make sense. But as the pen moved, something cracked open inside her.

*All my life I thought I wasn't worth choosing.*

*All my life I prepared for the moment everyone would realize I didn't belong.*

*Tonight they chose me anyway.*

*Tonight they called me daughter.*

*Tonight they wrote it down.*

She didn't realize she was crying until the tears splattered on the page, smudging the ink.

It wasn't sadness. It was the opposite—a grief that came from finally receiving what she'd always wanted, and realizing how long she'd convinced herself she couldn't have it.

The old voices rose up: *This isn't real. They'll change their minds. You're not worth this.*

But tonight, for the first time, other voices answered back. Hawk's voice: *You're mine now. That doesn't end.* Addie's voice: *You've always belonged.* The weight of the pendant. The red key. The pink key. The signatures on the book.

Evidence. Proof. Something to hold onto when the doubts came flooding back.

She wrote until her hand cramped. Wrote until the tears stopped. Wrote until the gym lights flickered on with the first hint of dawn.

Later, Hawk would find out she'd been here. He always found out.

And later still, at the fire pit, he would sit with her and remind her that breaking open wasn't the same as breaking apart.

But for now, in the quiet of the gym, Bella let herself feel it all.

The terror of being seen.

The relief of being claimed.

The beginning of believing it might be true.
