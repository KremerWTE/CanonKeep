# Chapter 1: The Three Gifts

## Book 3: "I Could Have Danced All Night"
### Part One: The Ceremony

---

The engagement dinner had already felt overwhelming—laughter, toasts, champagne, the pendant glinting at her throat.

---

Bella had spent three hours getting ready.

The dress was champagne-colored silk—not white, not gold, but something in between that seemed to glow in candlelight. Elena had helped her choose it during a shopping trip the week before, steering her away from the safe blacks and navys she usually gravitated toward.

"Tonight you're not blending in," Elena had said, holding the dress against Bella's frame. "Tonight you're being seen. Dress like you mean it."

The gown was floor-length with a subtle A-line silhouette, fitted through the bodice with a sweetheart neckline that was elegant without being revealing. Delicate beading traced the waistline—tiny crystals that caught light when she moved. The straps were thin, almost invisible, giving the illusion that the dress simply floated against her skin.

Her shoes were nude heels—higher than she usually wore, but Kendra had insisted. "Stand tall tonight. You've earned it."

Her hair was down, styled in soft waves that cascaded over her shoulders. Harper had sent her stylist, who'd spent an hour creating curls that looked effortless but would hold all evening. A few tendrils were pinned back with small crystal clips that matched the dress's beading.

Her makeup was more dramatic than usual—smoky eyes that made her gaze deeper, a nude lip that wouldn't compete with everything else, subtle highlighting that caught the light. She barely recognized herself in the mirror.

When she arrived, the other women were waiting.

**Addie** wore emerald green—a fitted cocktail dress that fell just below the knee, her signature color, making her hazel eyes glow like gemstones. Her ash-blonde hair was swept into an elegant updo, a few pieces framing her face. She looked like royalty—which, Bella was learning, she essentially was.

**Elena** was in ivory—a column gown that moved like liquid when she walked, her blonde hair pinned in a sophisticated chignon. Diamonds at her ears, her throat, her wrist. The CEO of Maison Aurelia dressed like a woman who shaped the world's most elegant events, because she was.

**Harper** had chosen deep burgundy—a structured dress with architectural lines that somehow made her look both powerful and feminine. Her dark hair was sleek and straight, her makeup minimal but precise. Everything about Harper suggested calculated elegance.

**Kendra** wore sapphire blue—athletic and elegant at once, a dress that showed off the shoulders and arms she'd earned through years of CrossFit. Her black hair was pulled back in a low ponytail, practical and beautiful simultaneously.

Together, they looked like a council of queens.

And somehow, impossibly, they'd made room for Bella among them.

---

But for Bella, the night unfolded into something more profound.

**The First Gift — The Pendant**

In front of the room, Addie placed the delicate gold pendant in her hands. Etched with both the Club symbol and the subtle sigil of their sorority subsection.

It wasn't jewelry. It was recognition—public, undeniable.

The whispered dream Bella had carried since Vee first told her about the Wives Club was now around her neck. Real. Tangible. Hers.

**The Second Gift — The Check**

Minutes later, Hawk handed her an envelope in full view of the table.

"For the last four months of work," he said simply.

Inside: her bonus check. The number stunned her—larger than her entire yearly salary at the Boston finance firm.

For Bella, who had always measured her worth in quiet competence and background labor, it was tangible proof: her value was real, measurable, seen.

**The Third Gift — The Book (Private)**

Later, when the night quieted and the guests had gone, Hawk and Addie pulled Bella aside.

They handed her a small, leather-bound book: My Fair Lady.

"Go on," Addie urged.

Bella flipped it open. Inside the back cover, she found scrawled signatures:

*"Mom" — Addie*
*"Dad" — Hawk*

And beneath, in Hawk's sharp hand: *"You've always been on the board. Now you know it."*

Her throat tightened. "I don't know what to say."

Hawk stepped forward. "You don't have to say anything. This isn't just a story about a girl becoming something more. It's about her finally seeing what was already there. You're not being made into someone new, Bella. You're being asked to believe in who you already are."

Addie touched her arm. "We wanted you to have this from us—not just as partners in BSS, not just as Club sisters, but as family."

"Family?" Bella whispered.

"Mom and Dad," Hawk said. "It's written down now. You don't walk this board alone."

Bella clutched the book, the signatures pressing into her palm.

For the first time in her life, the voice that always whispered *you're not enough* went quiet.
