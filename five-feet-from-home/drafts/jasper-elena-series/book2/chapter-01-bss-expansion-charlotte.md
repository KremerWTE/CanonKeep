## Why Charlotte?

Jasper started in Charlotte because it was a financial hub but not oversaturated like New York. He quietly built his first network here with hedge funds, private equity shops, and Southern family offices.

The city was perfect—old money meeting new ambition, banking fortunes rubbing shoulders with tech entrepreneurs, close enough to Washington for access but far enough to avoid the suffocating politics.

"Why here?" Harper had asked when he first pitched the location.

"Because nobody expects power to come from Charlotte," Jasper replied. "They'll underestimate us. That's an advantage."

---

## The First Big Case

The key catalyst came from an unexpected source.

A scandal tied to a NASCAR sponsor and a regional bank almost collapsing gave Jasper his first big case. The sponsor—a household name, plastered across race cars and beer commercials—had been caught in a web of financial irregularities that threatened to bring down both the racing team and the bank that backed them.

"This is a mess," Harper said, reviewing the files in their cramped first office.

"This is an opportunity," Jasper corrected. "If we fix this quietly, we earn a reputation for fixing problems without headlines."

He was right. By the time the dust settled, the sponsor kept his name, the bank kept its doors open, and BSS had its first major success story. The grateful clients sent referrals. The referrals sent more referrals.

Within two years, BSS was the name whispered in board rooms when things went wrong.

---

## The Early Team

Harper anchored him from Miami, handling structure and finance while he did client-facing work.

Elena—not yet his wife, but already becoming something more—was in his orbit, her events company occasionally overlapping with his client base.

And Addie was just entering the story as the rough but sharp assistant who was evolving into a force of her own.

"She's good," Harper observed during one of her Charlotte visits, watching Addie handle a nervous client with surprising grace.

"She's raw," Jasper corrected. "But she has instincts. We can train instincts."

"Or she trains us," Harper replied, half-joking.

Neither of them knew how right she was.

---

## The Forge

Charlotte became the forge—the place where BSS's identity was shaped.

They learned to move quietly, to solve problems without creating new ones, to build relationships that lasted beyond single transactions. They made mistakes and fixed them. They lost cases and analyzed what went wrong. They built systems and then rebuilt them when the systems failed.

"Every empire starts somewhere," Jasper said one evening, standing in their new office—bigger now, with actual windows and proper furniture.

"This isn't an empire yet," Harper reminded him. "This is a startup with good suits."

"Give it time."

She looked at him, at the determination in his eyes, and believed him.
