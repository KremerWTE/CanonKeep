### Part One: NYC, DC, Boston

## New York City

After Charlotte's success, Jasper was pulled into Wall Street and media crises. Anchors, networks, hedge funds—the problems were bigger but the mechanics were the same.

NYC became the second hub, giving him international visibility. The first case was a hedge fund manager caught in a public scandal that threatened to tank not just his fund but three related institutions.

"This is above our pay grade," Harper warned from Miami.

"It was," Jasper agreed. "Now it's exactly our pay grade."

He flew up, met with the principals, and within seventy-two hours had a plan. The scandal didn't disappear—that wasn't possible—but it was contained, managed, and ultimately survivable.

The hedge fund manager sent a bottle of champagne with a note: *You're worth every penny. Here's hoping I never need you again.*

He did, of course. They always did.

## Washington, D.C.

Leveraging military and intel ties, BSS built influence in defense contracting, lobbying scandals, and foreign-agent registration cases.

"D.C. is different," Harper warned. "The game is dirtier."

"The game is politics," Jasper replied. "We're not here to play—we're here to be useful."

He was careful in Washington. He didn't take sides, didn't align with parties or factions. BSS became known as a resource, not a player. Senators, lobbyists, and defense contractors all knew the number to call when things went sideways.

Hawk's Tier 1 background started feeding into these contracts. His connections opened doors that money alone couldn't unlock.

"You're building something dangerous," Hawk observed one evening over bourbon.

"I'm building something necessary," Jasper countered. "There's a difference."

---

## Boston

BSS plugged into biotech and elite universities—a different ecosystem entirely, but one that needed their services just as badly.

A pharmaceutical startup was being squeezed by a competitor using industrial espionage. A university president was facing whispers of misconduct that threatened to explode into public scandal. A family office was bleeding money to an employee who'd been skimming for years.

Addie and Kendra's analyst networks thrived here. Boston became their proving ground—a place where sharp minds mattered more than muscle.

"These people think differently," Kendra reported. "They're used to solving problems with data and peer review. We have to speak their language."

"Then learn it," Jasper said. "That's what we do."

They did. And Boston became another spoke in the wheel.

---

## The Machine Takes Shape

By year five, BSS had three offices on the East Coast, each with its own character:
- **Charlotte**: The heart. Family, compound, home base.
- **New York**: The engine. Money, media, international visibility.
- **D.C.**: The nerve center. Government, defense, intelligence.
- **Boston**: The brain trust. Biotech, academia, old-money families.

"We're not a company anymore," Harper said, reviewing the quarterly numbers. "We're an institution."

"Not yet," Jasper replied. "But we're getting there."

The next step was across the ocean.
