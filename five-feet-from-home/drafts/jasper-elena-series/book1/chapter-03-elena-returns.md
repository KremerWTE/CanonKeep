## On Pause

During Jasper's peak travel years, Elena had pulled back from her full CEO role to be the stabilizer at home.

She still kept her hand in Maison Aurelia, running select high-profile events but mostly letting her deputy team lead. She attended board meetings by video. She reviewed proposals between school pickups. She was present, but diminished.

Some days, she barely recognized herself. The woman who had built a global events empire now coordinated playdates and managed household logistics. It was important work—she knew that. But something inside her flickered, waiting to reignite.

## The Call

A former client called: "We only trust Elena."

She hesitated. The event was a Vatican-linked gala in New York—exactly the kind of high-profile production she'd sworn off while Jasper was traveling.

But Jasper was home more now. The kids were older. And the voice on the phone was practically begging.

"One event," she told herself. "Just one."

## The Fire

The ballroom shimmered under crystal chandeliers, the scent of orchids drifting through the air. Elena moved like a queen through her court, adjusting a wine glass here, smoothing a nervous client's hand there.

She had said yes to the gala almost out of guilt—the client had begged, and Jasper had urged her. But now, watching ambassadors, CEOs, and bishops lean in when she spoke, she felt the old fire.

Everything clicked. The timing of the toasts. The flow of the room. The way she could read tension and redirect it with a smile, a touch, a perfectly placed comment.

By the end of the night, even the Archbishop raised a glass in her honor.

"This is where you belong," Jasper whispered when he caught her eye across the room.

And for the first time in years, she allowed herself to believe it.

## The Restructure

Months later, Elena stood at the head of her new headquarters.

Maison Aurelia wasn't just event planning anymore—it was diplomacy dressed in couture. She restructured the company as a global event and lifestyle management firm: cultural diplomacy dinners, Vatican-linked fundraisers, Fortune 100 CEO summits.

Her calendar glittered with places where influence was bartered as easily as champagne. Rome. Dubai. New York. London.

She built a client expectation system: her name brought prestige, but she trained protégés to run day-to-day operations while she curated vision and elite deals.

Her clients threatened to walk if she didn't personally run things, but she smartly packaged her team as "extensions of Elena"—just like BSS built fixers under Addie.

## The Partnership

At home, she and Jasper traded shifts like generals.

If she flew to Rome, he held the fort. If he had to anchor a client dinner, she skipped Paris. Their calendars, once competing, now bent toward a shared center: family.

"We're doing this together now," she said one night, curled against him in bed. "Really together."

"Took us long enough to figure that out," he admitted.

"Better late than never."

She ran joint ventures with BSS: dinners where crises were smoothed over socially, creating a natural partnership between their empires. Jasper shifted into more office-based strategic command, freeing her to be front-facing at elite events.

Their marriage evolved into a power couple model:
- **Jasper** → Builder of legacies, strategist at HQ.
- **Elena** → Face of elegance, architect of influence through events.

Together they hosted Palace of Honor-style galas, merging his networks and her artistry, reinforcing each other.
