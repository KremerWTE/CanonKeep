# Chapter 1: The Missed Play

## Jasper & Elena Series - Book 1: "Empire and Home"

## Work 90% / Family 10%

Jasper lived in airports and boardrooms, running BSS and elite crisis ops almost nonstop.

Elena and the kids saw him at dinners and Sundays, but he was mentally still on work calls. His body occupied the chair at the head of the table, but his mind was three time zones away, calculating outcomes, anticipating crises.

[SETTING DETAILS NEEDED - Barrett family home, dining room]

"Dad, guess what happened at soccer today?" His daughter's voice cut through the fog of his thoughts.

"Hmm?" Jasper looked up from his phone. "That's great, sweetheart."

"You didn't even hear what I said."

"Of course I did. Soccer. Something happened."

Elena's eyes met his across the table. She said nothing. She didn't have to.

---

## Hawk's Warning

Later that week, Jasper sat with Hawk by the fire pit, bourbon in hand, the flames casting dancing shadows across the compound.

"You're building an empire but missing the kingdom at home," Hawk said quietly, puffing his cigar.

Jasper bristled. "I'm providing for my family."

"You're hiding from them." Hawk didn't flinch. "There's a difference."

"I don't need parenting advice from—"

"From someone who's been where you're headed?" Hawk turned to face him fully. "I've seen men lose everything because they couldn't tell the difference between duty and obsession. Don't be one of them."

The fire crackled. Jasper stared into it, seeing nothing but flames.

---

## The Breaking Point

The breaking point came on a Tuesday.

Jasper adjusted the cuff of his tailored jacket as the boardroom lights dimmed for the presentation. A billion-dollar deal on the line, hedge funders in a panic, and only he could calm the storm.

But when his phone buzzed, the image that lit the screen wasn't another crisis alert—it was Elena, streaming a grainy video of their daughter on stage.

She wore a crown made of paper stars, her voice shaky but proud as she sang. The applause roared through the phone, tinny but undeniable.

Jasper smiled, then frowned.

His seat at the play was empty.

---

## The Silence That Cut

Later that night, Elena didn't yell. She simply asked, "How many more plays do you want to watch on someone else's phone?"

The silence that followed cut deeper than any boardroom battle.

[INTIMATE SCENE - EXPAND - The tension between them that night, the distance in their bedroom]

Jasper sat on the edge of the bed, head in his hands. "I thought I was doing the right thing. The deal—"

"There's always a deal." Elena's voice was gentle, which somehow made it worse. "There's always a crisis. There's always something that feels more urgent than being here."

"I'm trying to build something for them—"

"They don't need a legacy, Jasper. They need a father." She sat beside him, took his hand. "You'll build ten more companies. You only get one chance to be their father."

He didn't sleep that night. He just stared at the ceiling, replaying the video of his daughter in her paper crown, singing to an empty seat.

---

## The First Step

The next morning, Jasper called Harper.

"I need you to restructure my schedule," he said. "Starting now."

Harper paused. In all their years working together, she'd never heard him sound quite like this. "What kind of restructuring?"

"The kind where I'm not on a plane every week. The kind where I'm home for dinner more than twice a month." He took a breath. "The kind where my kids actually see me."

Silence on the line. Then: "It's about time."

"Don't gloat."

"I'm not gloating. I'm already pulling up your calendar." He could hear her smile through the phone. "Let's get to work."
