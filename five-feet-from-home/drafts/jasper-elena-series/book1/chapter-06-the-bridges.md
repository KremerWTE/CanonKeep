## Airport Hand-Off

Jasper came down the escalator at CLT, still on a call. Addie was waiting, her hair tied back, a dossier in hand. Behind her, Bella had the kids in tow with ice cream cones, and Kendra pushed a stroller.

Addie cut Jasper off mid-call. "Plane's late, client's furious, but we've contained it. Go home. Bella's got the kids. I'll deal with the sharks."

Bella saluted with an ice cream spoon. "Fun aunt to the rescue."

Kendra gave him a look only another parent could give. "You've got twenty-four hours before Elena's wheels up for Paris. Use them wisely."

For once, Jasper ended a call without finishing. He bent down to hug his daughter instead.

Addie leaned back in one of the new chairs, smirking. "You can thank us later. You're not allowed to use 'the office was too far' as an excuse anymore."

Kendra set down a box of files. "This is about accountability. You said you wanted to be here more. Now you can. No excuses."

## Jasper Gets Called Out

Late one night, Jasper sat hunched over his phone, scrolling through case updates. Addie caught him from the doorway.

"You said no phones after nine."

He didn't look up. "This one's important."

Bella walked in behind her, arms folded. "They're all important. That's the problem."

Kendra joined, carrying tea. "You either set the example or your kids will grow up thinking work always wins."

Three pairs of eyes leveled at him. Jasper groaned and dropped the phone on the table. "You three are relentless."

Addie smirked. "That's why you keep us."

---

## Elena Pulled Back to Center

Elena had just landed from Paris, exhausted, buzzing from the success of a major event. She was already reaching for her laptop when Bella grabbed her wrist.

"Nope. Family dinner first. Clients can wait."

Addie backed her up. "You tell us all the time to guard priorities. Now we're telling you."

Kendra handed Elena her daughter, who squealed in delight. "See? This is what you come home for."

Elena laughed, finally closing the laptop. "You're as bad as you are with Jasper."

Bella winked. "Exactly. Equal opportunity honesty."

## The Dynamic

They were the bridges—and the guardrails.

**Addie** → Carries Jasper's crises so he can stay home; teases him into relaxing. Seamlessly translates between BSS operations and Palace of Honor society.

**Kendra** → Guides Elena's re-entry into CEO life, shares motherhood balance wisdom. Embodies the professional/maternal duality.

**Bella** → Becomes the kids' fun/protective aunt, giving Jasper and Elena breathing space. The relational glue that brings lightness into heavy worlds.

**Sara** → The steady family anchor, stepping in whenever both parents are pulled away. The grounding presence who doesn't let anyone take themselves too seriously.

Together, they didn't just support Jasper and Elena's empire. They made it possible.
