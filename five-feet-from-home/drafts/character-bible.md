## 1. JASPER BARRETT

### Book Appearances
- **Book 1:** Five Feet From Home *(Main protagonist)*
- **Book 2:** The Ownership Game *(Main protagonist)*
- **Book 3:** African Thunder *(Main protagonist)*
- **Books 4-9:** Royal Phoenix Series *(Main protagonist throughout)*

### Physical Description
- **Age:** Implied 40s-early 50s in current timeline
- **Build:** Fit, professional bearing
- **Eyes:** Intense, calculating
- **Hair:** Generally styled professionally
- **Distinguishing features:** Sharp, commanding presence; has a "dive watch" mentioned; typically dressed in suits (charcoal gray, pale blue shirts, often with sleeves rolled)

### Occupation/Role
- CEO of Barrett Security Solutions (BSS) - corporate crisis management firm
- Former high-ranking military/operational background (implied Tier 1)
- Handles international crises: London data breaches, Hong Kong scandals, Chicago contamination cases
- Mentor to junior consultants

### Personality Traits
- Obsessively task-focused; masters pressure and high-stakes situations
- Compartmentalizes: work bubble vs. family sphere
- Conflict between professional ambition and family duty
- Eventually learns to prioritize family over career expansion
- Strategic thinker; plays "chessmaster"
- Protective but emotionally distant initially
- Reflective and vulnerable with Elena and Grace (selective vulnerability)
- Uses military-style briefing language at work
- Southern roots occasionally surface

### Backstory/Arc
- Built BSS into an elite firm
- Brilliant at managing crises but failing at managing marriage
- Elena's medical crisis (SVT/aneurysm) becomes the turning point
- Realizes "five-foot rule" mindset (focusing only on immediate crisis) destroys family
- Chooses family over global expansion at novel's climax
- **Story arc:** work-obsessed manager → present father and devoted husband

### Key Relationships
- **Elena:** Marriage strained by absences; rebuilds love story through novel
- **Grace:** Daughter; central motivation for change; promises to attend her recital
- **Addison:** Sister; she manages his business crises while Elena manages home
- **Cole:** Right hand man/friend; Tier 1 background connection
- **Hawk:** Parallel ally with similar background; different path (Vegas security)

### Specific Details
- Carries wallet photo of Elena and Grace from beach (kite in background)
- Fixes Grace's bike (sawdust smell)
- Mentioned smelling of drizzle/rain
- Works from kitchen table when home
- Drives; has work calls while at child's sports events

## 2. ELENA BARRETT

### Book Appearances
- **Book 1:** Five Feet From Home *(Main character - medical crisis arc)*
- **Book 2:** The Ownership Game *(Main character - pregnancy arc)*
- **Book 3:** African Thunder *(Supporting role)*
- **Books 4-9:** Royal Phoenix Series *(Major recurring character)*

### Physical Description
- **Age:** Implied late 30s-early 40s
- **Hair:** Frequently described as damp, in messy knot, loose, pinned up; appears naturally dark
- **Eyes:** Warm but tired/strained
- **Build:** Graceful, feminine
- **Clothing style:** College sweatshirts at home, pale blue blouse, fitted slacks, knit dress in sage green, patterned leggings, maternity wear (second pregnancy)
- **Distinguishing features:** Gold charm necklace; exhaustion visible under eyes; "pallor of a woman who hadn't been sleeping well"

### Occupation/Role
- Mother to Grace (and Lucas in later books)
- CEO/Founder of Maison Aurelia (global event/lifestyle management firm)
- Specialties: Vatican-linked fundraisers, Fortune 100 CEO summits, cultural diplomacy dinners, Palace of Honor galas
- Real estate background (high-end agent before family)
- Nonprofit board roles
- Previously mentioned as "Southern-born"

### Personality Traits
- Warm, nurturing heart of the Barrett home
- Initially quiet about struggles (doesn't ask for help)
- Strong beneath softness
- Values family deeply
- Diplomatic and sophisticated in social settings
- Slowly rediscovers herself as marriage rebuilds
- Supportive of Addie and Kendra's relationship
- Acts as anchor/therapist figure for others
- Eventually becomes more assertive and present

### Backstory/Arc
- Southern upbringing (mentioned Knights of Columbus family background)
- Built Maison Aurelia while raising Grace
- Marriage deteriorated as Jasper traveled constantly
- Medical crisis (SVT/possible aneurysm) - sudden collapse during dinner
- Hospitalization becomes turning point for family
- Eventually reopens intimacy with Jasper
- Begins working in real estate and nonprofit board role

### Key Relationships
- **Jasper:** Central love story; reconnects after medical crisis
- **Grace:** Devoted mother; protective
- **Addison:** Close sister-in-law; friendship deepens
- **Kendra:** Becomes close; involved in polyamorous exploration
- **Sara:** Sister; supportive presence
- **Jessica:** Friend (appears to be mother or family friend)
- **Lucas:** Son; born during novel's events

### Specific Details
- Makes soup (rainy days)
- Preferred nap blanket from living room
- Drives to CrossFit gym
- Loves tea (frequently has decaf during pregnancy)
- Celebrates events with wine and firepit conversations
- Helps Grace with school projects (heritage, genealogy)

## 3. GRACE BARRETT

### Book Appearances
- **Book 1:** Five Feet From Home *(Key character - "Is Daddy on our team?")*
- **Book 2:** The Ownership Game *(Supporting role)*
- **Book 3:** African Thunder *(Supporting role)*
- **Books 4-9:** Royal Phoenix Series *(Ages 7-10 across series, grows into mentor role)*

### Physical Description
- **Age:** 7 (Book 1) → continues aging through series (ages 7-10 across first 3 books)
- **Hair:** Brown, frequently curly/wispy from humidity, often in crooked braid, messy ponytail, or with ribbons/clips
- **Eyes:** Expressive, bright, wide; catches light easily
- **Build:** Small, child-sized; described as "light sleeper"
- **Distinguishing features:** Precocious for age; serious/appraising look; dimple-like expressions when smiling
- **Clothing:** School polos, leggings, tulle dress (blush and champagne), glitter sneakers, unicorn leggings, school hoodie

### Occupation/Role
- Student (1st grade Book 1)
- Becomes assistant coach at Bella's swim facility
- Mentors younger swimmers (especially Jazz)

### Personality Traits
- Playful but perceptive
- Jamie Scott vibes (OTH character reference) - can be both comic relief and surprisingly wise
- Protective/loyal to family
- Direct communicator; asks important questions innocently
- Brave but anxious about Jasper's absences
- Acts as family peacemaker
- Has natural leadership/mentor instincts with younger kids
- Observant of adult dynamics
- Calls things out (loving, not mean)

### Backstory/Arc
- Daughter of Jasper and Elena
- Grows up with absent father (Jasper's work obsession)
- Suffers broken foot (soft cast) early in Book 1 → triggers family crisis
- Asks pivotal question: **"Is Daddy on our team?"** (becomes central to story)
- Worries about Jasper missing her recital (he does initially, becomes turning point)
- Natural bond with Addie, Kendra, Bella (aunts)
- Becomes mentor to younger swimmers

### Key Relationships
- **Jasper:** Adores him; central to his character arc; wants him present
- **Elena:** Close, protective mother
- **Addison:** "Tiger Aunt" (after zoo trip); Grace adores her; calls her warrior goddess
- **Kendra:** Playful aunt; helps with hair/fashion
- **Bella:** Swimming mentor
- **Lucas:** Baby brother (born during series)
- **Emma:** Cousin (Sara's daughter); close friendship
- **Jazz:** Younger swimmer she mentors

### Specific Details
- Makes drawings for fridge (crooked house, blue cat, "DADDY SAVES THE DAY" in bubble letters with backwards A)
- Smells like strawberries and pencil shavings
- Makes paper lighthouse for doorway guard
- Loves Disney (especially Frozen); has plush Olaf
- Enjoys swimming, soccer, art projects
- Says direct but innocent things that cut to truth
- Loves watching grown-ups she cares about; acts as small judge/observer

- **Height:** Fit and capable
- **Distinguishing features:** Model-like appearance; polished; "stepped off runway"; moves with confidence and control
- **Clothing:** Silk robes, emerald gown, deep navy halter dress, professional blazers, leggings (bright coral, patterned), typical office wear

### Occupation/Role

- Right-hand woman to Jasper
- Crisis management specialist
- Handles business development, client relationships
- Eventually becomes co-founder figure
- Mentors junior staff
- Serves on Phoenix Foundation board (Hawk's nonprofit)

### Personality Traits
- Brilliant strategist and problem solver
- Guarded; wears "armor" (emotional protection)
- Perfectionistic; demands excellence
- Capable of extreme focus and compartmentalization
- Loyal to core people (Jasper, Elena, Kendra, Cole)
- Protective instincts run deep
- Initially cold/distant; gradually shows vulnerability
- Competitive but fair leader
- Uses humor as deflection
- Deeply uncomfortable with loss of control
- Eventually learns to trust others

### Backstory/Arc
- Worked way up in crisis management field
- Created BSS with Jasper; became integral to success
- Overwork leads to health crisis/collapse (Miami hospital scene)
- Medical emergency becomes catalyst for life reevaluation
- Had history with Kendra (possibly romantic) that fractured
- Complex emotions about Elena and marriage
- Meets Hawk; rebuilds life with his support
- Eventually moves toward authentic self-expression
- Polyamorous exploration (with Elena and Kendra)
- Becomes Royal Phoenix at Palace of Honor (title/role)

### Key Relationships
- **Jasper:** Sister figure/co-founder; mutual respect and loyalty
- **Elena:** Complicated; deep affection; romantic/sexual exploration
- **Kendra:** Former romantic connection; rebuilds throughout series
- **Grace:** Grows to adore Grace; "Tiger Aunt"; mentors her
- **Hawk:** Boyfriend/partner; stabilizing force; security head
- **Cole:** Colleague; part of inner circle
- **Harper:** Possibly former flame or complicated connection (implied)

### Specific Details
- Works intensely (laptop balanced on hospital tray while recovering)
- Makes coffee in specific way; cares about ritual
- Trains hard at gym; uses physical exertion to process emotion
- Protective of Grace (tucking hair, kissing forehead)
- Watches Frozen with Grace multiple times; identifies with Elsa
- Has Tier 1 understanding (through Hawk and Cole)
- Strong in crisis; weak in vulnerability (learning curve)
