# Chapter 9: The Chosen Aunts

## Harper Series - Book 1: "The Shadow's Ledger"
### Part Four: Charlotte Weekend

---

The CLT compound was quieter than usual that Saturday morning, the hum of servers muffled by the sound of children's laughter drifting in from the courtyard.

Grace stood by the French doors, coffee in hand, as Harper's three kids tumbled across the lawn with her own.

"Your brood fits right in," Grace said with a smile.

"They think this place is summer camp," Harper replied, half-smiling as she leaned against the doorframe. Her heels were still on, though her jacket hung loose over a silk blouse. She never fully relaxed, even here.

## The Great Yard Battle

Outside, Izzy—sharp-eyed, hair pulled back into a neat braid—was trying to organize the chaos.

"Okay, teams!" she shouted. "Nico, you're with me. Sofia, you're with Emily. First to the oak tree wins!"

"Not fair," Nico protested, his grin already wide as he bolted anyway. Sofia squealed, chasing after him with Emily, Grace's daughter, hot on her heels.

Grace chuckled. "Izzy's definitely your daughter. Already running operations."

Harper allowed herself a rare laugh. "She'll be writing rotation schedules by ten."

Behind them, Chiara moved gracefully through the kitchen, setting down plates of cut fruit for the stampede she knew was coming. Her Italian accent softened her English: "They will need fuel if they are to conquer the world."

"Or just the yard," Selene teased as she walked in, sunglasses perched in her hair, already dressed like she had somewhere glamorous to be later. She peered outside at the kids. "Which one's the general?"

"Sofia," Harper and Grace said at the same time.

---

## Favorite Aunts

They watched as the youngest twin stood on a stone bench, hands on her hips, issuing orders like a commander. "No, Nico, we're *storming* the castle, not hiding in it! Emily, take the flag!"

Bella arrived next, arms full of craft supplies, grinning as she dropped them on the counter. "Figured they'd want banners. Every army needs a banner."

Within minutes, the kitchen was alive with glitter, markers, and construction paper. Izzy carefully drew an emblem—a shield with a star—while Nico scrawled something that looked more like a dragon breathing fire. Sofia, naturally, chose pink and gold for hers.

"Chosen aunts," Harper muttered dryly, watching Bella crouch to help Izzy, Selene sneak Sofia an extra handful of glitter, and Kendra—who had slipped in quietly—show Nico how to fold paper into makeshift spears.

"They love it here," Chiara said softly beside her. "Every chance they get, they ask when we visit again."

"And you?" Harper asked without looking at her.

Chiara smiled, her dark eyes following the children. "For me, it is family. Different, but real."

---

## The Declaration

The courtyard was loud with laughter as Nico tumbled into Selene's lap, glitter still stuck in his hair.

"You're my favorite," he declared boldly.

Selene smirked, ruffling his hair. "Good answer, kid."

Inside, Izzy sat at the table with Kendra, carefully practicing cursive while Kendra corrected her loops. "You're my favorite aunt," she whispered seriously.

Kendra arched a brow. "Why's that?"

"Because you explain things. And you don't talk to me like I'm little."

Kendra smiled, brushing Izzy's braid back over her shoulder. "That's because you're not little. You're already thinking bigger than half the adults."

Meanwhile, Sofia swept into the kitchen in a glittery crown Bella had made for her. She planted herself in Bella's lap. "You're my favorite. Because you know how to treat royalty."

Bella gave a mock bow. "Anything for Her Majesty."

---

## Grace's Assessment

Grace ran through the compound, her arms full of drawings. She dropped them onto Kendra's lap, then tugged on Bella's sleeve, then climbed into Addie's chair without asking.

"You're supposed to pick one favorite," Bella teased.

Grace shook her head fiercely. "No. I like all three. You're the best."

She paused, frowning. "But not Selene. Selene's boring. She doesn't play."

Addie stifled a laugh. Kendra just shrugged. "Not everyone's a kid person. That's why you've got us."

From the doorway, Harper crossed her arms, watching the scene unfold with a shake of her head. "Chosen aunts," she muttered, though a hint of pride tugged at her lips.

Grace nudged Harper with her elbow. "See? You didn't just build an empire. You built a village."

Harper didn't answer right away. She was watching Izzy stand tall, holding her finished banner high as the twins rallied beneath her. In the distance, Jasper's voice carried across the courtyard, calling for a briefing upstairs.

"I just hope," Harper said at last, "that they never forget where they belong. With us. With all of this."

Grace clinked her coffee cup against Harper's. "Trust me. They won't."

Outside, the three Barrett kids charged the oak tree, their "aunts" cheering from the porch—and for one rare, fleeting morning, BSS felt less like a machine of war and more like a family.
