Izzy acts like a second mother—reminding the twins about manners and rules.

Together, they bring both laughter and grounding into Harper's high-stakes life.

## The Hotel Suite Dilemma (NYC)

The Manhattan skyline glowed outside the suite window. Jasper paced, phone pressed to his ear, fury in every step. A deal was unraveling.

Harper sat on the couch, laptop open, half-watching the baby monitor stream from her au pair's phone. The kids were already asleep back in Miami.

"Harper, focus," Jasper snapped. "We're about to lose this client."

"I am focused," she said coolly. Her eyes never left the screen. "Your client is lying about the breach. It's not a hack—it's an inside job. Check the CTO's accounts. He's cashing out."

Jasper froze. "How the hell do you know that?"

Harper finally looked up. "Because people don't change. They just scale their vices. And because my daughter's preschool teacher makes the same face when she's hiding something."

He stared at her. Then he laughed. "Only you could connect a billion-dollar betrayal to finger painting."

Harper smirked, then closed the baby monitor feed. "It's all the same game, Jasper. People reveal themselves. Kids or CEOs, they're predictable. You just have to be watching."
