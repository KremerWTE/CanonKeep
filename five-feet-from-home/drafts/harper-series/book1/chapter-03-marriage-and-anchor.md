The ocean was black glass beyond the balcony of Harper's Coral Gables home. Her new husband, Daniel—a shipping magnate with a mind as sharp as hers and the calm of a man who had weathered hurricanes—handed her a glass of wine.

"You're not here," he said softly, touching her wrist.

"I'm always here," Harper replied, but her eyes flicked to her phone as it buzzed on the counter. A secure message from the London team. A potential betrayal. A name she already recognized.

Daniel shook his head, amused. "You can run a global empire from Miami, Harper, but you can't sit through dinner without thinking about it."

She set the phone down deliberately, face down. "I can be both. I can keep Jasper's empire intact and be your wife. The trick is knowing which fight deserves me."

He leaned in, kissed her temple, and smiled. "Just remember—sometimes the fight isn't out there. Sometimes it's right here."

For once, Harper let herself believe she could have both.

---

## The Sanctuary

Their home in Miami became a sanctuary: sleek, ocean-facing, filled with art and order—the opposite of the chaos she managed daily. Daniel had designed it with her in mind. Clean lines. Open spaces. Windows that framed the sunrise like it was a gift she'd earned.

Harper found herself standing at those windows more often now. Watching the water. Letting her mind go still, if only for moments.

"You're different here," Daniel said one morning, watching her from the kitchen doorway.

"Different how?"

"Softer. Like the armor comes off."

She turned to face him, coffee in hand. "The armor never comes off. I just... adjust the fit."

He laughed, crossing to her, wrapping his arms around her waist. "Then let me help you carry it."

Harper leaned into him. For once, she didn't argue.

---

## The Balance

Their relationship became a balance—he let her roam with Jasper and the teams, but he also gave her a grounding life outside the adrenaline of BSS.

Daniel sometimes traveled with her. Other times, she went alone. But she always carved out one week a month just for him.

Jasper respected her marriage deeply, often joking he "stole Harper from BSS three days a month." The truth was more complicated. Harper had learned, slowly, that you couldn't pour from an empty cup. Daniel filled hers.

"You're sure about the Rome trip?" Daniel asked one evening, watching her pack.

"I'm sure I have to go. I'm also sure I'll be back in four days, not six." She zipped the suitcase, then crossed to him. "And I'm sure you're the reason I come back at all."

He kissed her forehead. "Then go save the world. I'll keep the wine cold."
