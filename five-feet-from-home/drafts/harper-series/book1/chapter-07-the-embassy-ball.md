# Chapter 7: The Embassy Ball

## Harper Series - Book 1: "The Shadow's Ledger"
### Part Three: London

---

As COO, Harper was often Jasper's shadow in European cities. But tonight, things had shifted.

Jasper had been pulled away by diplomats for back-to-back conversations the moment they'd arrived. Harper watched him disappear into a private room, his assistant trailing behind with worried eyes.

"Mrs. Barrett?" A young woman approached, tablet in hand. "Mr. Barrett asked me to—"

"I know what he asked." Harper took the tablet smoothly. "Guest list and seating chart?"

"Yes, ma'am. And the donor briefings."

Harper scanned the information in seconds, committing names and faces to memory. Then she stepped into Jasper's role like slipping on a glove.

---

## The Invisible Queen

She moved through the room with purpose, handling introductions, recalling names and family details, quietly maneuvering a nervous client through the crowd.

"Lord Ashford, how lovely to see you. I trust Lady Ashford is well? Jasper mentioned she's taken up gardening—roses, wasn't it?"

The elderly lord beamed. "How did you know?"

Harper smiled. "We make it our business to know the things that matter."

She guided him toward a cluster of potential donors, making the introduction so smoothly that no one noticed she'd been steering the entire conversation.

A young wife of a new BSS client stood frozen by the bar, eyes wide with overwhelm. Harper approached gently.

"First gala?" she asked.

The woman nodded, mortified. "Is it that obvious?"

"Only to someone who's been there." Harper linked her arm through the woman's. "Let me introduce you to some friendly faces. By the end of the night, you'll wonder why you were ever nervous."

---

## The Return

By the time Jasper emerged from his diplomatic meetings, everything was running smoothly. Donors were laughing. The Ambassador's staff had stopped hovering nervously. Even the problem client who'd threatened to walk was now chatting amiably with a European investment banker.

Jasper found Harper by the terrace doors, a glass of sparkling water in hand, surveying the room like a general after victory.

"Remind me again which one of us is the COO?" he said, genuine admiration in his voice.

Harper didn't turn. "I believe that's always been me. You're just the face."

He laughed, then grew serious. "Harper, what you did tonight—"

"What I did was my job." She finally looked at him. "You built BSS to handle anything. I'm part of that 'anything.' Don't act surprised when I prove it."

The Ambassador's wife approached, beaming. "Mr. Barrett! Your colleague has been absolutely marvelous. You should bring her to London more often."

"She's not just a colleague," Jasper said, and the weight of those words hung in the air. "She's my partner."

---

## The After

Later, in the car back to the hotel, Harper finally let her shoulders drop.

"You were magnificent," Jasper said quietly.

"I was prepared." She stared out the window at the London lights. "There's a difference."

"No." He shook his head. "That wasn't just preparation. That was you. The way you read people, the way you moved them... the Ambassador's staff assumed you were the real power behind BSS."

Harper smiled faintly. "Aren't I?"

He didn't argue.

Her phone buzzed—Chiara, sending a video of the kids making pancakes with Daniel. Sofia's face was covered in flour. Nico was attempting to flip one and missing spectacularly. Izzy was reading a cookbook aloud, oblivious to the chaos.

Harper watched, heart aching with love and distance.

"Worth it?" Jasper asked, watching her face.

"Always," she said. "But I can't wait to go home."
