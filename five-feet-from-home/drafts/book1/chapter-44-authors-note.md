# Chapter 44 – Author's Note & Series Preview

*Source: book1_all_sources.txt - Series Bible*

---

**Author's Note**

*Five Feet From Home* began as a simple question: What happens when a man who's built his entire identity around solving other people's crises finally has to face his own?

Jasper Barrett's journey in Book One is about coming home — not just physically, but emotionally. It's about learning that the distance between where you are and where you belong can sometimes be measured in five feet.

---

**What's Next**

Book Two picks up the threads left spinning at the end of this story:

- **Harper's Discovery** – Something is happening in the field that even Jasper doesn't know about yet.
- **The Ownership Battle** – Chris Cole's team is caught in a war of money and ego, and Jasper's being pulled in.
- **Africa's Unfinished Business** – Rafe's warning wasn't idle. The continent has more to give — and take.

---

**The Royal Phoenix Series**

Book One: *Five Feet From Home* – The foundation. Jasper learns to choose family.

Book Two: *The Ownership Game* – The stakes rise. Corporate warfare meets personal loyalty.

Book Three: *African Thunder* – International intrigue. Old debts come due.

Books Four through Nine: The saga continues across continents, through crises, and into the heart of what it means to protect the people you love.

---

**Acknowledgments**

To everyone who helped build this world — the characters who demanded their stories be told, the relationships that insisted on complexity, and the readers who asked, "What happens next?"

The answer is: everything.
