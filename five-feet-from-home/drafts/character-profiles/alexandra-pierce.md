# Alexandra Pierce – Character Profile

## Background

Alexandra Pierce is a local client in Jasper's world—dangerously intelligent, sophisticated, and fully aware of the effect she has on people. She uses charm and allure as tools, moving through professional circles with the ease of someone who's never been told no.

Her business brings her into regular contact with Jasper, and she's made no secret of her interest in him—though she frames it professionally enough to maintain plausible deniability.

## Role in the Story

Alexandra represents temptation—not necessarily an affair in the traditional sense, but the seduction of feeling essential, of being pursued, of mattering in ways that are simpler than the complicated terrain of a long marriage.

She pushes Jasper's buttons, creates "urgent" situations that demand his attention, and makes work feel more exciting and validating than it should. She's a test of his commitment to change.

## Appearance

- **Age:** Late 30s to early 40s
- **Build:** Elegant, polished
- **Hair:** Well-styled, professional
- **Style:** Power dressing with subtle sensuality—tailored suits, silk blouses, strategic jewelry

## Personality

Alexandra is sharp, calculating, and accustomed to getting what she wants. She reads people expertly and knows exactly how to position herself as valuable. She's not malicious, exactly—she simply pursues what interests her without much regard for collateral damage.

## Quirk

Alexandra sends messages with manufactured urgency—"Need you now. Ten minutes."—knowing that Jasper will respond. She's mastered the art of making herself feel essential.

## Sample Quotes

- "Need you now. Ten minutes."
- (Her texts are her primary dialogue—short, demanding, strategically timed)
