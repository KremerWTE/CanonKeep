# Harper Caldwell – Character Profile

## Background

Harper Caldwell grew up in Savannah, Georgia, in a family that valued poise, tradition, and the art of making everyone feel welcome. She attended Columbia University for her undergraduate degree, where she was a prominent figure in Greek life—the kind of woman who ran recruitment while making everyone feel like family. She later earned her MBA from Wharton.

Harper started her career organizing charity galas before transitioning into corporate consulting and crisis management. She joined Jasper's firm (BSS) in its early years and quickly became his right hand—handling major overseas operations, managing high-stakes negotiations, and serving as the strategic counterbalance to Jasper's instinct-driven approach.

## Role in the Story

Harper is Jasper's most trusted professional partner. She handles the international crises—Singapore, Africa, Lagos—while Jasper manages domestic situations. She's also a bridge between Jasper and Elena, understanding both of them well enough to see what each needs but can't always articulate.

She serves as a mentor to younger women in the organization and maintains deep friendships with the wives' circle from her Columbia sorority days—including Maddie Cole.

## Appearance

- **Age:** Early 40s
- **Build:** Tall, elegant, commanding presence
- **Hair:** Dark, usually perfectly styled despite whatever crisis she's managing
- **Style:** Southern Belle polish meets corporate power—silk scarves, cashmere, always camera-ready
- **Signature Accessories:** Emerald scarf (knotted at the neck), burgundy leather tote (scuffed at corners from years of travel)

## Personality

Harper is the person who makes chaos feel manageable. She's strategic, composed under pressure, and possessed of the kind of charm that can convince anyone of anything. She never looks rattled, even when she is.

Beneath the polish is a fierce loyalty to the people she considers family. She'll move mountains for them while making it look effortless.

## Quirk

Harper always looks put-together—even at 3 AM after a 20-hour flight. She doesn't know how to turn off the professional polish and has never been photographed looking anything less than camera-ready.

## Sample Quotes

- "Sugar, I could charm a man into drinking anything."
- "I expect champagne."
- "If you wait for the work to slow down, you'll be waiting forever."
- "I look like I ended one." (In response to "You look like you survived a war")
