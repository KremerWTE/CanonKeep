# Chris & Maddie Cole – Character Profile

## Background

Chris Cole is a star NFL quarterback dealing with team ownership drama. He's at the top of his profession but caught in the middle of franchise politics—pressured by different factions to take sides while recovering from a shoulder injury that has him questioning his future in the game.

Madison "Maddie" Cole is his wife—a former Columbia swimmer who met Harper in their sorority days and has navigated the particular challenges of being an NFL wife with grace. She runs luxury wellness studios and understands the unique pressures of living in the spotlight.

They live on fifteen acres of manicured land outside Charlotte—part horse country aesthetic, part NFL money—where photographers might show up at the gate at any moment and every outfit choice matters.

## Role in the Story

Chris represents another facet of Jasper's work—the high-profile clients who need crisis management at the intersection of sports, business, and media. The ownership battle for his team is one of the threads setting up Book 2.

Maddie provides perspective on the wives' circle dynamic and serves as a connection point between Harper's professional world and the broader social network. Her chapter (Chapter 32) offers insight into the pressures of NFL marriages and the wisdom Harper gave her about "breathing under pressure."

## Chris's Appearance

- **Age:** Early to mid-30s
- **Build:** Athletic quarterback physique (currently recovering from shoulder injury)
- **Style:** Casual wealth—workout gear at home, polished when public

## Maddie's Appearance

- **Age:** Early 30s
- **Build:** Former swimmer's build—athletic, elegant
- **Hair:** Often in a messy bun that looks artfully casual
- **Style:** Camera-ready casual—leggings and fitted pullovers that still look put-together

## Personalities

**Chris:** Competitive, frustrated by politics he can't control through athletic performance. Used to winning through effort and skill, uncomfortable with backroom negotiations.

**Maddie:** Wise beyond her years about the realities of spotlight life. She sees through performance to what's real and values authenticity over impression.

## Sample Quotes (Maddie)

- "You hate those things." / "I hate fake things. This sounds different."
- "We need someone who knows how to breathe under pressure." (What Harper said when they first met)
- "No, that's the excuse. The job is throwing a football and winning games. Everything else is just noise."
