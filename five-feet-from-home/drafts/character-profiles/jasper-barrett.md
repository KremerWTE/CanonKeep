## Background

Jasper Barrett built his career on being the man companies call when everything is on fire. Starting in corporate PR in his twenties, managing launch events and brand partnerships, he spent a decade learning the language of crisis before becoming the architect of solutions no one else could see.

He founded Barrett Security Solutions (BSS) as a boutique crisis management firm that handles the impossible: billion-dollar product recalls, cyberattacks that threaten reputations overnight, executive scandals with bodies buried in footnotes. His client list reads like a Fortune 500 directory, and his reputation is built on a simple truth—Jasper Barrett never loses.

But that reputation came at a cost. While he was saving companies from ruin, his marriage was quietly deteriorating. His daughter grew up with a father who existed more as a voice on the phone than a presence at the dinner table. The "five-foot rule" that made him brilliant in crisis management—focusing only on what's immediately in front of him—became the very thing that blinded him to what he was losing at home.

His military background (implied Tier 1 operational experience) gave him the discipline and strategic thinking that defines his approach. He treats every crisis like a mission: contain, control, resolve. But he's learning that some battles can't be won with tactics alone.

---

## Role in the Story

Jasper is the central protagonist whose internal conflict drives the narrative. His arc moves from **work-obsessed crisis manager → present husband and devoted father**. The story explores whether a man who built his identity on being indispensable to everyone else can learn to be irreplaceable to the people who matter most.

**Book 1 (Five Feet From Home):** Elena's medical crisis forces Jasper to confront the cost of his absence. He must choose between his career trajectory and his family.

**Book 2+ (Royal Phoenix Series):** Continues navigating the balance between high-stakes work and family commitment, mentoring the next generation of crisis managers while building a life that doesn't require him to choose.

## Personality

### Core Traits
- **Strategic Thinker:** Sees five moves ahead; treats life like a chess game
- **Compartmentalizer:** Separates work and home into distinct mental spaces
- **Control-Focused:** Needs to feel in command of situations
- **Protective:** Deep instinct to shield those he loves (even when it pushes them away)
- **Reflective:** Capable of deep introspection when forced to slow down
- **Southern Roots:** Occasional drawl surfaces under stress

### Strengths
- Unshakeable calm under pressure
- Brilliant at reading people and situations
- Loyal to his core team
- Can inspire confidence in chaos
- Strategic vision that others miss

### Weaknesses
- Prioritizes work over family (learning to change)
- Emotional distance as self-protection
- Difficulty asking for help
- "Five-foot rule" blinds him to bigger picture
- Struggles to be present even when physically home

## Quirk

**The Pocket Photo:** Jasper carries a worn wallet photo of Elena and Grace at the beach—Grace flying a kite, Elena laughing, caught mid-motion. He touches it unconsciously before high-stakes meetings, a superstition he won't admit to but can't break.

**Coffee Ritual:** Makes his own coffee in a specific way, even at 3 AM before flights. The ritual is as much about control as caffeine.

## Character Arc Summary

**Beginning:** Work-obsessed executive who treats family like another item on the to-do list. Lives by the "five-foot rule"—focus only on the immediate crisis. Brilliant at work, failing at home.

**Middle:** Elena's medical crisis shatters his compartmentalization. He's forced to choose between a career-defining opportunity and being present for his family. Tempted by Alexandra but recognizes the line he can't cross.

**End:** Chooses family without abandoning his career. Learns to delegate, trust his team, and be present. Discovers that "being the best" means something different than he thought.

**Core Transformation:** From "the man who saves companies" to "the man who shows up for his family."
