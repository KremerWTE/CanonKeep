# Jessica Vaughn – Character Profile

## Background

Jessica Vaughn is the woman who discovered Jasper Barrett and transformed him from a struggling project manager into the crisis management expert he is today. When she was 47, working as a consultant for investment groups, she walked into a failing hotel renovation in Charlotte and found a young man holding the entire project together through sheer force of will.

She recognized talent that day—the kind that couldn't be taught but could be unleashed if given the right opportunity. She recommended keeping the project alive only if Jasper ran point, then later reached out to help him build his own firm.

Jessica comes from the old-money world of serious investors and quiet power. She's razor-sharp, impeccably dressed, and possesses the kind of wisdom that only comes from decades of watching careers rise and fall.

## Role in the Story

Jessica is Jasper's mentor and moral compass. She sees through his defenses and challenges him to be better—not just professionally, but personally. When Elena is in the hospital, Jessica is there, offering the hard truths Jasper needs to hear about work-life balance and what he stands to lose.

She represents what Jasper could become if he learns wisdom to go with his talent—someone who succeeded without sacrificing everything that mattered.

## Appearance

- **Age:** Mid-50s
- **Build:** Elegant, commanding presence
- **Hair:** Silver, usually pulled back in a precise chignon or loose braid
- **Style:** Tailored, understated luxury—the kind of clothing that whispers expense without shouting

## Personality

Jessica has zero patience for excuses and unlimited capacity for seeing potential. She studies people the way she studies contracts—searching for the clause that will cost too much later. But beneath the sharp exterior is genuine warmth for those who've earned her respect.

## Quirk

Jessica carries the same oxblood leather tote she's had for years—worn at the corners but still elegant. She refuses to replace it because "some things get better with age."

## Sample Quotes

- "You're not just putting out fires. You're redesigning the building while it's burning down."
- "You've been telling yourself *just thirty minutes* for twenty years."
- "They'll survive without you. She won't."
- "If you want her to keep choosing you, you have to make it easy for her."
- "When she wakes up, be here. Really here."
