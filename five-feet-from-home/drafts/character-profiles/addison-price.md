## Background

Addison Price didn't climb the ladder of crisis management—she built her own. Coming from a background where nothing was handed to her, she developed the kind of armor that makes people brilliant in boardrooms but lonely everywhere else.

She met Jasper Barrett when she was young and hungry, and together they built BSS into the elite firm it is today. She's his right hand, his confidante, and the sister he never had. She handles the impossible while he gets the credit, and she's fine with that—or tells herself she is.

But Addison carries secrets. Her relationship with Kendra—something between sisterhood and intimacy, never quite named—fractured somewhere along the way. She's drawn to Elena with a complexity she can barely admit to herself. And she's running so hard from her own vulnerability that she'll eventually collapse from the weight of it.

Her arc across the series is one of the most dramatic: from armored workaholic to someone who learns that strength isn't the same as isolation, and that letting people in doesn't mean losing yourself.

---

## Role in the Story

Addison is Jasper's mirror—showing what happens when you give everything to work and nothing to yourself. Her eventual collapse and rebuilding parallel his transformation but go even deeper.

**Book 1 (Five Feet From Home):** Manages the business crises while Jasper focuses on Elena. Her competence allows his transformation but at a cost to herself.

**Book 2+ (Royal Phoenix Series):** Her own health crisis forces a reckoning. Relationship with Hawk, reconciliation with Kendra, and exploration of her authentic self.

## Appearance

### Physical Description
- **Build:** Athletic, strong; works out intensely as emotional processing
- **Height:** Tall and commanding (5'9"+)
- **Hair:** Dark, usually in sharp bun or perfect styling; messy braid when exhausted
- **Eyes:** Intense, storm-like when emotional; usually controlled
- **Face:** Model-like features; polished and composed
- **Distinguishing features:** Moves with confidence bordering on intimidation; "stepped off a runway"

### Signature Look
Addison's appearance is her armor. Every element is controlled, deliberate, perfect. When she's slipping, you can see it in the small details: a messy braid instead of a sharp bun, the laptop balanced on a hospital tray, the circles under her eyes she didn't have time to conceal.

## Personality

### Core Traits
- **Brilliant:** Strategic mind that sees angles others miss
- **Guarded:** Emotional walls built brick by brick
- **Perfectionistic:** Demands excellence from herself and others
- **Loyal:** Fierce devotion to her core people
- **Driven:** Can't stop pushing; doesn't know how
- **Vulnerable:** Hidden beneath layers of armor

### Strengths
- Unmatched crisis management skills
- Can command any room
- Reads people and situations expertly
- Protective instincts for those she loves
- Never gives less than 110%

### Weaknesses
- Can't ask for help
- Uses work to avoid emotions
- Pushes herself to collapse
- Struggles with vulnerability and intimacy
- Complicated relationships she won't address

## Quirk

**The Five-Foot World (Inherited):** Addie adopts Jasper's coping mechanism—focusing only on the immediate crisis, ignoring the bigger picture. It makes her excellent at her job and terrible at her life.

**The Museum Keepsake:** She keeps a small prize from a museum trip with Kendra and Grace in her purse. Never mentioned. Never discarded. A symbol of what she's running from and can't let go.

## Character Arc Summary

**Beginning:** Armored workaholic who's brilliant at saving everyone except herself. Uses work to avoid emotional complexity. Carries unresolved feelings about Kendra, Elena, and her own identity.

**Middle:** Her own collapse forces a reckoning. Hawk's patient presence starts breaking through. She has to face what she's been running from.

**End:** Learns that strength includes vulnerability. Builds authentic relationships. Becomes "Royal Phoenix"—a title that represents her transformation from ashes.

**Core Transformation:** From "woman who holds everything together alone" to "woman who lets herself be held."
