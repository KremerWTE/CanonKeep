# Cole & Dean – Character Profile

## Background

Cole and Dean are both ex-Special Forces who served before transitioning to intelligence work (CIA and NSA respectively). They now work as Jasper's embedded cyber and intelligence specialists, handling the "dirty digital work" and occasional real-world extractions that the firm's clients require.

They've been in the field together for high-stakes operations—encrypted tunnels at 2 AM, back-channel meetings with people whose real names they'll never know, extractions that involve more firepower than anyone will ever admit on paper.

## Role in the Story

Cole and Dean represent the dangerous, morally gray side of Jasper's work—the operations that never make the papers, the crises that require more than corporate strategy to resolve. They're fiercely loyal to Jasper and respect Elena for her strength during the hospital crisis.

Cole in particular serves as a perspective character, offering insight into the toll that crisis work takes on those who do it and the importance of having something to come back to.

## Cole's Appearance

- **Age:** Late 30s to early 40s
- **Build:** Athletic, military bearing that never quite faded
- **Style:** Tactical casual—tactical pants, faded polos, functional clothing
- **Presence:** The posture of a man who never stops scanning for threats

## Dean's Appearance

- **Age:** Similar to Cole
- **Build:** Athletic, slightly more relaxed demeanor
- **Style:** Button-downs, casual but ready for action
- **Quirk:** Stress-eats protein bars; snores loudly when he finally lets himself sleep

## Personality

**Cole:** Observant, protective, thoughtful. He notices things others miss and cares deeply about the people he works with, even if he doesn't say it out loud.

**Dean:** More outwardly expressive, quicker to joke, but just as competent when things get serious. He's the one who lightens the mood after tense operations.

Both share the unshakeable bond of men who've been in combat together.

## Sample Quotes (Cole)

- "Tell me we're not landing into another crisis."
- "Barrett's throwing a party." "Like, with actual food and drinks and people who aren't trying to kill us?"
- "Ma'am, keeping Barrett grounded is above my pay grade. But I try."
- "It's the first time in months we'll all be in one place without a crisis. That matters."
