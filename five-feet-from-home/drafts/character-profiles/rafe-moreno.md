# Rafe Moreno – Character Profile

## Background

Rafe Moreno is an ex-con and former art thief in the vein of Neal Caffrey from White Collar. He was recruited by Jasper for his charm, unconventional skills, and street-level instincts—the kind of capabilities that can't be taught at business school but prove invaluable when traditional approaches fail.

His background gives him unique insight into the criminal underworld and an ability to navigate situations that would be impossible for more conventional operators. He's paid his debts to society and now uses his talents for legitimate (mostly) purposes.

## Role in the Story

Rafe handles the unconventional problems—the ones that require charm, creativity, and a willingness to operate in gray areas. He's the final push on deals, the back-channel negotiator, the person who can read a room and find the angle that everyone else missed.

In Book 1, his work on the Whitaker crisis is critical to its resolution, and his warning about Africa "not cooling down" sets up threads for Book 2.

## Appearance

- **Age:** Late 30s to early 40s
- **Build:** Athletic, charismatic presence
- **Hair:** Dark, well-styled
- **Style:** Polished casual—the look of someone who could charm his way into or out of anywhere

## Personality

Rafe is charming, quick-thinking, and utterly loyal to those who've given him a second chance. He's self-aware about his past and has developed genuine ethics, even if his methods remain unconventional. He can read people instantly and knows exactly how to position himself for maximum effect.

## Quirk

Rafe uses elaborate hand gestures when telling stories about past operations, making them 80% truth and 20% embellished for effect.

## Sample Quotes

- "Africa's not cooling down. Call me before someone else does. This one has teeth."
- Stories involving "improbable escapes from Lagos hotels"
