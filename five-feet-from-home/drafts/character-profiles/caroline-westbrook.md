# Caroline Westbrook – Character Profile

## Background

Caroline Westbrook is brilliant, polished, and devastatingly articulate. She's a finance-world insider with academic credentials—a respected professor based in New York who moves seamlessly between high-net-worth boardrooms, lecture halls, and television panels as a commentator.

Her expertise makes her invaluable in corporate situations requiring both strategic insight and public-facing credibility. She's the kind of person networks call when they need someone who can explain complex financial situations while looking camera-ready.

## Role in the Story

Caroline brings academic rigor and media connections to Jasper's operations. She's involved in the NFL ownership group discussions and is developing a mentorship program with Jessica and the wives' circle that will likely become significant in future books.

Her message about the ownership group meeting being "moved up" is one of the three threads that sets up Book 2.

## Appearance

- **Age:** Mid-40s to 50s
- **Build:** Elegant, professional
- **Hair:** Carefully styled, television-ready
- **Style:** The polished elegance of someone who appears on cable news—tailored, sophisticated, never a hair out of place

## Personality

Caroline is intellectually formidable and knows it. She can hold her own with anyone in finance or academia and isn't afraid to leverage her credentials. Despite her polish, she's collaborative and genuinely invested in elevating other women through mentorship.

## Quirk

Caroline can shift from academic jargon to television-friendly explanations in the same sentence—a skill honed through years of translating complex finance for general audiences.

## Sample Quotes

- "Ownership group meeting moved up. Certain people want you in the room. This isn't a request, Jasper. They need you."
