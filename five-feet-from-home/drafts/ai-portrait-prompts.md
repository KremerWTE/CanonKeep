## Style Notes for All Prompts

**Add these modifiers for better results:**
- `--ar 2:3` (portrait aspect ratio)
- `photorealistic, 8k, highly detailed`
- `cinematic lighting, depth of field`
- `professional photography, studio quality`

**Negative prompts to avoid:**
- cartoon, anime, illustration
- deformed, distorted features
- low quality, blurry
- oversaturated colors

---

## Usage Instructions

1. Copy the prompt for your desired character
2. Add style modifiers based on your AI tool
3. Generate 4-6 variations and select best match
4. Upscale chosen image for final use
5. Consider consistency across character set
